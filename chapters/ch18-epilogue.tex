\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

The monograph constructs a single derivation chain from a non-Abelian
matrix order parameter to an infrared equation that is Einstein gravity
plus a divergenceless entanglement stress, with a structural heat-kernel
map for \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and a massless spin-2 sector forced by
entanglement equilibrium. That chain is complete only under the named
assumptions of Chapters~3--12. Several critical-path derivations
remain open.

\section{What has been obtained}
\label{sec:concl-obtained}

Under those assumptions one has:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a composite metric defined from the condensate,
  \item \(m_T=0\) from the diamond Ward identity,
  \item the structural form of \(1/G_{\mathrm{eff}}\),
  \item covariant conservation of the total stress including
        \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{ent}}\),
  \item a consistent regulation of the leading vacuum-energy term
        via the holographic boundary functional.
\end{itemize}
Coefficient-level compatibility of the published RAR interpolator with
the SPARC sample is recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:empirical}. It is not
a derivation of that interpolator.

\section{What has not been obtained}
\label{sec:concl-not}

\begin{itemize}
  \item entanglement equilibrium from the microscopic path integral
        (Problem~B);
  \item a matrix-derived local Lorentz symmetry and specified full
        field content (Problem~D1);
  \item the infrared boundary entropy and stationarity derived from
        the matrix theory (Problem~G);
  \item a derivation of \(\nu_{\sqrt{}}\) (or any other interpolator)
        from mutual information or the modular spectrum (Problem~H);
  \item scheme-fixed values of the heat-kernel coefficients
        \(A,B,C\) (Problem~F);
  \item diagonalization of the conformal-mode mass matrix
        (Problem~C);
  \item the dictionary map into the modular Hamiltonian
        (Problem~A);
  \item an unsuppressed, observationally allowed laboratory coupling
        to the entanglement stress;
  \item empirical support from Gaia DR3 wide binaries once the
        Galactic external field is included (Appendix~\ref{app:gaia});
  \item any redshift-dependent growth multiplier; such constructions
        lie outside the proved chain.
\end{itemize}
The framework therefore does not yet resolve the dark-matter problem,
nor does it supersede existing phenomenological fits on galactic or
binary scales.

\section{Next steps}
\label{sec:concl-next}

Progress toward a completed matrix-derived infrared derivation requires
deriving, not retaining, the critical-path steps:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Problem~A: the dictionary of Assumption~\ref{ass:dictionary}
        derived from the microscopic definition;
  \item Problem~B: entanglement equilibrium from the microscopic
        path integral, including non-conformal tensor variations;
  \item Problem~C: the scalar operator with a coupling test;
  \item Problem~D1: local Lorentz symmetry and a non-degenerate frame
        derived from the matrix algebra, with the
        \(\omega,A_\mu,B\) and ghost operators;
  \item Problem~E: Lorentzian signature derived, not retained;
  \item Problem~F: \(A,B,C\) and \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from specified
        operators, with \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\) not an input;
  \item Problem~G: infrared entropy density and stationarity derived
        from the matrix theory;
  \item Problem~N: the effective expansion controlled, with no
        propagating Ostrogradsky ghosts for \(p<\LUV\).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Alternate scope.}
Specifying the operator content of an explicitly scope-limited
Presentation-B model (imposed local Lorentz symmetry, externally
justified boundary datum, or an input \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\)) can make
that model calculable but does not derive the critical-path steps and
leaves the matrix-derived completion open. Problems H, I, and J
are optional within their stated scopes; Problem I is a prerequisite
only if drag is claimed to be derived or predicted.

Until all the critical-path problems are derived, the infrared equation
remains exactly as written in the main text and no stronger claim is
licensed. The open-problem ledger of Appendix~\ref{app:status} is the
working list.
