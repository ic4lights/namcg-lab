\chapter[Research agenda]{Open Calculations and a Research Agenda}
\label{ch:agenda}

\paragraph{Learning outcomes.}
After this chapter the reader should be able to:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item distinguish a missing load-bearing proof from a missing
        numerical coefficient;
  \item pick a first calculation that actually moves the framework,
        rather than a calculation that only decorates Chapter~15;
  \item state what would have to be true before NAMCG may be
        described as finished rather than as a programme;
  \item use the appendices as tools, not as a substitute for the
        open problems listed here.
\end{enumerate}

Chapter~16 said what would kill the theory. This chapter says what
would complete it. The two lists are not the same.

\section{What is closed inside the book}
\label{sec:ch17-closed}

The following are closed \emph{as arguments from named hypotheses
to named conclusions}. They are not closed as derivations from
empty premises.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Composite metric from a polar condensate
        (Definitions~\ref{def:vierbein}, \ref{def:composite-metric}).
  \item Algebraic split of \(\delta\Phi_{\mu\nu}\) (Chapter~5).
  \item \(m_T=0\) from entanglement equilibrium plus Fierz--Pauli
        (Theorem~\ref{thm:mT}).
  \item Structure of the coefficient map
        \(1/\Geff = A\,\mathrm{Tr}(\mathbb{I})\LUV^2
        +B v^2\ln(\LUV/\mu)+C\mu^2\)
        (Theorem~\ref{thm:Geff}).
  \item Lorentz locking in unitary gauge
        (Theorem~\ref{thm:locking}).
  \item Conservation of the total stress
        (Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation}).
  \item Replacement of a bulk quartic by an infrared boundary
        datum (Theorem~\ref{thm:Leff}).
  \item A legal Newtonian laboratory for
        Assumption~\ref{ass:drag} (Chapter~15).
\end{itemize}

A reader who has worked every exercise through Chapter~16 can
reproduce that list. Reproducing it is not the same as removing
the hypotheses.

\section{Load-bearing openings}
\label{sec:ch17-bearing}

These problems change the status of the theory if they are solved.
They are ordered by how much of the later book depends on them.

\paragraph{Problem A: the infrared dictionary.}
Assumption~\ref{ass:dictionary} is a map
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:dict-again}
  [\hat X_\mu,\Phi]
  \;\longleftrightarrow\;
  i\Theta_{\mu\nu}D^\nu\Phi
\end{equation}
plus higher-derivative corrections. Construct it as a lemma from
Presentation~A: specify the Hilbert space, the Weyl map, the
sense in which a slowly varying \(\Phi(x)\) is recovered from
functions of \(\hat X^\mu\), and the error term at each order in
\(\Theta\). Until this exists, Chapter~8 is a calculation in
Presentation~B that is only morally a calculation in the matrix
model.

\paragraph{Problem B: entanglement equilibrium from the path
integral.}
Assumption~\ref{ass:EE} is the physical engine of
Theorem~\ref{thm:mT}. Derive \(\delta S_D=0\) at fixed volume as
a Ward identity of the microscopic action~\eqref{eq:Smicro}, or
of~\eqref{eq:Smatrix} after Problem~A, without importing Jacobson's
hypothesis from a theory that already has a metric. A replica
computation on small diamonds, with the polar vacuum as background,
is the obvious route. A result that holds only for conformal
fluctuations must be flagged as such, because the tensor block is
not conformal.

\paragraph{Problem C: the conformal mode.}
Assumption~\ref{ass:gap-psi} is the remaining scalar-tensor
ceiling. Compute the quadratic mixing of \(\psi\) with \(T_{\mu\nu}\)
and the mass \(m_\psi\) from~\eqref{eq:V} on the locked vacuum,
including the non-minimal coupling generated by the frame. Produce
a number, or a ratio \(m_\psi/v\), that can be compared with
Cassini bounds. If \(m_\psi\) is not parametrically of order
\(\sqrt{\lambda}\,v\), the assumption is false and the infrared
theory must be rewritten as scalar-tensor NAMCG.

\paragraph{Lemma (tree-level radial mass).}
\label{lem:msigma}
On the locked vacuum \(\langle\Phi^{I}{}_{A}\rangle=v\delta^{I}{}_{A}\),
write the polar field
\(\Phi=(v+\sigma)\Lambda\) with \(\Lambda^{\dagger}\Lambda=\mathbb{I}\)
and \(\langle\sigma\rangle=0\).
Then \(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi=(v+\sigma)^{2}\mathbb{I}\), and
\eqref{eq:V} collapses to a singlet potential.
With \(N=\mathrm{Tr}(\mathbb{I})\) locked directions,
\begin{equation}
  V
  =\frac{\lambda}{4}N\bigl[(v+\sigma)^{2}-v^{2}\bigr]^{2}
  =N\lambda v^{2}\,\sigma^{2}+O(\sigma^{3}).
\end{equation}
The kinetic term in~\eqref{eq:Smicro} contributes
\(N\,(\partial\sigma)^{2}\).
Canonical normalisation \(\sigma_{c}=\sigma\sqrt{2N}\)
puts the quadratic action in the form
\(\tfrac12(\partial\sigma_{c})^{2}-\tfrac12 m_{\sigma}^{2}\sigma_{c}^{2}\),
with
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:msigma}
  m_{\sigma}^{2}=\lambda v^{2},
  \qquad
  m_{\sigma}=v\sqrt{\lambda}.
\end{equation}
The would-be Goldstones in \(\Lambda\) remain massless at this order.
They are the modes Chapter~10 is required to eat.
They are not \(\psi\).

\paragraph{Lemma (no light conformal partner at tree level).}
\label{lem:psi-sigma}
Work in the pure-trace sector: \(T_{\mu\nu}=0\), \(F_{\mu\nu}=0\),
and \(\omega_{\mu\nu}=0\). Two repairs of the frame must be
kept distinct.

\emph{Repair II, unitary gauge.}
Chapter~10 permits \(\Lambda=\mathbb{I}\) wherever
Assumption~\ref{ass:nondeg} holds. The vacuum is then the frame,
\(e^{A}{}_{\mu}=\Phi^{A}{}_{\mu}/v\), so
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:repairII-trace}
  g_{\mu\nu}
  =(1+\sigma/v)^{2}\eta_{\mu\nu},
  \qquad
  \psi
  \equiv\eta^{\mu\nu}h_{\mu\nu}
  =8\sigma/v+O(\sigma^{2}).
\end{equation}
The conformal mode and the radial singlet are the same field.
Lemma~\ref{lem:msigma} therefore supplies the pole directly,
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:mpsi}
  m_{\psi}^{2}=\lambda v^{2}.
\end{equation}
There is no second scalar in this sector.

\emph{Repair I, official frame.}
Definition~\ref{def:vierbein} builds \(e^{A}{}_{\mu}\) from
\(\partial\Lambda\) at \(\sigma=0\). The potential~\eqref{eq:V}
depends only on the invariant \(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi=(v+\sigma)^{2}\mathbb{I}\),
so its Hessian on \((\sigma,\psi)\) is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:mass-matrix}
  \mathcal{M}
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    \lambda v^{2} & 0 \\
    0 & 0
  \end{pmatrix}
\end{equation}
in the field basis of Lemma~\ref{lem:msigma}, before any
identification of the scaffold with the composite metric.
The zero eigenvalue is the metric trace. It is not a second
condensate scalar: \(\Lambda\) is Lorentz-valued, and a pure
trace cannot sit in \(\mathfrak{so}(3,1)\). Once the induced
Einstein--Hilbert term is present, that zero mode is the
conformal factor already handled by the diffeomorphism
constraint. It is not a Brans--Dicke partner.

\emph{Identification \(g^{(0)}=g[e]\).}
Expanding \(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu}\) generates kinetic mixing
between \(\sigma\) and \(\psi\). The potential is unchanged, so
\(\mathrm{rank}\,\mathcal{M}=1\) survives the mixing.
The heavy eigenvalue remains a positive multiple of
\(\lambda v^{2}\). The light eigenvalue remains zero and
carries no \(\sigma\) residue beyond a field redefinition into
the metric trace.

\paragraph{Cassini, revised.}
The only scalar pole in the pure-trace sector sits at
\(m=v\sqrt{\lambda}\).
For any \(\lambda\) not tuned below
\((10^{-18}\,\mathrm{eV}/v)^{2}\), that pole is short-range
on an astronomical unit and does not contribute to the
Cassini link. The bound
\(\gamma-1=(2.1\pm 2.3)\times 10^{-5}\)
\cite{Bertotti2003}
constrains a massless partner. No such partner is produced
by~\eqref{eq:V} on the locked vacuum.

\paragraph{Status of Assumption~\ref{ass:gap-psi}.}
Promoted from assumption to tree-level lemma in the
pure-trace sector, contingent on Assumption~\ref{ass:frame-lock}
and on the induced Einstein--Hilbert term being the kinetic
operator of \(\psi\). Not contingent on a galaxy fit.
Loop corrections and the wrong-sign conformal kinetic term
of the heat kernel remain Chapter~8 bookkeeping. They can
flip a coefficient. They do not, at this order, create a
second light pole.


\paragraph{Problem D: local Lorentz invariance from Presentation~A.}
Assumption~\ref{ass:local-lorentz} puts
\(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\) into Presentation~B by hand. Either derive a
local Lorentz gauge symmetry from the matrix algebra and the
condensate, or state that NAMCG is a theory of a condensate field
on an auxiliary spacetime and stop calling Presentation~A
ontologically prior.

\paragraph{Problem E: Lorentzian signature.}
Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian} is unproved. A Euclidean matrix
integral that prefers a Lorentzian saddle would be a genuine
addition. A Wick rotation performed because the infrared world is
Lorentzian is not.

\section{Coefficient openings}
\label{sec:ch17-coeff}

These problems change numbers and regimes. They do not, by
themselves, create or destroy the graviton.

\paragraph{Problem F: the rationals \(A,B,C\).}
Recompute the Seeley--DeWitt coefficients on the bundle of
\(\delta\Phi_{\mu\nu}\), including ghosts for diffeomorphisms and
for local Lorentz transformations, and including the radial mode
threshold. Report \(A,B,C\) in~\eqref{eq:Geff-structure} with
the scheme specified. Until this is done, Theorem~\ref{thm:Geff}
is a structural result with working rationals inherited from the
notes.

\paragraph{Lemma (universal piece of $A$, and the sign).}
\label{lem:A-sign}
On a Euclidean section the curvature piece of~\eqref{eq:appA-a1} is
\(a_{1}\supset (\operatorname{tr}_{V}\mathbb{I})\,R_{E}/6\).
Insert it into~\eqref{eq:appA-Wexp} and continue as in
Appendix~\ref{app:seeley-dewitt}: \(\sqrt{g}\to\sqrt{-g}\) and
\(R_{E}\to -R_{L}\). The Lorentzian effective action contains
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:EH-universal}
  W[g]
  \supset
  \frac{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}{96\pi^{2}}
  \bigl(\LUV^{2}-\mu^{2}\bigr)
  \int\mathrm{d}^{4}x\,\sqrt{-g}\,R,
\end{equation}
where \(N_{\mathrm{eff}}=\operatorname{tr}_{V}\mathbb{I}\) is the net fibre trace,
bosons minus ghosts. Matching~\eqref{eq:EH-full} gives
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:A-universal}
  \frac{1}{16\pi\Geff}
  =
  \frac{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}{96\pi^{2}}
  \bigl(\LUV^{2}-\mu^{2}\bigr)
  +\text{threshold logarithms}.
\end{equation}
The prefactor is positive if and only if \(N_{\mathrm{eff}}>0\).
That is the attractive sign required by Theorem~\ref{thm:Geff}.
A net ghost excess is the killer already named in
Chapter~16; it is not a new one.

\paragraph{Fibre trace on the locked block.}
Before gauge fixing, Chapter~5 gives
\(\operatorname{tr}_{V}\mathbb{I}=16=1+6+9\).
Two subtractions are required by the redundancies already
proved, and no others.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\hline
Block & Weight & Trace \\
\hline
locked \(\delta\Phi_{\mu\nu}\) & \(+1\) & \(+16\) \\
diffeomorphism ghosts (complex vector) & \(-2\) & \(-8\) \\
local-Lorentz ghosts (antisymmetric) & \(-1\) & \(-6\) \\
\hline
net, Goldstones eaten & & \(+2\) \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The net \(+2\) is the transverse-traceless count of
Theorem~\ref{thm:mT}, read at the level of \(a_{0}\).
The radial mode is not in that net: it is heavy by
Lemma~\ref{lem:msigma} and is integrated down to
\(\mu\sim m_{\sigma}\), where it contributes to \(B\), not to
the \(\LUV^{2}\) piece.
Both legal partial counts, \(16-8=8\) before the Lorentz
ghosts and \(16-8-6=2\) after them, are positive.
Neither flips~\eqref{eq:EH-universal}.

\paragraph{The conformal mode does not flip \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\).}
Appendix~\ref{app:seeley-dewitt} records that a real scalar with
\(E=\xi R+m^{2}\) contributes \((1/6-\xi)R\) to \(a_{1}\).
At the conformal value \(\xi=1/6\) the trace mode cancels
in \(a_{1}\) and does not renormalise \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\).
Lemma~\ref{lem:psi-sigma} already identified the light
eigenvalue in the pure-trace sector with the metric factor.
Its kinetic sign in the induced Einstein--Hilbert term is
the ordinary conformal-factor problem of general relativity,
removed by the diffeomorphism constraint, not a second pole.
A computation that kept \(\xi_{\psi}\neq 1/6\) would change
\(N_{\mathrm{eff}}\) by \(6(1/6-\xi_{\psi})\), an order-one
shift, not a sign change, for any \(\xi_{\psi}\) of order one.

\paragraph{Rationals, scheme stated.}
Convert~\eqref{eq:A-universal} to the normalisation of
\eqref{eq:Geff-structure}. With \(N_{\mathrm{eff}}\) the net
trace above,
\begin{align}
  A &= \frac{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}{6\pi\,\mathrm{Tr}(\mathbb{I})_{\mathrm{notes}}},
  \label{eq:A-value}\\
  B &= \frac{4\pi}{3}\,\kappa,
  \label{eq:B-value}\\
  C &= 0,
  \label{eq:C-value}
\end{align}
at this order. The notes' value \(A=1/\pi\) is recovered
exactly when \(\mathrm{Tr}(\mathbb{I})_{\mathrm{notes}}=N_{\mathrm{eff}}/6\),
which is the absorption of the universal \(1/6\) already
declared in the proof sketch of Theorem~\ref{thm:Geff}.
The factor \(\kappa\) in \(B\) is \(1\) if the logarithm in
\eqref{eq:Geff-structure} means \(\ln(\LUV^{2}/\mu^{2})\),
and \(2\) if it means \(\ln(\LUV/\mu)\).
Remark~8.3 quotes \(B=4\pi/3\) against \(\ln(\LUV/\mu)\);
the conversion from~\eqref{eq:Geff-structure} produces
\(8\pi/3\) for that same logarithm. The discrepancy is a
scheme label, not a new diagram. It should be corrected
in the remark, not fitted.

\paragraph{What remains open inside Problem~F.}
The \(a_{2}\) trace on the symmetric-traceless block,
including \(\operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})\), has not been
evaluated. It can move \(\kappa\) by a factor of order one.
It cannot move \(A\) through zero while \(N_{\mathrm{eff}}>0\),
and it cannot revive a light \(\psi\) pole closed by
Lemma~\ref{lem:psi-sigma}.


\paragraph{Problem G: infrared boundary entropy.}
Evaluate \(S_{\mathrm{ent}}^{\mathrm{(IR)}}\) on an explicit
cosmological or large-tube boundary and produce a candidate
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from~\eqref{eq:Leff}. A symbolic
formula in terms of a late-time entanglement scale is more useful
than a claim to have obtained \(10^{-122}\).

\paragraph{Problem H: interpolating function.}
Derive \(\nu_{\sqrt{}}\) in~\eqref{eq:nu-sqrt}, or a function
that beats it on a SPARC hold-out, from the modular data of
Chapters~6 and~12. The target is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:nu-target}
  \nu_{\sqrt{}}(g_{\mathrm{N}})
  =\frac{1}{1-\exp\bigl(-\sqrt{g_{\mathrm{N}}/g_s}\bigr)},
\end{equation}
which is identically \(1+n_{\mathrm{BE}}(\sqrt{g_{\mathrm{N}}/g_s})\).
Appendix~\ref{app:empirical} already shows that this law, frozen
at the published \(g_\dagger\), is equivalent to the RAR and
about \(0.2\%\) behind simple MOND, while baryons-only fails.
That ranking is a coefficient check. It does not solve
Problem~H.

The first finite computation is the replica free energy of a
free field on a weak-field diamond in the infrared geometry of
Chapter~12, with \(\Lambda=\Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\). Read the
shift of the lowest modular eigenvalue \(\lambda_0\) when a
Newtonian source of acceleration \(g_{\mathrm{N}}\) is
introduced. Problem~H is solved if
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:lambda0}
  \lambda_0
  \propto
  \sqrt{\frac{g_{\mathrm{N}}}{\kappa_{\mathrm{IR}}}},
  \qquad
  g_s=\kappa_{\mathrm{IR}},
\end{equation}
and the occupation is bosonic, so that~\eqref{eq:nu-target}
follows with no SPARC input. A linear eigenvalue
\(\lambda_0\propto g_{\mathrm{N}}/\kappa_{\mathrm{IR}}\)
reproduces the retired \(\nu_{\mathrm{exp}}\) and kills
Assumption~\ref{ass:sqrt-mod}.

Alternatively, prove that no local
\(\mu_{\mathrm{ent}}(g_{\mathrm{N}})\) exists and write the
nonlocal kernel instead. Refitting \(\alpha\), \(r_s\), or
\(g_s\) to rotmod files is not a solution. Identifying
\(g_s\) with \(g_\dagger\) inside the replica calculation is
not allowed; compare \(\kappa_{\mathrm{IR}}\) to \(g_\dagger\)
only after \(\lambda_0\) is obtained.

\paragraph{Problem I: dynamical origin of the drag.}
Replace Assumption~\ref{ass:drag} by a low-frequency response
calculation of \(\Gamma_{ij}(\mathbf r;\ell_A,\ell_B)\) from
specified microscopic dynamics and a specified stationary reference
state, including the finite-size factors \(\ell_A,\ell_B\) and the
longitudinal and transverse components. Report the exponent and the
coefficient. Then, and only then, estimate \(\eta\) at
tidal radii and at LIGO-band separations. The static tail and its
assumptions are recorded in Problem~\ref{sec:status-I}; no static-tail
power determines the retarded response.

\paragraph{Problem J: spherical ECG solutions.}
State the precise cubic invariant \(\mathcal{P}\) being used and
prove or disprove that~\eqref{eq:f-reg} is exact for that
invariant. If it is only approximate, publish the residual and
the corrected \(f(r)\).

\section{Problems that look like work and are not}
\label{sec:ch17-not}

The following activities are useful as exercises and do not move
the framework.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Producing further Newtonian integrators with new colour
        maps of Figure~\ref{fig:lab}.
  \item Fitting SPARC with an unconstrained \(\mu_{\mathrm{ent}}\),
        a per-galaxy \((\alpha,r_s)\), or a new \(\nu\) that is
        not~\eqref{eq:nu-target} and is not derived from
        \(I(A:B)\). Appendix~\ref{app:empirical} already did the
        legitimate coefficient ranking.
  \item Writing \(\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{irrev}}\) with additional
        free exponents and calling each a prediction.
  \item Replacing \(G_{\mathrm{toy}}\) by \(G_{\mathrm{N}}\) in
        a slide.
  \item Adding adjectives (``holographic,'' ``non-Abelian,''
        ``entropic'') to an abstract without adding a lemma.
\end{itemize}

Chapter~15 exists so that a legal laboratory is on the record.
It does not exist so that the laboratory can become the theory.

\section{A recommended order of work}
\label{sec:ch17-order}

For a reader who has read the book and wants a first project:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Recompute Problem~F for the locked \(4\times 4\) block
        only, ignoring unlocked scalars. This is finite and
        checks whether the working rationals survive.
  \item Compute \(m_\psi\) in Problem~C at tree level. This
        decides whether the infrared theory is GR or
        scalar-tensor before any galaxy is discussed.
  \item Attempt the replica variation of Problem~B on a Minkowski
        diamond for a free tensor field with a mass term. This
        is a miniature of Theorem~\ref{thm:mT} with the logic
        reversed: one sees whether \(\delta S_D=0\) really
        forbids \(m_T\neq 0\) for that free field.
  \item Only then open Problem~A. It is larger than a term-long
        project if done honestly.
\end{enumerate}

Problems~H--J remain phenomenological. Appendix~\ref{app:empirical}
has already spent the SPARC budget of Problem~H as a coefficient
check. Further galactic work waits on a derivation from
\(I(A:B)\), and should not start before Problems~C and~F have
rough answers. Otherwise \(\nu_{\sqrt{}}\) will be fitted in a
theory whose Newton coupling and extra scalar are not under
control.

\section{What ``finished'' would mean}
\label{sec:ch17-done}

NAMCG would be a completed infrared derivation, rather than a
programme, if all of the following held.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Problems~A and~B are lemmas, so Presentation~A and
        entanglement equilibrium are no longer assumptions.
  \item Problem~C gives \(m_\psi\) large enough that Cassini is
        safe, or the theory is rewritten as scalar-tensor and
        tested as such.
  \item Problem~F reports \(A,B,C\) with ghosts included.
  \item Problem~D is decided in one direction or the other.
  \item Conservation and HBEV remain as they are now, with
        Problem~G supplying at least a scaling for
        \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\).
\end{enumerate}

Problems~E and~H--J can remain open in a completed infrared
derivation. Signature, galaxies, and cores are then research
inside the theory, not pillars of it.

Until the first four items exist, the honest public description
is the subtitle of the book, read as a programme:
\emph{an attempt} to derive general relativity and massless
spin-2 dynamics from a matrix condensate held at entanglement
equilibrium. The attempt is written so that it can fail in
public. That is the point of Chapter~16.

\section{Bridge to the appendices}
\label{sec:ch17-bridge}

The main text is complete. Appendix~A records the Seeley--DeWitt
algorithm at the working thickness used in Chapter~8.
Appendix~B records the replica formula and the modular Hamiltonian
at the thickness used in Chapter~6. Appendix~C records the
Verlet laboratory and the energy identity used in Chapter~15.
Appendix~D records the SPARC ranking of \(\nu_{\sqrt{}}\) against
the RAR, simple MOND, and baryons-only. None of the appendices
introduces a new physical hypothesis. If an appendix appears to
do so, the hypothesis belongs in the main text or it does not
belong in the book.
