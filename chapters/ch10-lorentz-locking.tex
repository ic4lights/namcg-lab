\chapter[Lorentz locking]{Local Lorentz Locking and the Fate of the Goldstones}
\label{ch:lorentz-locking}

\paragraph{Learning outcomes.}
After this chapter the reader should be able to:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item write the simultaneous action of internal gauge
        transformations and local Lorentz transformations on
        \(\Phi^I{}_A\);
  \item identify the diagonal subgroup preserved by the polar vacuum
        and the six broken generators;
  \item exhibit the Stueckelberg replacement that removes the
        Goldstone orbit variables \(\omega_{AB}\) in unitary gauge;
  \item state the provisional sector bookkeeping through the
        Goldstone and vector lines;
  \item enter Chapter~11 with a composite frame that transforms as a
        genuine vierbein, so that \(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}\) is well
        defined.
\end{enumerate}

Since Chapter~5 the antisymmetric piece \(\omega_{AB}\) has been
called a local-Lorentz Goldstone and postponed. This chapter assumes the local gauge symmetry and shows that the six
Goldstone orbit variables \(\omega_{AB}\) are removable in unitary
gauge. It does not determine the full propagating spectrum.

\section{The two groups that act on \(\Phi\)}
\label{sec:ch10-groups}

The condensate field of Presentation~B transforms, by
\eqref{eq:Phi-transform}, as
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:Phi-tr-again}
  \Phi^I{}_A(x)
  \;\longmapsto\;
  \Omega^I{}_J(x)\,\Phi^J{}_B(x)\,\Lambda^B{}_A(x).
\end{equation}
Here \(\Omega(x)\) takes values in the internal gauge group
\(G_{\mathrm{int}}\) and \(\Lambda(x)\) takes values in
\(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\), or in its double cover if spinors are later
coupled. Infinitesimally,
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:Phi-inf}
  \delta\Phi^I{}_A
  =\alpha^I{}_J\Phi^J{}_A
   +\Phi^I{}_B\,\beta^B{}_A,
\end{equation}
with \(\alpha\) in the Lie algebra of \(G_{\mathrm{int}}\) and
\(\beta_{AB}=-\beta_{BA}\) in \(\mathfrak{so}(3,1)\).

These are independent transformations at the level of field content.
Nothing in Chapter~3 forced \(\Lambda(x)\) to be a gauge symmetry
rather than a global classification of the index \(A\). Treating it
as local is a choice, recorded as such.

\begin{assumption}[Local Lorentz gauge symmetry]
  \label{ass:local-lorentz}
  The microscopic action~\eqref{eq:Smicro} is invariant under
  local \(\Lambda(x)\in\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\) once a spin connection
  \(\omega_\mu{}^A{}_B\) is introduced through the covariant
  derivative on the tangent index,
  \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:D-both}
    \mathcal{D}_\mu\Phi^I{}_A
    =\partial_\mu\Phi^I{}_A
     -i(A_\mu)^I{}_J\Phi^J{}_A
     +\omega_\mu{}^B{}_A\Phi^I{}_B.
  \end{equation}
  The connection \(\omega_\mu{}^{AB}\) is, at this stage, an
  independent gauge field.
\end{assumption}

Assumption~\ref{ass:local-lorentz} is the step that makes \(A\) a
genuine tangent index rather than an internal flavour that happens
to have four values. Combined with Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian},
it is the origin of local Lorentz invariance in NAMCG. It is not
derived from Presentation~A.

\section{The vacuum and the broken generators}
\label{sec:ch10-broken}

The polar vacuum of Chapter~4, in the Minkowski frame,
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:vac-lock}
  \langle\Phi^I{}_A\rangle=v\,\delta^I{}_A,
\end{equation}
is invariant only under the diagonal combination
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:diagonal}
  \alpha^I{}_J
  =-\,v^{-1}\delta^I{}_B\,\beta^B{}_C\,\delta^C{}_J
   \Big|_{\text{locked \(4\times 4\) block}},
\end{equation}
that is, under simultaneous internal and Lorentz rotations that
rotate the locked axes together. If \(G_{\mathrm{int}}\) contains
\(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\) (or a group large enough to have an
\(\mathfrak{so}(3,1)\) subalgebra acting on the locked indices),
six generators are unbroken and six are broken.

\begin{proposition}[Goldstone count]
  \label{prop:goldstone-count}
  About the vacuum~\eqref{eq:vac-lock}, with
  Assumption~\ref{ass:local-lorentz} in force and with the locked
  block of dimension four, there are six broken generators. The
  corresponding Goldstone fields are the antisymmetric components
  \(\omega_{AB}\) of~\eqref{eq:linear-frame}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
  The vacuum stabilizer is the diagonal
  \(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)_{\mathrm{diag}}\). The coset
  \(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)_{\mathrm{Lorentz}}\times
   \mathrm{SO}(3,1)_{\mathrm{int}}/\mathrm{SO}(3,1)_{\mathrm{diag}}\)
  has dimension \(6+6-6=6\). An infinitesimal broken transformation
  with parameter \(\beta_{AB}\) shifts
  \(\Phi^I{}_A\) by \(v\beta^I{}_A\), which is antisymmetric in
  \((I,A)\) after lowering with \(\eta\). That shift is the
  fluctuation \(\omega_{AB}\).
\end{proof}

If \(G_{\mathrm{int}}\) is larger than \(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\),
additional Goldstones live in the unlocked block. They belong to
Assumption~\ref{ass:locked-block} and are taken heavy.

\section{Eating \(\omega_{AB}\)}
\label{sec:ch10-eat}

A local broken symmetry with an independent connection is a Higgs
mechanism. Introduce the Stueckelberg field
\(\Lambda(x)\in\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\) already present in the polar
decomposition~\eqref{eq:polar}, and replace the spin connection by
the dressed connection
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:dressed-omega}
  \omega_\mu
  \;\longmapsto\;
  \Lambda^{-1}\omega_\mu\Lambda+\Lambda^{-1}\partial_\mu\Lambda.
\end{equation}
Choose the unitary gauge
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:unitary}
  \Lambda(x)=\mathbb{I},
\end{equation}
which is admissible wherever the polar decomposition is smooth
(that is, wherever Assumption~\ref{ass:nondeg} holds and no
condensate defect forces \(\det e=0\)). In that gauge the six orbit variables \(\omega_{AB}\) are removed.
Identify the locked indices \(I\leftrightarrow A\) through
\(\langle\Phi^I{}_A\rangle=v\delta^I{}_A\), and write
\((A_\mu)^I{}_A=(A_\mu)^I{}_J\delta^J{}_A\) on the locked block.
Then~\eqref{eq:D-both} gives
\[
  \mathcal D_\mu\langle\Phi^I{}_A\rangle
  =v\bigl[(\omega_\mu)^I{}_A-i(A_\mu)^I{}_A\bigr].
\]
The factor \(i\) follows the Hermitian-generator convention in
\eqref{eq:D-both}; with real antisymmetric generators the corresponding
combination is \(\omega-A\). Define the broken-direction combination
\(\Xi_\mu:=\omega_\mu-iA_\mu|_{\mathrm{locked}}\) in this convention.
Write \(B_\mu\) for the connection along the unbroken diagonal
stabilizer direction; no complementary connection combination is
canonical until kinetic terms specify a metric on the
\((\omega,A)\) field space. The stabilizer direction lies in the
kernel of the condensate covariant derivative. The invariant
contraction on the locked block depends on the action and generator
conventions, so the quadratic term is written as
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:omega-mass}
  \mathcal L_{\mathrm{cond}}^{(2)}
  \supset v^2 Q_{\mathrm{locked}}(\Xi_\mu),
\end{equation}
where \(Q_{\mathrm{locked}}\) denotes that contraction; it is not
assumed to be a real Lorentz-vector square. This is a quadratic
Higgs-type contribution, not a physical mass assertion. Its pole and
sign require gauge-field kinetic terms and canonical normalization,
which are unspecified. Torsion and the dynamics of the diagonal
connection remain open (Problem~D).

\begin{theorem}[Lorentz locking]
  \label{thm:locking}
  Under Assumptions~\ref{ass:frame-lock},
  \ref{ass:local-lorentz}, and~\ref{ass:nondeg}, there exists a
  gauge in which
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item the six Goldstone orbit variables \(\omega_{AB}\) are
          removable in unitary gauge; this makes no claim about the
          full propagating spectrum;
    \item the frame \(e^A_\mu=\Phi^A{}_\mu/v\) of
          Repair~II,~\eqref{eq:vev-is-frame} (or an independently
          supplied non-degenerate coframe), transforms only under
          the diagonal local Lorentz group, as a vierbein;
    \item the condensate term supplies a quadratic Higgs-type
          contribution in the broken direction \(\Xi_\mu\); the
          diagonal connection's dynamics are not determined. A
          torsion-free result follows only under the conditional
          hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:B-torsion-free}.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  Proposition~\ref{prop:goldstone-count} supplies six Goldstones.
  The Stueckelberg replacement~\eqref{eq:dressed-omega} and the
  gauge~\eqref{eq:unitary} remove the six orbit variables. In that
  gauge \(\Phi^{IA}=(v+\sigma)\delta^{IA}\) plus tensor and vector
  blocks, so the Repair~II frame \(e^A_\mu=\Phi^A{}_\mu/v\) transforms
  as \(e^A_\mu\mapsto\Lambda_{\mathrm{diag}}{}^A{}_B e^B_\mu\).
  This argument establishes the frame transformation and the
  quadratic broken-direction term, but does not determine the
  diagonal connection's spectrum or torsion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Defects]
  Unitary gauge fails where \(\Lambda(x)\) cannot be set to the
  identity continuously---at defects where \(\det e=0\). Those loci
  are condensate defects, not coordinate singularities of an
  already existing manifold. They are possible ultraviolet
  endpoints of the regular cores of Chapter~13, and they are not
  analyzed further here.
\end{remark}

\section{The internal vector after locking}
\label{sec:ch10-vector}

The internal connection \(A_\mu\) is distinct from the independent
Lorentz connection \(\omega_\mu{}^{AB}\). The condensate term mixes
them only through \(\Xi_\mu\); an orthogonal complement is defined
only after kinetic terms provide a metric on the \((\omega,A)\)
field space. Neither a Higgs mass \(m_A\sim g_{\mathrm{YM}}v\) nor a
decoupling scale follows without those terms. If a specified
internal-vector quadratic operator gaps the four components counted
in Section~\ref{sec:ch5-count}, they are omitted from the low-energy
frame/metric-sector bookkeeping; otherwise their spectrum remains
open. If an internal subgroup \(H\) is unbroken, its corresponding
gauge field is massless (conditional). This identifies the
conditional vector row of the provisional sector bookkeeping; it
does not establish the dynamics of the diagonal connection
(Problem~D).

\begin{proposition}[Conditional torsion-free diagonal connection]
  \label{prop:B-torsion-free}
  Let \(e^A{}_\mu\) be an invertible coframe and \(B_\mu{}^{AB}\) an
  independent diagonal Lorentz connection. Suppose its dependence in
  the action is either the Palatini term
  \(\epsilon_{ABCD}e^A\wedge e^B\wedge F^{CD}(B)\), or that term
  plus the Holst term
  \(\gamma^{-1}e_A\wedge e_B\wedge F^{AB}(B)\), with finite real
  \(\gamma\ne0\), \(\gamma^2\ne-1\), or \(\gamma=\infty\) (pure
  Palatini). Assume there are no spin sources and no other
  torsion-dependent terms, including \(F(B)^2\), torsion-squared
  terms, or matter couplings to \(B\). Then the \(B\) equation is
  the Holst-modified Cartan equation and gives \(T^A=0\);
  consequently \(B\) is the Levi-Civita spin connection of \(e\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
  Since \(\delta F^{AB}=D(\delta B^{AB})\), compactly supported
  variations give, for the Palatini term,
  \[
    \delta_B S_{\mathrm P}
    \simeq -4\int \epsilon_{ABCD}\,
       T^A\wedge e^B\wedge\delta B^{CD}.
  \]
  Thus \(\epsilon_{ABCD}T^A\wedge e^B=0\). Expand
  \(T^A=\tfrac12T^A{}_{EF}e^E\wedge e^F\) and contract the
  three-form equation with \(\epsilon^{EFGM}\). In frame components
  this yields, up to an overall nonzero convention-dependent factor,
  \[
    2(\tau_C\delta_D^M-\tau_D\delta_C^M)
       +2T^M{}_{CD}=0,\qquad
    \tau_C:=T^A{}_{AC}.
  \]
  Tracing \(M=D\) gives \(4\tau_C=0\), since
  \(T^D{}_{CD}=-\tau_C\). Hence \(\tau_C=0\), and the uncontracted
  equation then gives \(T^M{}_{CD}=0\).

  With the Holst term, the equation is
  \((\star+\gamma^{-1})Y_{CD}=0\), where
  \(Y_{CD}:=T_{[C}\wedge e_{D]}\), and \(\star\) is the internal
  Lorentzian Hodge dual on antisymmetric internal index pairs. The
  internal dual of \(Y\) is proportional to the Palatini Cartan
  equation. Since \(\star^2=-1\),
  \[
    (\star+\gamma^{-1})(-\star+\gamma^{-1})
    =(1+\gamma^{-2})\mathbb I.
  \]
  The map is invertible for the stated parameters. Therefore
  \(Y=0\), which implies the Palatini Cartan
  equation and \(T^A=0\) by the preceding argument. For an
  invertible coframe, vanishing torsion fixes the metric-compatible
  spin connection uniquely to the Levi-Civita one.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
  This proposition constrains \(B\) only under its hypotheses. It is
  not needed to define the Levi-Civita connection \(\nabla\) of
  \(g=e^T e\) or to state diffeomorphism conservation
  \(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}=0\). NAMCG has not been shown to induce
  these Palatini or Holst hypotheses. For a Laplace-type operator,
  \(a_1=\operatorname{tr}(R(g)/6-E)\); the explicit universal
  curvature term is the Levi-Civita scalar \(R(g)\). Because \(E\)
  is unspecified, no conclusion about additional \(B\)-dependence
  of \(a_1\) follows, and a Palatini \(R(B)\) term is not
  established. If \(B\) enters the bundle connection of the actual
  operator, \(a_2\) may contain a
  \(\operatorname{tr}F(B)^2\) term; whether it survives in the
  weighted trace depends on the unspecified operator
  representation and weights. Deriving the \(B\)-dependence of
  \(E\) and the bundle connection is open.
\end{remark}

\section{Diffeomorphisms}
\label{sec:ch10-diffs}

Diffeomorphisms act on the coordinate index of every tensor
constructed from \(\Phi(x)\). They are not eaten. They are the
gauge symmetry of the composite metric, and they are what force
\(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{total}}=0\) in Chapter~11.

After Theorem~\ref{thm:locking}, an infinitesimal diffeomorphism
\(\xi^\mu\) acts on the vierbein in the standard hybrid way,
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:lie-vierbein}
  \delta_\xi e^A_\mu
  =\xi^\rho\partial_\rho e^A_\mu
   +\partial_\mu\xi^\rho\,e^A_\rho,
\end{equation}
up to a local Lorentz transformation that can be used to keep a
chosen gauge on the frame. The induced action on \(g_{\mu\nu}\) is
the Lie derivative~\eqref{eq:lie-g}. No extra scalar Goldstone of
diffeomorphisms appears: diffeomorphisms are gauged, not broken, in
the condensed phase.

\section{What locking does not do}
\label{sec:ch10-not}

Theorem~\ref{thm:locking} does not derive Lorentzian signature
(Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian} remains). It does not derive local
Lorentz invariance from Presentation~A
(Assumption~\ref{ass:local-lorentz} remains). It does not gap
\(\psi\). It does not compute \(\Geff\). It does not conserve
\(\Tent_{\mu\nu}\).

What it does is make the later covariant derivatives honest. From
here on, \(\nabla_\mu\) denotes the Levi-Civita connection of the
composite metric \(g=e^T e\). This is distinct from the independent
Lorentz connection \(B\), whose dynamics remain open. Statements
such as \(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{total}}=0\) refer to this
Levi-Civita connection.

\section{Exercises}
\label{sec:ch10-exercises}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stabilizer.}
    Verify that~\eqref{eq:vac-lock} is invariant under the
    diagonal combination~\eqref{eq:diagonal} and under no other
    independent pair \((\alpha,\beta)\) acting on the locked block.
  \item \textbf{Coset dimension.}
    Compute
    \(\dim\bigl(\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\times\mathrm{SO}(3,1)
    /\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\bigr)\) and match it to the six components
    of \(\omega_{AB}\).
  \item \textbf{Stueckelberg.}
    Starting from~\eqref{eq:D-both} and~\eqref{eq:polar}, perform
    the replacement~\eqref{eq:dressed-omega} and isolate the
    quadratic term~\eqref{eq:omega-mass} in unitary gauge.
  \item \textbf{Torsion-free spin connection.}
    Given \(e^A_\mu\), write the explicit formula for the
    torsion-free spin connection
    \(\omega_\mu{}^{AB}(e)\). This is not a statement that the
    independent connection \(B\) equals \(\omega(e)\).
    Confirm that \(\omega(e)\) transforms inhomogeneously under local
    Lorentz transformations, as a connection must.
  \item \textbf{Defect.}
    Construct a hedgehog configuration of \(\Lambda(x)\) on a
    spatial 2-sphere inside which \(\det e=0\) at a point. Why
    can unitary gauge not be imposed globally on that slice?
  \item \textbf{Residual Yang--Mills.}
    Suppose \(G_{\mathrm{int}}=\mathrm{SO}(3,1)\times H\) with
    \(H\) unbroken. What is the infrared field content after
    Theorem~\ref{thm:locking}, in addition to the graviton?
  \item \textbf{Hybrid diffeomorphism.}
    Derive~\eqref{eq:lie-vierbein} from the action of a
    coordinate shift on \(\Phi(x)\) and the Repair~II frame
    \(e^A_\mu=\Phi^A{}_\mu/v\) of~\eqref{eq:vev-is-frame}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Bridge to Chapter~11}
\label{sec:ch10-bridge}

The emergent geometry now has a metric \(g=e^T e\) with its
Levi-Civita connection \(\nabla\), a frame-sector target of two
massless TT polarizations, and a \(\Geff\) whose heat-kernel matching
remains conditional on the specified sector calculation in
Problem~F. The independent Lorentz connection \(B\) is a separate
field: its dynamics are not determined here, and it is torsion-free
only under the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:B-torsion-free}.
This does not affect the Levi-Civita connection \(\nabla\), to which
\(\nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}=0\) refers.
Diffeomorphism invariance is in force. Noether's second theorem
therefore requires
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:cons-next}
  \nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{total}}=0
\end{equation}
of whatever stress tensor sources the Einstein equation. Chapter~11
defines \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{total}}\) as the sum of ordinary
matter, the tensor-block stress already used in Chapter~7, and the
entropic stress \(\Tent_{\mu\nu}\) obtained by varying
\(U_{\mathrm{ent}}\). It then proves that the sum is divergenceless
on shell. This conservation statement constrains the total stress
when it is derived from the specified diffeomorphism-invariant
action. It does not, by itself, derive the phenomenological drag law
or show that a reduced drag model is its covariant limit; that
connection remains Problem~\ref{sec:status-I}.
