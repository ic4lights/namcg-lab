## 3. Specific advice for the currently open item 053

(No revision demand on the r3 text as accepted; these are the research steps and referee questions attached to its open points.)

- **N≥3 extension of the Pfaffian bound.** As far as I could verify, no published work proves (or even states) convergence of the Lorentzian/boost-rotated IKKT Pfaffian integral at N≥3; 2604.19836 works numerically up to N=128 only after a SUSY-inspired deformation that removes the singular-Pfaffian drift, which is not the bare integral of 053. Keeping "conditional on a reading of hep-th/0103159" is exactly the field-accurate posture. If a sharpening is wanted later, the hypotheses to demand are: an entry-wise bound on the coefficient matrices uniform along R_u plus a flat-direction counting statement at N≥3 — nothing outside 053 supplies either.
- **Non-polynomial observables.** Highest-value next step. The obstruction is precise: on R_u at u→1, `e^{ik·A}` grows exponentially in Ã for timelike k, so dominated convergence with the e^{-S_E} weight fails by degree-type reasons even at N=2; the informative targets are (i) the maximal analyticity domain in k for which the limit still holds (a strip-type statement would directly serve the IR two-point-function programme, since real-time Wightman reconstruction is continuation in k), and (ii) whether the failure at timelike k is an artifact of the contour or of the observable. The published mechanism for handling such growth is the thimble decomposition itself (2501.17798v3) — but that is a numerical route and changes the definition of the integral, so it should be framed as a comparison, not as the definition.
- **γ>0.** 053's non-claim is consistent with the field: 2501.17798v3 (PRL) reports different saddle structure for γ>0 and around γ=0, and 2404.14045 exists precisely because mass/boost terms interact badly with cutoffs. Keep fixed γ>0 untreated; the N=2 deformation route (gatekeeper avenue 4) remains open but low value until (b)'s comparison is written.
- **Classicalization cross-check (new, referee-visible).** 2404.14045 claims that with a Lorentz-breaking IR cutoff, Lorentz-invariant observables "become classical" as the cutoff is removed. A referee will ask whether the 053 (A)-regulated finite-N limits exhibit classicalization — the insert's N=2 numerics (fluctuation moments growing as powers of 1/ε at high degree) suggest fluctuations persist at finite N, so there is probably no contradiction (their statement is presumably about large-N/thermodynamic behavior, and 053's is a finite-N ε→0-last statement), but one sentence of disambiguation (in a future proposal, not now) would close an easy objection.
- **The γ<0 discrepancy with 2501.17798 plots.** The v3 abstract attributes model differences to "different saddle points"; 053's exact N=2 equality holds on the single contour R_1, while thimble sums can carry additional thimble contributions and their FP/delta-function smearing differs. My advice is exactly what the insert already says: leave it unresolved, and if a round is ever spent on it, spend it on reproducing their γ<0 normalization with the 053 reduction before attempting theory.
- **N→∞ order of limits.** No published tool makes the C_{O,N} constants uniform in N; the 2604.19836 deformation changes the integrand. Keep the non-uniformity as an explicit scope limit; there is no external route to import.
