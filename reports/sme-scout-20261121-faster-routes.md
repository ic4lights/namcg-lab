## Direct answer to the author's question

**No — there is no published route that reaches NAMCG's stated criterion faster than the current programme.** Every family that has gone further on emergent spacetime gets there by assuming what the Criterion (ch17 sec:ch17-done: A, B, C, D1, E, F, G-derived, N all derived, no assumed premise; chains through `ass:dictionary`/`ass:lorentzian`/`ass:frame-lock` etc. disqualified) requires to be derived: a continuum, a metric, local Lorentz symmetry, or the dictionary itself. The one programme claiming comparable speed at full GR — Ho–Kawai–Steinacker, arXiv:2509.06646 — obtains "GR in the IIB matrix model" by **imposing** a polarized vacuum and deriving only diffeomorphism-invariance and unitarity of bilocal observables, not the Einstein equations to any order; under NAMCG's own criterion that is a D2-style imposed-symmetry outcome, not D1. What the field does offer is four concrete mechanism transplants (below) that can shave time off C/D1, E, G and F without touching emergence. NAMCG's derivation discipline is, on every critical-path item except bilocal operator technology, at or ahead of the published frontier.

## 1. Shortcut routes and what they actually derive

Criterion reference used throughout: full-GR scope requires A–N derived with no assumed premise (ch17 sec:ch17-done; work order D1→C→F→N→B→G; A, E removable at any time).

**Jacobson-style gravity-as-EoS / entanglement first law.** Derived: linearized Einstein equations from δS_D = δ⟨H_mod⟩ for small balls, given (i) a Lorentzian QFT on Minkowski spacetime, (ii) known modular Hamiltonians (conformal input), (iii) Bekenstein–Hawking identification as input. Not derived: microscopic entropy density, nonlinear GR, coefficients, signature, the algebra-without-metric. Verified: Jacobson arXiv:1505.04753 (PRL 116, 201101 (2016)); Casini–Galante–Myers 1601.00528; Bueno–Min–Speranza–Visser 1612.04374; Cao–Carroll 1712.02803; Jensen–Sorce–Speranza 2306.01837 (type II₁, first law without eom); Svesko 1810.12236; Alonso-Serrano–Liška 2008.04805 (ends in unimodular gravity — an honest limit of the route). Verdict vs criterion: assumes the metric, the signature and the modular data. It **cannot** shortcut B: the family always starts from a metric-having theory, which is exactly what NAMCG's B-missing-step forbids. It does supply the proof pattern for B (relative-entropy monotonicity for inclusions) — usable only once A provides the inclusion.

**Holography / bit-from-bit / tensor networks / QEC.** Derived: bulk linearity of Einstein equations from entanglement first law in AdS/CFT (Jafferis–Lewkowycz–Maldacena–Suh, arXiv:1512.06431, JHEP 06 (2016) 004); bulk locality as QECC structure (Pastawski–Yoshida–Harlow–Preskill, arXiv:1503.06237, JHEP 06 (2015) 149; Jahn et al. 1905.03268); entanglement-connects-geometry (Van Raamsdonk 1005.3035). Assumes: holographic duality, large-N boundary CFT on a fixed background, semiclassical bulk. Swingle's tensor-network paper: not independently verified this session (an id guess failed), so not cited. Verdict: this is the field's proof that a full emergent-gravity dictionary is *possible*, but the microscopic derivation of the dictionary (NAMCG's A) exists nowhere in the literature outside AdS. No shortcut; it is NAMCG's blueprint.

**SYK / JT / matrix quantum mechanics.** Derived: JT gravity as a genuine matrix integral including nonperturbative effects (Saad–Shenker–Stanford, arXiv:1903.11115). Assumes: 1+1-dimensional topological gravity, Euclidean ensemble. No local propagating graviton, no signature problem addressed. Verdict: nothing transfers to 4D full-GR criterion; NAMCG is strictly ahead in dimension/signature demands.

**Lorentzian IKKT/BFSS Monte Carlo (Nishimura programme).** Same theory class as NAMCG. Derived: numerical evidence for dynamical (3+1) expansion, dimensional reduction, and Lorentzian-vacuum selection. Verified: Nishimura 1209.6386; Kim–Nishimura–Tsuchiya 1108.1540 (PRL 108, 011601 (2012)) + 1110.4803; Klinkhamer 1912.12229 (PTEP 2020, 103B03); Stern–Xu 1808.07963; Chaney–Stern 1612.01964; Asano–Nishimura–Piensuk–Yamamori 2404.14045; Chou–Nishimura–Tripathi 2501.17798. Verdict vs criterion: simulation, not derivation — zero of A, B, C, D1, E, F, G is analytically closed here. It corroborates E numerically but supplies no control. NAMCG's E being open is the frontier, not slowness.

**Steinacker-type induced gravity from matrix models (closest competitor).** Verified: Ho–Kawai–Steinacker 2509.06646 (abstract verbatim: diffeomorphism invariance + unitarity emerge from bilocal fields about a Lorentz-invariant vacuum; no Einstein equations derived to an order claimed); Steinacker corpus 2609.08955, 2605.13294, 2601.08031, 2510.21283 (PRD 113, 024050 (2026)), 2509.24753, 1709.10480, 1111.2732; Karczmarek–Steinacker 2207.00399. What they assume: the polarization/vacuum (NAMCG's frame-lock, imposed), continuum limit. Relevance: their **biloc (bilocal-field) technology is the single most transferable item in the field** for NAMCG's C/D1 missing frame operator — a frame and connection built as matrix bilinears over a chosen vacuum. NAMCG's extra burden (deriving the lock itself, non-degeneracy) is untouched by this programme; representation-theoretic obstruction recorded for C is not addressed anywhere in this literature.

**Causal sets.** Verified: Carlip "Causal Sets and an Emerging Continuum" 2405.14059; Carlip–Carlip–Surya 2311.18238 (entropically dominant causal sets → Einstein–Hilbert action), 2209.00327; Surya 2510.19403; Srivastava–Surya 2603.25503; Bhattacharya–Mathur–Surya 2301.06480. Derived: EH action as a statistical dominance statement; continuum-smoothness evidence. Not derived: deterministic Einstein equations, G as a computed coefficient, Lorentzian signature from a positive measure. Verdict: the nearest discrete programme has the same G/F-grade gap as NAMCG. No shortcut.

**CDT.** Verified: Ambjørn–Loll 2604.05641; Loll 2501.17972; van der Duin–Loll–Schiffer–Silva 2510.05693/2510.05695. Derived: numerical emergence of 4D de-Sitter-like geometries with a fixed triangulation measure. Not derived: Einstein equations, coefficients, signature (Lorentzian built in by construction). No shortcut.

**Asymptotic safety.** Verified: Ferrero review 2507.14296; D'Angelo "Asymptotic Safety in Lorentzian quantum gravity" 2310.20603; "Asymptotic safety is not generic" 2611.07002; Kotlarski et al. 2304.08959. Assumes the continuum Lorentzian metric — i.e. assumes NAMCG's conclusion. Useful only as a cautionary template for N (Lorentzian higher-derivative ghost analyses; non-universality warning). No shortcut.

**GFT condensates / loop.** Verified: Gielen–Mickel 2312.10016 (CQG 41, 165002 (2024): metric reconstructed from GFT, effective Friedmann equation), 2505.07951 (CQG 42, 225015 (2025): perturbations deviate from GR); Gielen 2412.07847 (CQG 42, 083001 (2025), review); Calcinari–Gielen 2407.03432. Derived: cosmological (symmetry-reduced) dynamics from condensate states; metric as a collective observable. Not derived: local frame operator, full GR, coefficients. Verdict: the condensate-expectation-value method is a published precedent for NAMCG's Repair-II programme (frame and Z from a collective state), at homogeneous scale only.

**NCG / spectral triples.** Verified: Gayral–Gracia-Bondía–Iochum–Schücker–Várilly "Moyal Planes are Spectral Triples", hep-th/0307241 (CMP 246, 569 (2004)); Gayral, LMP 65, 147 (2003) (Moyal-plane action functional); Cagnache–D'Andrea–Martinetti–Wallet 0912.0906 (JGP 61, 1881 (2011): Connes' spectral distance on the Moyal plane is **not** a metric-space distance); Nieuviarts 2502.18105 and 2512.15450 (**emergence of Lorentzian signature from twisted almost-commutative spectral triples**, no Wick rotation); van den Dungen 1711.07299 (JMP 59, 063507 (2018)). Derived: heat-kernel/action coefficients on constant-Θ Moyal spaces; signature emergence in an algebraic (twisted-real-structure) setting. Assumes: an underlying continuum manifold (Nieuviarts) or nonunital triple data (Gayral et al.). Verdict: Nieuviarts' twist is the only concrete published mechanism producing Lorentzian signature from algebraic data short of NAMCG's contour approach — an existence proof, not a shortcut, but transplantable (see §3, E). Cagnache et al. is a caution for A's metric-recovery step.

**Amplitudinic / on-shell.** Derived: massless spin-2 + Lorentz-invariant S-matrix ⇒ universal coupling / equivalence principle (Weinberg, PR 138, B988 (1965), DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.138.B988, and companion PR 140, B516 (1965), verified). Assumes spacetime and the S-matrix outright — the reverse direction. No spacetime-from-amplitudes result exists to shortcut anything.

**Induced gravity / Sakharov heat-kernel.** Verified: Visser gr-qc/0204062 (MPL A 17, 977 (2002)); Azri 1808.09348 (CQG 36, 165006 (2019)); Chaichian–Oksanen–Tureanu 1805.03148. Carlip's "Reflections on induced gravity" and Toms' "Does Sakharov's induced gravity work?": **not found on arXiv/Crossref this session — not independently verified; excluded.** NAMCG's F is this programme done without a background metric; the literature says the method alone never fixes signature (E) and generically misfixes G's sign — a warning, not a shortcut.

## 2. No-gos and hardness bearing on D1 and E

- **Fermion doubling.** Verified: Nielsen–Ninomiya, "A no-go theorem for regularizing chiral fermions", PLB 104, 315 (1981), DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(81)91026-1 (companion NPB 185, 544 (1981) confirmed via the record's reference list). Premises: local, Hermitian, translation-invariant **lattice** regulator. Loophole NAMCG already sits in: a (0+0)-dimensional reduced matrix model has no Brillouin zone, so doubling does not bind; it only re-enters if NAMCG ever lattice-regulates internal directions. Counterexample to strong emergent-Lorentz pessimism: Kharuk–Sibiryakov, "Emergent Lorentz invariance with chiral fermions", 1505.04130 — chiral relativistic IR fixed points exist in published models. So no literature no-go blocks D1 in NAMCG's hypothesis class.
- **Weinberg–Witten.** Verified: PLB 96, 59 (1980), DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(80)90212-9. Forbids composite massless spin-2 with a Lorentz-covariant conserved stress tensor. NAMCG's microscopic theory is itself SO(3,1)-covariant and its graviton is not an S-matrix composite of a covariant current algebra, so no direct conflict; the known loophole (drop covariance or drop gauge-invariant T) is exactly where composite-graviton models live. Bears on B/G only as a consistency check, not an obstacle.
- **Emergent relativistic locality.** The task referenced "Adlam–Preskill-type constraints". **The scout could not verify that any Adlam–Preskill paper exists** (arXiv author query and Crossref author+bibliographic queries both negative; Emily Adlam's records are on other topics). What is real and verified is weaker and constructive: QECC/monogamy constraints on subregion reconstruction (Pastawski et al. 1503.06237). No published theorem prohibits emergent local Lorentz/diffeomorphism invariance from matrix DOF. The published difficulty is positive-programme absence (Ho–Kawai–Steinacker stop at diffeo+unitarity; Nishimura at numerics), not a no-go. This is good news for D1's feasibility and bad news for any claim of urgency-by-no-go.
- **Euclidean→Lorentzian / signature.** The manuscript's own Problem E records a bounded reflection-positivity failure on the invariant polynomial algebra at N=2 — an internal, gatekeeper-verified obstruction. Externally: the only positive mechanisms in print are contour/real-structure choices (NAMCG's (B)/(C) definitions; Nieuviarts' twist, 2502.18105) and Osterwalder–Schrader-type reconstruction — the latter is standard background (already cited in the manuscript; originals not re-verified this session). Reviews claiming general Euclidean→Lorentzian emergence: none found; the field has no general solution of E either.
- Coleman–Mandula adjacency: local/gauge emergent SO(3,1) evades exact-symmetry no-mixing theorems; the original C–M record was not verified this session and no citation is asserted for it.
