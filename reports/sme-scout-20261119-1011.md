## Task 1 — where 094's criterion lives in the literature, and is NAMCG's presentation correct?

**My accuracy verdict: the presentation is correct.** I re-derived both thresholds from scratch. (i) Depth-m interior stratum: the transverse space to the stratum in the trace-simplex has dimension m−1 and exactly C(m,2) pair factors each carrying exponent β−1, so the radial exponent is (m−1)−1+C(m,2)(β−1) and e+1 = C(m,2)(β−(1−2/m)) exactly as written — positivity is equivalent to β&gt;1−2/m, giving thresholds 0, 1/3, 1/2 at m=2,3,4. No error found.

**Literature home.** The *method and general criterion* are textbook-standard, in three published places:

1. **Weighted hyperplane-arrangement integrals (hypergeometric integrals).** The general convergence criterion for ∫∏_H|α_H|^{−s_H} near a central arrangement is: for every flat X of the intersection lattice, Σ_{H⊇X}s_H &lt; codim X. Applied to the braid (Coxeter A) arrangement with uniform weight s=1−β, a depth-m flat has codim m−1 and carries C(m,2) hyperplanes, giving s·C(m,2) &lt; m−1 ⇔ β &gt; 1−2/m — the 094 criterion verbatim. A live confirmation of the criterion in its modern standard form: **arXiv:2608.16363** (Matuzas, 2026, "The second pole of Witten zeta functions and exact evaluations in types F4 and D5") states, as standing hypotheses for arrangement-period statements, exactly "for every nonzero proper intersection flat X, the sum of λ_H over H containing X is strictly less than codim X", and evaluates period sums via Selberg's integral. The classical book home is Varchenko, *Multidimensional Hypergeometric Functions and the Theory of Arrangements of Hyperplanes* (Springer Grundlehren 354, 2005) — **not independently verified this pass** (no open arXiv record; cite only as the standard reference).
2. **Selberg integral convergence.** Selberg's integral (Selberg, Duke Math. J. 12 (1944) 47–65) and its convergence region Re a, Re b &gt; 0, Re c &gt; −min(Re a, Re b)/(n−1) are the single-stratum global shadow of the same bookkeeping; Aomoto's papers (SIAM J. Math. Anal. 18 (1987)) give the arrangement formulation. **Not independently verified this pass** (no open records fetched). Forrester, *Log-gases and Random Matrices* (LMS Monographs 12, Princeton 2010) is the standard modern statement, incl. negative-exponent moments — **not independently verified**.
3. **Igusa local zeta / resolution of singularities viewpoint.** Integrability of |f|^{−s} for monomial-type f after embedded (real) blow-up, and Denef-type formulas for the poles (Denef, Amer. J. Math. 106 (1984); Denef–Loeser, J. Algebraic Geom. 7 (1998), alg-geom/9603013 — **Denef–Loeser not independently verified this pass**) are the general framework 094 executes elementarily. The closest *published analogue of the exact question* I found is **arXiv:2312.01591** (Glazer–Gordon–Hendel, "Integrability and singularities of Harish-Chandra characters", 2023, v2): integrability thresholds of character integrals expressed as the log-canonical threshold of a relative **Weyl discriminant**. The Vandermonde is precisely the type-A discriminant, so 094's family of thresholds is exactly real-lct data of the A_{D−1} discriminant restricted to the chamber; read off their criterion, the real log-canonical exponent of V itself is 1 (the m=2 stratum), and 094 refines it per stratum.

**Verdict:** known method, known general criterion; I found **no printed statement of the per-depth threshold β &gt; 1−2/m for the Dirichlet-beta family** in this pass. 094 is best classified as a correct, self-contained evaluation of a standard criterion for the braid arrangement — an exercise of known theory, sharply and correctly done, not a new theorem. ## Task 2 — can published machinery make the matrix weight exactly the Dirichlet-beta family (chain 2)?

**Positive side (already in 091/L3195, and it is textbook):** for a complex Gaussian edge block A (constant entry variance), the joint density of the ordered eigenvalues of the Gram matrix AA† is ∝ ∏λ_i^{n−m} e^{−Σλ}∏(λ_i−λ_j)² (complex Wishart), and conditioning on Tr AA† is the exact scale-fibration giving the (a, β)=(|N−2M|+1, 2) Dirichlet-Vandermonde member on p=λ/τ. This is Muirhead, *Aspects of Multivariate Statistical Theory* (Wiley 1982, Ch. 3, esp. Thm 3.3.1 in the real/complex Wishart sections) and Forrester l.c. — **not independently verified this pass** (textbooks, no open records); arXiv phrase searches for "Wishart + Dirichlet + Vandermonde" returned zero hits, which reflects search-phrase rigidity, not absence. The geometric origin of β=2 is the Weyl volume formula — the Jacobian of the eigenvalue map, i.e. a Duistermaat–Heckman/Witten-volume statement for the conjugation action. NAMCG's own statement that β=2 is "kinematic, fixed by complex Hermitian structure" is exactly this, and is the correct reading.

**Negative side — the actual gap.** The remaining chain-2 demand is that the weight the core matrix model induces on the *moment coordinates* ρ_p of the profile equals a member of the family. The published Wishart/DH/Witten apparatus produces the family only on **Gram-spectrum coordinates** of Gaussian blocks. Nothing in the literature converts Gram-spectrum coordinates into the diagonal/moment profile coordinates of NAMCG's moment map — and NAMCG's own texts argue the two are genuinely different charts in the named subclasses (rank-one Gram spectrum is a single atom at the vertex, L3197; slot-power profile is flat at D=2, L3195, while the Gram profile carries the β=2 repulsion). My honest literature verdict: **no published mechanism plausibly bridges Gram-vs-moment-profile coordinates for NAMCG's edge datum; and no published mechanism produces β≠2 from a Gaussian core action** (β≠2 arises in the literature only from Dyson-index changes, Calogero–Sutherland-type deformed measures, or Jack-deformed ensembles — none of which is the core's gaussian coupling). A negative literature verdict, stated as such, is a useful, citable conclusion: it means any exactness step must come from inside the core (a state-side or coordinate-identification derivation), not from imports.
