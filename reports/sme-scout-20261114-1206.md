## 1. Ranked routes

### R1 (A). Rescope the matrix-to-modular lemma to wedges; use the warped-convolution invariance of modular data
- **Sources (verified, abstract only):**
  - Buchholz-Lechner-Summers, CMP 304 (2011) 95, arXiv:1005.2656: deformed theories "keep their Tomita-Takesaki modular data" but are generally inequivalent to the undeformed theory.
  - Grosse-Lechner, JHEP 11 (2007) 012, arXiv:0706.3992 (abstract via search, record confirmed there): wedge-local fields on non-commutative Minkowski space, with partial negative results on sharper localisation.
  - Grosse-Lechner follow-up, "Noncommutative Deformations of Wightman QFTs", arXiv:0808.3459 (search record only).
- **Step supplied:** the "Free/Poisson sector" and "General sector" paragraphs of A claim a modular statement for a constant-Theta Moyal realisation that survives interactions. These results say the wedge modular group is not changed by a Moyal/Rieffel-type deformation. That is the exact-in-interaction statement the lemma lacks at wedge level.
- **Sub-lemma for the researcher:**
  - Hypotheses: a Haag-Kastler or Wightman IR theory with vacuum, constant Theta, and a warped-convolution deformation along a Lorentz-frame-fixed Theta.
  - Expected result: the deformed wedge algebra has the same Tomita-Takesaki data, hence boosts at the wedge edge for any mass and coupling.
  - NAMCG objects needed: Theta, the quadratic sector, and a stated Poincare-covariant IR theory (this comes from D1/E).
- **Constraints:**
  - The deformed theory is generally inequivalent to the original and is wedge-local, not strictly local (Grosse-Lechner negative results). The diamond statement is therefore not recoverable by this route.
  - Using it requires the IR theory to be Poincare-covariant with a vacuum. That makes A inherit D1/E, as the earlier scout (20261015-1110, R2) already noted.
  - The Moyal-element reading in 066 (a dilatation weighting rather than a boost) must be reconciled first.
- **Feasibility:** medium, and only if the author accepts a wedge-level A. The author alone decides whether that is a scope change. Success would be a conditional sharpening that could unstall 066 by replacing the diamond sentence with a wedge-level one.
- **Emergence:** preserved. The metric is not used; the wedge is defined by the algebra.

### R2 (A). Do not import any massive diamond result; state the Speranza borderline
- Longo-Morsella 2012.00565 v4 and 2602.03606 (above) plus Cadamuro-Froeb-Minz, Ann. Henri Poincare 2024, arXiv:2312.04629 (abstract: small-mass perturbative corrections for massive fermions in a 2D diamond; the massless modular Hamiltonian is known, the massive case "was an open problem"). The massive diamond modular Hamiltonian is open in the field.
- Speranza 1602.01380 (JHEP 04 (2016) 105 per DOI): for a CFT deformed by a relevant operator of dimension Delta, ball entropies get terms of order R^(2 Delta). These dominate over R^d when Delta <= d/2, "which suggests that Jacobson's conjecture may need modification".
  - **MY READING, not verified from the text:** a mass term phi^2 in d = 4 has Delta = 2 = d/2, exactly borderline. Any entanglement-equilibrium argument that uses the massive radial mode would need this checked before claiming the Einstein term is the leading order.
- Use: this is a constraint for A and B, not a derivation route. Feasibility is high (it is checking); value is a guard against overstatement.

### R3 (G-derived, avenue 1). Spacetime-block subsystem via the symbol map
- **Sources:**
  - Fliss-Frenkel review 2512.03163 (abstract read: subsystems with gauge redundancy, target-space entanglement in non-commutative geometry, the minimal-area link when U(N) invariance holds).
  - Karczmarek-Sabella-Garnier 1310.8345 (abstract read, above).
  - Dou-Ydri and Sabella-Garnier 1705.01969 appear in search results but were not opened.
  - Sugishita 2105.13726 (algebraic target-space entropy; I opened only the proceedings 2202.01626, which cites it).
- **Step supplied:** 065 avenue 1 asks for a region of the 4D base times the fibre, rather than a compact internal factor.
  - The symbol map defines a region in the Poisson limit.
  - The entropy for such a region in the fuzzy-sphere literature is extensive below the non-locality scale and area-like only above it after coarse-graining.
  - On the CP^{1,2} / M^{3,1} background the analogue of that scale is the non-commutativity scale.
- **Sub-lemma:** on the doubleton or conjugate-coordinate member, take the spectral/symbol projector Theta of a region of the base; determine whether the Gauss-law/edge quantity log d_mu has a regime with entropy per unit boundary area measured in the composite metric, and what its extensive part is.
- **Constraints:**
  - Expect a bounded no-go in the extensive regime. This is a likely outcome and would be a consequence of the literature, not a failure.
  - The area appears in target space with the supplied contraction. 065 shows it equals the induced length only for the round sphere. Any claim that it equals the composite-metric area is what A/D1 must still supply.
  - The coarse-graining scale and the state remain supplied inputs (065).
  - 2512.03163 is a review: confirm each result against the primary paper before citing.
- **Feasibility:** low to medium for the regime analysis; low for the metric identification. Conditional sharpening, or a bounded no-go.
- **Emergence:** preserved if the area is expressed through G built from Theta; at risk if the target-space contraction is used unmodified (as the previous scout warned).

### R4 (G-derived). Fixed-volume Euclidean partition function; the volume constraint from the Liouville density
- **Source:** Jacobson-Visser, "Partition function for a volume of space", arXiv:2212.10607, PRL 130, 221501 (DOI as given on the arXiv page).
  - Abstract: a saddle-point count of states of a ball of fixed proper volume gives exp(A/4G), with a mild boundary singularity regulated by higher-curvature terms.
  - Tavlayan-Tekin 2307.14210 (abstract) extend this to a higher-curvature theory in three dimensions, with the Wald entropy.
  - Already in earlier scouts (20261015-1110, R4) as a pointer. The new element is the sub-lemma tied to 065.
- **Why it fits NAMCG:** it needs a Euclidean path integral with a volume constraint, no Hilbert-space factorisation, and no replica trick. 065's no-go says the matrix factorisation route cannot supply ass:Sent-density. The earlier ledger notes (013/014) say the density rho = 1/|Pf Theta| is fixed by Liouville, so the fixed-volume constraint would be part of the kinematics.
- **Sub-lemma:** insert delta(integral of rho - V) into the matrix integral at the induced-gravity level; show the saddle of the induced Einstein-Hilbert action (Problem F) gives S = A/4G_eff with the same G_eff.
- **Constraints:**
  - It uses the induced action and hence F's open status. The result would be an entropy density on the boundary, conditional on F, not independent of it.
  - Their singularity needs higher-curvature regularisation. NAMCG's L_UV^2 term might supply it, but this is MY SUGGESTION, not checked.
  - It does not discharge ass:bdy-EE (stationarity).
- **Feasibility:** medium for the formal step; low for turning it into a matrix-integral statement. Value: conditional sharpening that reduces G to F.

### R5 (G-derived, avenue 3). Algebraic / centre-based generalised entropy
- Sugishita (above; verified only through the proceedings abstract) defines target-space entanglement by a generalised entropy based on an algebraic approach, with no tensor factorisation.
- Donnelly-Wall 1412.1895 (PRL 114, 111603 per search record; abstract via search) identify the Maxwell contact term as the entanglement entropy of edge modes. This is a published mechanism for the kind of mismatch 065 finds: an area-term piece that the Euclidean replica calculation gives and the Hilbert-space calculation lacks. Disputes: Ball-Law-Wong (JHEP 2024) and 2403.14542 on zero modes.
- **Sub-lemma:** treat log d_mu (rank-dependent) as a centre-of-algebra entropy plus a Shannon term. Test whether the |mu| log M piece is the Shannon term and whether the remaining piece is additive.
- **Feasibility:** low to medium. Value: possible refinement of 065's bounded no-go. Not a closure.

## 2. Constraints and no-go loopholes to respect
- Massive diamond modular Hamiltonian: no closed form exists (R2).
- Wedge algebras of deformed theories: modular data survive, but locality is only wedge-wise (R1).
- Fuzzy-sphere entropy is extensive or area-like depending on scale (R3); Gauss-law entropy is the log dimension of one dominant representation.
- Dorau-Much 2510.24491 (PRL 136, 091602 (2026); abstract read) derives semiclassical Einstein equations from Araki-Uhlmann relative entropy on a bifurcate Killing horizon but assumes the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy-area formula and a given geometry. It cannot supply G's entropy density, and using it would introduce a metric.
- Dias 2506.00265 assumes a metric field; not a NAMCG route.
- Weinberg-Witten: no new item this session.
