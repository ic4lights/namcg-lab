## (a) FRONTPAGE — top 5 ranked routes

1. **G.selrule-imalpha → structured quadratic-pencil / standard-triple theory.** NAMCG's `2q×2q` pencil `T = -2i[[C₀†,Z₀],[-1_q,-C₀]]` is a companion linearization of a quadratic matrix polynomial in `t` (det(tI−T) reduces to a degree-`2q` polynomial in the `q×q` data `C₀=U†R`, `Z₀=R†R`). The published structure theory (Al-Ammari–Tisseur, standard triples of structured matrix polynomials, 2012; Tisseur–Higham, structured pseudospectra of polynomial eigenvalue problems, 2001) is the natural home for "when does this pencil's spectrum double" — it supplies invariants and perturbation lemmas the q>2 construction can quote instead of rediscover. *Why it matters:* the rank-1 edge needs the exact-q mechanism for the Im-alpha branch, and this converts it from random-matrix numerics into structured eigenvalue algebra.
2. **G.selrule-imalpha → pairing-by-symmetry (antiunitary/Kramers-type) + transversality.** Where a Hermitian matrix commutes with an anti-linear structure squaring to −1, every eigenvalue multiplicity is forced even — the AII/Kramers pairing mechanism (tenfold way, Ryu–Schnyder–Furusaki–Ludwig, NJP 12 (2010) 065010). A copy-space symmetry of `Q̃` with that property would *force* the double pair structurally, i.e. a positive selection mechanism; its absence in the tested ensembles would then be a checkable statement. Complement: Keller's codimension count for matrix classes with prescribed eigenvalue multiplicities (arXiv:0711.3948) gives the exact codimension of the "Im-alpha match ∧ norm match" locus, turning the bounded-negation exit into a transversality statement. *Why it matters:* it names the two published exits (forced doubling vs. codimension ≥1 genericity) for the edge's dilemma, without touching emergence.
3. **G.frameavg-residual → concentration of spectral measures + exact group-averaging calculus.** Meckes–Meckes (arXiv:1601.03720) give soft rates of convergence for empirical spectral measures; Guntuboyina–Leeb (arXiv:0810.2753) give concentration of the spectral measure for Wishart/Gram matrices with dependent entries — the two published inequalities closest to a frame-uniform residual bound for the Gram-dependent edge-length fraction. For the conjugacy-class side: the colored Weingarten calculus for block-unitary ensembles (arXiv:2609.30385) and symbolic Haar integrators (IntegrateUnitary.jl, arXiv:2605.23830) are current, citable tools for exactly the averages 075 had to do numerically. *Why it matters:* the rank-2 edge is a uniformity-of-convergence problem, and these are its standard lemmas.
4. **G.frameavg-conjclass (+ general) → published conjugacy-class/character saddle templates beyond 2408.05274.** The polarized-IKKT programme is now a second, independent saddle-template literature: Hartnoll–Liu (arXiv:2409.18706, 2504.06481), Komatsu–Martina–Penedones–Vuignier–Zhao I/II (arXiv:2410.18173, 2411.18678, localization), D'Hoker–Gutperle–Uhlemann (arXiv:2508.14959, 2609.35963), Ciceri–Samtleben (arXiv:2503.08771, 2511.23111), Fliss–Frenkel review (arXiv:2512.03163). *Why it matters:* the 2408.05274 saddle template the edge names is no longer the only published large-N saddle machinery for matrix integrals; subgroup/window control lemmas now exist in a form the reviewer can point at.
5. **G.selrule-mixedmult → Horn/Littlewood–Richardson combinatorics for tensor-product-induced spectra.** The C4 negation (uniform charge multiplicity `q₁q₂` or `q₁`, never per-factor `(q₁,q₂)`) says the in-class construction fails; the published way to ask *which* mixed-depth multiplicity patterns can arise from product-type (Kronecker) data is the saturation/LR-combinatorics machinery (Knutson–Tao, JAMS 1999; Knutson–Tao–Woodward, JAMS 2004). *Why it matters:* it turns "construct a mixed-multiplicity spectrum" into a realizability question with an established combinatorial answer format.

## (c) General scan notes

- **Matrix-model emergent spacetime is very much alive in 2024–2026, and NAMCG's inputs sit on the same tree.** Steinacker's line runs straight through: arXiv:2303.08012 (one-loop effective action/emergent gravity), 2405.09804, 2411.02598 (hs-extended gravity), 2503.19568 (FLRW Big Bang from matrix geometry), 2509.24753 (dynamical covariant quantum spacetime with fuzzy extra dimensions), 2510.21283, 2601.08031 (modified gravity on quantum spacetime), 2605.13294 (weak-coupling fluctuations), 2609.08955 ("an invitation" overview); HR' "accretion/mergers of fuzzy spheres" arXiv:2609.00328. NAMCG's Steinacker-type fuzzy-simplex dictionary inputs are consistent with the live line (not behind), but the field's frontier has moved to one-loop effective actions on stabilized branes, hs-extensions, and polarized/holographic IKKT — none of which reaches NAMCG's Problem G missing steps (i)–(iv). (All verified as listing records.)
- **Polarized IKKT / holography is a competing route to "gravity from a matrix integral"** (Hartnoll–Liu 2409.18706/2504.06481; Komatsu et al. 2410.18173/2411.18678; Ciceri–Samtleben 2503.08771/2511.23111; D'Hoker–Gutperle–Uhlemann 2508.14959/2609.35963; Najafian et al. 2611.03807). A referee will know these. They do not touch the selection-rule edge, but they sharpen the question of why NAMCG uses the semiclassical edge-charge route rather than localization. Liao 2609.40183 ("Criteria for the Emergence of Time" from Lie-algebra saddles) is the closest published analogue of a NAMCG-style emergence-criterion programme — worth one targeted read for vocabulary overlap with ch17.
- **Entanglement-equilibrium line:** Jacobson 1505.04753 (2015); **Bueno–Min–Speranza–Visser 1612.04374** (higher-derivative/Wald entropy — directly adjacent to NAMCG's quadratic terms, ch6/ch12); Alonso-Serrano–Liška 2212.03168 (quadratic gravity from entanglement equilibrium); Jacobson–Visser 1812.01596; Jensen–Sorce–Speranza 2306.01837 (type-II algebras version); Kumar 2404.16912 (generalized entropy without the equilibrium assumption — a caution: derivations exist that *drop* stationarity, relevant to NAMCG's ass:bdy-EE status).
