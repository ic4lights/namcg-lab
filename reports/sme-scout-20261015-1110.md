## 1. Problem A: matrix-to-modular map (Assumption ass:dictionary)

### Papers that could supply the missing step

- **R1. Gauss-law (edge-mode) entanglement of matrix algebras.**
  - Frenkel–Hartnoll, arXiv:2111.05967 (Quantum Hall matrix model), arXiv:2301.01325 (JHEP05(2023)084).
  - Fliss–Frenkel–Hartnoll–Soni, arXiv:2408.05274 (SciPost Phys. 18, 171 (2025)).
  - What they do:
    - They define subsystems of matrix quantum mechanics relationally. The geometric subregion is picked out by spectral projections, i.e. by the matrices themselves, not by an external metric.
    - The SU(N) Gauss law correlates the inside with the outside. The entropy is ln dim of the dominant representation.
    - After momentum-space coarse-graining this is an area law with coefficient ~ 1/(emergent Maxwell coupling).
    - The entanglement entropy of the relational subsystem becomes a minimisation over subregions, RT-like.
  - This is the only published construction I found that maps a matrix algebra to a geometric entropy without first putting in a metric.
- **R2. Modular-locality shortcut for the infrared theory.**
  - Papers:
    - Casini–Teste–Torroba, arXiv:1703.10656: null-plane modular Hamiltonians are local integrals of the stress tensor; the abstract states this for CFTs, with an algebraic proof.
    - Hollands–Longo, "A New Proof of the QNEC", arXiv:2503.04651 (Commun. Math. Phys. 406, 269 (2025)).
    - Ceyhan–Faulkner, arXiv:1812.04683.
    - Mohan–Thorlacius, arXiv:2606.10924: metric, curvature and the Einstein equation reconstructed from a matter operator algebra without the area law.
  - Mohan–Thorlacius needs the following (my reading of Sec. 2 up to ~100k characters): a Hadamard state, local Rindler subalgebras, Bisognano–Wichmann flows, half-sided modular inclusions, and a causal structure.
  - Mohan–Thorlacius says outright that it "assumed the existence of an underlying geometry". It is a test of consistency, not a source of a metric.
  - Reason for including R2: the manuscript's A asks for a microscopic H_D. If the matrix-derived IR theory is a Poincaré-covariant local QFT with a vacuum state, then H_D for wedges and small diamonds follows from standard algebraic QFT theorems and is not a new microscopic object. The Bisognano–Wichmann theorem is not independently verified here.
  - Consequence: A would then be inherited from D1, E and the locality and positivity of the infrared theory. It is not independent.
- **R3. Hilbert-space-only geometry.**
  - Cao–Carroll–Michalakis, arXiv:1606.08444 (Phys. Rev. D 95, 024031): "Space from Hilbert space".
  - Cao–Carroll, arXiv:1712.02803 (Phys. Rev. D 97, 086003): entanglement data on a factorised Hilbert space gives a spatial metric via Radon transforms, followed by a modified Jacobson argument for the weak-field Einstein equation.
  - This is the closest published analogue of NAMCG's U_ent[I(A:B)] route. The abstract presents the Einstein result as an argument, not a derivation.
  - Other tensor-network and holographic-code papers I found (e.g. arXiv:2102.12022, arXiv:2607.12047) do not supply anything NAMCG lacks. I do not recommend them as targets.
- **Background reference in matrix-model entanglement:** Gray–Sahakian–Warfield, arXiv:2103.06941 (JHEP08(2021)072). They find that entanglement entropy between a probe and a source in BMN and BFSS matrix theory is tied to local tidal acceleration. This is evidence of the idea, not a map.

### Precise sub-lemma the researcher could attempt (R1)

- **Hypotheses:**
  - The Poisson-limit leaf in the CP^{1,2} class (matrices T^a, base functions y^μ).
  - Large-N gauge algebra with a Gauss law.
  - A quasi-coherent state supported near that leaf.
  - A subregion defined by spectral projections of the matrices, not by the scaffold.
  - Coarse-graining as in the Frenkel–Hartnoll papers.
- **Expected form of the result:** the reduced state is block-diagonal over the representations of the gauge algebra. The Gauss-law entropy S_D = ln dim R_dom becomes, after coarse-graining, S_D = A[G](D)/(4G_ent). Here A[G] is the area in the Θ-built tensor G (not in a scaffold metric), and 1/G_ent ∝ 1/g_eff², with g_eff the emergent Yang–Mills coupling, which is a function on the leaf.
- **Deliverable (a gate):** the identity G_ent = G_eff of Problem F. Both come from the same matrix-derived operators. If G_ent ≠ G_eff, B's area term is not the heat-kernel coefficient, and that is itself a result.
- **NAMCG objects needed:** the 013/014 tree-level form S^(2) ≃ (m²ϰ/4g²)∫ΣF_i², the density ρ = 1/|Pf Θ|, the identification of subregions with spectral data, and the area as a function of G (not η).

### Constraints and no-gos to respect

- The Frenkel–Hartnoll results are for Euclidean or spatial gauged matrix quantum mechanics near a fuzzy sphere. A Lorentzian leaf needs a time slicing before the construction applies.
- The area coefficient depends on the coarse-graining. A scheme-independent statement is needed before it can be compared with G_eff.
- The Dorau–Much result and Mohan–Thorlacius assume a metric and local Rindler structure. Using either as an input to R1 or R2 would introduce a metric. This is the main emergence risk on A: do not let the Rindler or Killing-flow input come from G, η or the scaffold unless it is derived.
- Casini–Galante–Myers (arXiv:1601.00528, JHEP03(2016)194): see B.

### Feasibility and breakthrough or sharpening

- R1: low to medium feasibility. It is a breakthrough if the area coefficient matches G_eff structurally, a conditional sharpening otherwise.
- R2: medium feasibility, but it makes A a corollary of D1/E and Lorentz covariance. It reduces A without solving it. A reduction of this kind may in itself be worth the author's attention; the author alone decides what it means for A's scope.
- R3: low priority. It is a framework, not a derivation.

## 4. Problem D1: dynamical locking and local Lorentz

### Papers

- **R7. Local Lorentz in the IKKT covariant quantum spacetime.**
  - Steinacker–Tran, arXiv:2311.14163: Lorentz transformations are part of the gauge invariance in the covariant formulation (abstract-level).
  - Gaß–Steinacker, arXiv:2510.21283 (Phys. Rev. D 113, 024050 (2026)): "approximate diffeomorphisms" arising from matrix gauge transformations.
  - The review arXiv:2609.08955, Secs. 5.2 and 5.3, as extracted: SO(3,1) acts as spacelike isometries on the background, the background admits no local Lorentz boosts (footnote 19), and local Lorentz invariance of the classical tensorial sector is ensured through volume-preserving diffeomorphisms (Sec. 5.3).
  - Reading: in this programme local Lorentz is not a separate gauge symmetry. The frame is derived from the Poisson structure. This matches NAMCG's own finding (013) that the class has divergence-free diffeomorphisms and "12 = 3 inert + 3 gauge + 6 gauge-invariant".
- **R8. Emergent gauge symmetry and no-go theorems.**
  - Weinberg–Witten, Phys. Lett. B 96, 59 (1980) (Crossref confirmed).
  - Marolf, arXiv:1409.2509 (PRL 114, 031104): emergent nonlinear gravity requires kinematic non-locality. It holds on a lattice or in the continuum and needs neither Lorentz nor translation invariance. This is the abstract-level reading.
  - Jenkins, arXiv:0904.0453 (Int. J. Mod. Phys. D 18, 2249).
  - Carlip, arXiv:1207.2504.
  - Barceló–Carballo-Rubio–Di Filippo–Garay, arXiv:1608.07473 (JHEP10(2016)084), and Barceló–Carballo-Rubio–Garay–García-Moreno, arXiv:2108.06582 (Appl. Sci. 11, 8763): a mechanism for emergent linearised diffeomorphisms from a Lorentz-invariant linear tensor theory, with discussion of Weinberg–Witten and Marolf.
  - Why Marolf matters here: it agrees with 003 Lemma 2. A kinematically local theory (the Presentation-B scaffold) cannot give nonlinear gravity. A matrix algebra is kinematically non-local with respect to emergent spacetime. This supports the direction of the ledger's Presentation-A work and is a constraint on any attempt to rescue Presentation B.
- **R9. Spinor-loop Ward-identity template.**
  - Maitiniyazi–Matsuzaki–Oda–Yamada, arXiv:2309.16230 (Phys. Rev. D 109, 106018), "Irreversible vierbein postulate".
  - Maitiniyazi–Matsuzaki–Oda–Uchida–Yamada, arXiv:2609.14627.
  - Matsuzaki–Miyawaki–Oda–Yamada, arXiv:2003.07126 (Phys. Lett. B 813, 135975).
  - What they do:
    - The tree-level action has no inverse vierbein.
    - The induced action is computed with a background-field vierbein, in dimensional regularisation, with Ward–Takahashi identities for both general-coordinate and local-Lorentz symmetry.
    - It contains a cosmological term, EH, Weyl² (bracket coefficient −1/40 in their Eq. 43), a torsion mass and a torsion kinetic term.
    - They keep only the logarithmic part.
    - The unitarity of the Weyl² pole is left open (they defer to an FRG analysis).
  - Caveat for D1: these models impose local Lorentz at the cutoff. They are D2-type with respect to NAMCG's criterion, so they do not discharge D1. They do give a worked method for the computation 003 showed was missing.
- **R10. Twisted spectral triples.** Martinetti–Nieuviarts–Zeitoun, arXiv:2401.07848 (Lorentz group as a normal subgroup of the twisted unitaries, torsion from the twist); Nieuviarts, arXiv:2502.18105, arXiv:2512.15450. The twist is extra structure, not part of the matrix model. Low priority.

## 5. Problem E: signature

### Papers

- **R11. Classification and the Casimir obstruction.** Liao, "Lie Algebra Saddles in the IKKT Matrix Model and Criteria for the Emergence of Time", arXiv:2609.40183 (30 Sep 2026; abstract-level only). As summarised from the abstract page:
  - Ten matrices forming a real Lie algebra reduce the equations of motion to an algebraic equation for a symmetric bilinear form.
  - For simple complexified algebras a Casimir obstruction forces the form to vanish, which is why fuzzy-sphere and expanding-universe solutions need mass terms.
  - Every nondegenerate Lorentzian saddle in at most eleven dimensions has a nonzero solvable radical, so "time must be solvable".
  - NAMCG's own 014 finding is the same phenomenon: T^a = M^{a4} is not stationary for the quartic term alone, because {T_a,{T^a,T^b}} = −3T^b, and stationarity requires the bilinear term with μ² = −3/R². So the "input" bilinear term is forced, not optional, in this class.
- **R12. Numerical emergence of Lorentzian expansion.**
  - Kim–Nishimura–Tsuchiya, arXiv:1108.1540 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 108, 011601).
  - Nishimura, arXiv:2205.04726 (Corfu proceedings): the Lorentzian model connects smoothly to the Euclidean one under Wick rotation, with SO(10) breaking to SO(3); a Lorentz-invariant mass term is added for the complex Langevin method.
  - Chou–Nishimura–Wang, arXiv:2507.18472 (Monte Carlo of the polarised IKKT).
  - These are numerical evidence on the SSB pattern, not a derivation of η_IJ.
- **R13. Emergent Lorentz invariance from RG, and its constraints.**
  - Collins–Perez–Sudarsky–Urrutia–Vucetich, arXiv:gr-qc/0403053 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 191301): Lorentz violation at the cutoff feeds into low energies without fine-tuning.
  - Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov, arXiv:1305.0011; Kharuk–Sibiryakov, arXiv:1505.04130; Anber–Donoghue, arXiv:1102.0789 (Phys. Rev. D 83, 105027).
  - Carroll–Harvey–Kostelecký–Lane–Okamoto, arXiv:hep-th/0105082 (Phys. Rev. Lett. 87, 141601): a constant Θ gives Lorentz violation. The covariant quantum spacetime and NAMCG's fibre-averaging classes (011, 013) are the way around this.
  - Girelli–Liberati–Sindoni, arXiv:0806.4239 (Phys. Rev. D 79, 044019): emergence of Lorentzian signature and scalar gravity (record verified via arXiv API only).
- **Spectral-triple signature change:** Nieuviarts, arXiv:2402.05839, arXiv:2502.18105, arXiv:2512.15450: Lorentzian from Riemannian via a twist, needing an almost-commutative structure. Extra structure.
- **A caution on a related result:** Komatsu–Martina–Penedones–Vuignier–Zhao, arXiv:2411.18678 (Part II): the original IKKT model is different from the polarised IKKT in the limit of vanishing mass deformation (as stated in the abstract). A mass-type input is therefore not innocuous nonperturbatively.

### Sub-lemma (R11, cheap)

- **Hypotheses:** Definition def:matrix-coords-ext, the Poisson limit, a real Lie-Poisson algebra, η_IJ either Lorentzian or Euclidean.
- **Expected form:** a classification statement for NAMCG's classes: for which signatures of the invariant form (and with which bilinear term) a stationary background with a Lorentzian induced tensor exists. The 014 μ² = −3/R² would become a corollary.
- **Result type:** sharpening of E, and it explains an assumption already in the manuscript.

### Constraints

- Signature in IKKT-type models is inherited from the ambient η_IJ. Deriving it from a Euclidean positive measure remains open in all the literature I found. I found no published derivation to import.
- Wick-rotation equivalence (Nishimura) means signature is a contour or saddle statement, and the NAMCG text already says Euclidean positivity does not select Lorentzian.

### Feasibility

- E is the hardest problem to advance. R11 is a cheap sharpening, R12 is evidence only, R13 is constraint. I see no route to a breakthrough on E in the current literature.

## 6. Problem F: coefficients A, B, C and G_eff

### Papers
