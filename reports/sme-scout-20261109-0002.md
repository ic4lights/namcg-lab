### G-R1 (TOP): conditional sub-lemma for ass:Sent-density from matrix-partition entanglement
- What the literature proves (all verified): Anous–Karczmarek–Mintun–Van Raamsdonk–Way, "Areas and entropies in BFSS/gravity duality", arXiv:1911.11145 (SciPost Phys. 8, 057 (2020)) — BFSS has no factorized Hilbert space over spatial regions; any region "entropy" must be "more general than the usual subsystem entanglement entropies". Hampapura–Harper–Lawrence, arXiv:2012.15683 (JHEP 10 (2021) 231) and follow-up arXiv:2105.13726 — a U(1) link-variable reformulation of matrix models with gauge-invariant edge modes, giving a factorization substitute defined on matrix/link data, not on a pre-existing geometry. Han–Hartnoll, arXiv:1906.08781 (PRX 10, 011069 (2020)) and arXiv:2111.05967; Han–Hartnoll–Mezei arXiv:1611.06336 — spatial-partition factorization of matrix quantum mechanics on fuzzy spaces with boundary-law EE. Caution: the Han–Hartnoll line partitions regions of an already-emergent fuzzy manifold; its area coefficient is NOT transferable without assuming geometry.
- Gap vs the ledger: none of these computes the entropy of a NAMCG finite-N measure, and none gives the full eq:Sent-exp (the α₁ R^(h) and α₂φ² terms need a curved background — out of one-cycle reach). What one cycle can realistically deliver: a conditional sub-lemma computing the leading α₀-type term (flat condensate background, Presentation B measure) of S_ent^IR for a specified matrix-index or link partition, with explicit statement of which generalized entropy functional is being used (the Anous et al. constraint), and a proof of the locality/boundedness conditions under which a dim-3 density is definable. Conditional result; G stays OPEN.
- Feasibility: medium. Conditional sharpening, not a breakthrough; matches the 052–060 deliverable style.
- Competition flags: arXiv:2602.02683 (area-law terms in c=1 matrix-model entanglement — nearest published analogue; a close relative could appear any time) and arXiv:2407.13336 (BFSS Page curve). Neither covers NAMCG's measure, but they show the technique is live and crowded.
