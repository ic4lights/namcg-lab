## 2. Routes

**R1 — Spectral-positivity (Källén–Lehmann-type) sublemma for the composite spin-two two-point function. Target: N.2; the only route aimed at the actual missing step.**
Shape: if the matrix model is a finite-dimensional unitary quantum system, a composite spin-two operator's two-point function should admit a spectral representation with positive measure; a ghost pole would require a negative-residue real pole, which contradicts positivity below Λ_UV. Hypotheses to state: unitarity of the matrix Hamiltonian; existence of the large-N connected two-point function Π₂(s); the condensate-vacuum sector lies in the physical Hilbert space (no indefinite metric in the fluctuation expansion around (Φ, X̂)). Expected form: "for s < Λ_UV², Im Π₂(s) ≥ 0 and the residue of any pole of the spin-two two-point function is positive; hence no propagating Ostrogradsky ghost for p < Λ_UV." NAMCG objects needed: the fluctuation expansion giving eq:W4 (ch09 L62) and eq:stelle's C² coefficient −α/2, plus the definition of the composite graviton two-point function from the matrix path integral.
Established elsewhere? Ostrogradsky/EFT literature handles ghosts in Lagrangian settings (de Rham–Matas JCAP 06 (2016) 041, arXiv:1604.08638; de Rham et al. PRL 100, 251603 (2008), arXiv:0711.2072); composite-graviton papers confront the ghost by construction, not by positivity theorem (Carone, MPLA, DOI 10.1142/S0217732320300025, explicitly "avoid that limit and the associated ghost concern" — abstract-level). The positivity-theorem shape for an induced/composite graviton appears unoccupied (metadata-level, above).
Feasibility: hard. The obstruction to expect: a KL representation may fail for composite operators in a strongly coupled condensate sector, or the fluctuation propagator may be defined only on-shell around the locked vacuum. Success = genuine breakthrough toward N; failure, if written as a *recorded* obstruction (why positivity is unavailable in this hypothesis class), is still a legitimate reproducible-obstruction package.

**R2 — Form-factor completion criterion for the resummed F(x). Targets: N.2; sharpens the existing appH conditional block L3453–3477.**
The existing block's weakness (its own L3465–3477 says so): everything is conditional on the truncated fixed-L identity — i.e., on F(x) = 1 − (x/Υ)(ln(1/x)+δ_W) being the whole story. Published anchors: Barvinsky–Vilkovisky, "Covariant perturbation theory (II). Second order in the curvature. General algorithms", NPB 333, 471–511 (1990), DOI 10.1016/0550-3213(90)90047-h (Crossref-verified this run; the manuscript bib record `BarvinskyVilkovisky1990` NPB 333 471–511 1990 matches but carries no DOI — adding the DOI is bookkeeping the integrator could do only with gatekeeper-accepted text). Also Feng–Yang arXiv:2509.03444, JHEP 01 (2026) 072; Boos–Carone, JHEP 06 (2023) 017, arXiv:2212.00861v3; Corianò et al. arXiv:2404.09225 (all Atom-verified this run) for the structure of two-point form factors beyond second curvature order.
Sub-lemma a researcher could attempt: *prove that any completion of the spin-two form factor by higher-curvature covariant form factors (in the BV class, which is the class the induced-vertices framework actually generates) that preserves the log coefficient at O(p⁴) cannot move the first zero of F below Λ_UV unless δ_W crosses the already-stated bounds* — i.e., stability of the zero-free region under BV-class completions. What this would and would not give: a conditional sharpening, still not matrix-derived (ch17 L515–516 keeps it a scope-limited model), but it converts "conditional on the truncated identity" into "conditional on a named, controlled class of completions" — real value, honestly bounded.

**R3 — Degeneracy / constraint loophole to Ostrogradsky. Target: N.2.**
Published anchors (Atom-verified): de Rham–Matas, JCAP 06 (2016) 041, arXiv:1604.08638 (Ostrogradsky evaded when momenta are degenerate / constraints remove the dangerous mode); Berkhahn–Hofmann–Niedermann, PRD 86, 124022 (2012), arXiv:1205.6801, "From a No-Go to a No-Ghost Theorem" (the dangerous spin-2 mode becomes non-dynamical in a regime); Hohm–Naseer–Zwiebach, JHEP 08 (2016) 173, arXiv:1607.01784 (constraints in HD gravity remove the ghost).
