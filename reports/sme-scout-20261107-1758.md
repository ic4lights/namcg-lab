AMS 168 (2004) 1-65) and matrix algebras converging to the sphere/coadjoint orbits with Leibniz seminorms (math/0108005, Mem. AMS 168(796) 67-91; 0707.3229; 0910.1968; 2108.01136, Comm. Math. Phys., DOI 10.1007/s00220-023-04682-0). Latremoliere's propinquity (1302.4058) gives *-isomorphism at distance zero; explicit fuzzy-tori-to-quantum-tori estimates (1312.0069, "an explicit approach"); **2102.03729, Comm. Math. Phys. 388 (2021) 1049-1128** proves fuzzy-tori *spectral triples* converge to the quantum-tori spectral triple in spectral propinquity, **including the Dirac-generated dynamics**; 1805.11229 (covariant propinquity) upgrades this to convergence of *actions/dynamical systems* — matrix commutator derivations converging to the torus translation action. Latest sequel: 2607.01681 (2026, "twisted tale").
Import without a fundamental metric: take NAMCG's X_hat algebra as the finite-dimensional algebra; define the Weyl map as an explicit quantum-metric bridge; state "[X_hat,Phi] <-> i Theta D Phi with error O(Theta^2)" as a covariant-propinquity bound on convergence of the commutator-action to the torus action. Obstructions: theorems cover compact/flat (torus, constant-Theta) settings; causal-diamond localization and variable Theta are outside the proved class; the seminorm must be Leibniz, and for interacting H the commutator seminorm need not be — that is the exact hard step. Feasibility: high as a theorem-level sharpening of the existing free/Poisson lemma; breakthrough-level (interacting, variable Theta) not in literature.

**2. Weyl/semiclassical pseudodifferential calculus: explicit O(hbar^2) remainders for commutator = i hbar Poisson bracket.**
Established (theorem-level, classical): for Weyl quantization on symbol classes, Op(f)Op(g) = Op(f #_h g) with norm-controlled remainders; [Op(f),Op(g)] = i h Op({f,g}) + O(h^2) in operator norm (standard Calderon-Vaillancourt/Shubin-type calculus). Rieffel's strict deformation quantization for R^d actions (funct-an/9508001, Canad. J. Math. 49 (1997) 160-174) proves the *classical limit of dynamics* in norm for its deformed C*-dynamical systems; Grosse-Lechner realize it on Moyal space at QFT level (0706.3992, JHEP 0711 (2007) 012; 0808.3459, JHEP 0809 (2008) 131). Exactness of the Moyal bracket for polynomials of degree <=2 (used in NAMCG's free-sector lemma) is the standard textbook identity.
Import: apply symbol calculus to K_0 = 1/2 Theta^{-1 mu nu} X_mu [X_nu, H] and turn appH:26-36's "exact at constant Theta" into an explicit O(Theta^2) norm bound on a weighted symbol class; then propagate per-order errors for H_int via the remainder terms.
Obstructions: norm bounds require bounded/decaying symbols; the boost/diamond charge Theta^{-1} X P is unbounded, and diamond localization (sharp characteristic cutoff of X_0) breaks the calculus at the boundary — literature offers no ready estimates there. Books (Shubin 1987; Dimassi-Sjostrand 1999; Rieffel Mem. AMS 106 (1993)) are standard references but not independently verified online this session (marked: not independently verified).
