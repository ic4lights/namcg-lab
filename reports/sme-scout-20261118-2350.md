## R2. Coupled frame/weight layers; theorems forcing or breaking product structure
- Hewitt–Savage, *Symmetric measures on Cartesian products*, Trans. AMS 80 (1955) 470, DOI 10.1090/s0002-9947-1955-0076206-8 (Crossref-verified). Exchangeability forces conditional product (mixture-of-products) structure in the infinite limit; at finite N it forces mixture, not independence. Consequence for axis 1: a genuinely non-product slot-frame law must break slot exchangeability or be a mixture of products — any "coupling" that is exchangeable across slots is asymptotically re-imported input data. This is a published structural constraint the researcher must respect.
- Standard facts (Muilhead, *Aspects of Multivariate Statistical Theory*; Meckes, *The Real Haar Distribution*) **[not independently verified — print]**: (a) any O(D)-invariant slot law factorizes exactly as radial x uniform-spherical, so within that class the frame layer is Haar and independent of weights — 089's factorization is not an artifact of Gaussians; (b) sample-covariance eigenvectors are exactly Haar, independent of the population covariance — covariance-quadratic coupling cannot transmit to the frame eigenvector layer; (c) normalized independent gamma slots give Dirichlet(alpha) — beta-gamma algebra (Letac–Mora; Muirhead) classifies ALL "nice" simplex pushforwards, and each is again determined by input slot parameters. These three facts collectively shrink axes 1-2 and should be cited as the reason.
- RTN max-flow, Fitter–Loulidi–Nechita, arXiv:2407.02559 (2024): limiting reduced-density eigenvalue law computed from cut structure — a genuine theorem where weights are induced by non-independent network geometry, not re-imported. Established. This is the strongest existence proof for axis 1.
- No published theorem was found forcing non-product frame/weight laws on the simplex from a single scalar frame measure; nothing found coupling Stiefel frame to Dirichlet weights beyond the flat case. Search negative.

## Ranked routes for target 090
1. **Axis 3 (interaction-generated induced log-densities) — strongest support.** The whole R3 corpus supplies the missing structural ingredient 089 lacked: with interaction, a featureless input yields a provably non-flat interior equilibrium profile (flat-V Wigner case; Dirichlet/Jacobi simplex ensembles; Kaminaga's Poisson–Dirichlet weights). Valuable as a negative closure, low new-positive value.
4. **Axis 4 (pi[g] self-consistency)** — least literature traction; Dyson-Brownian stationary measures are the only analogue; low feasibility of a published-grade result, and 088's rigidity bounds it.
