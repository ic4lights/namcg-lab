## Route assessment (ranked)

**Route 1 — S1 via Borchers structure theorem + Wiesbrock equivalence (modular-covariance criterion).** HIGH value. The published content: an inclusion N⊂M of standard subspaces/algebras is half-sided modular iff it is modularly covariant (Δ_M^{it}NΔ_M^{-it}⊂N, t≥0), and conversely Borchers' theorem gives half-sidedness from covariance under a one-parameter group with the right semigroup action; Araki–Zsidó extend the structure theorem from vector states to common faithful semifinite weights. Consequence for NAMCG: the sub-step is NOT "construct HSMI from a single self-adjoint K" — no such theorem exists (see no-go notes) — but it IS equivalent to a checkable lemma: prove modular covariance of the matrix-algebra pair under the K_0-generated flow. That converts the missing step into a concrete finite-N algebraic identity. Verified records: Wiesbrock, CMP 1993, DOI 10.1007/bf02098019 and LMP 1993, DOI 10.1007/bf00750303; Borchers, "The CPT-theorem in two-dimensional theories of local observables", CMP 1992, DOI 10.1007/BF02099011; Araki–Zsidó, arXiv:math/0412061. Feasibility: a genuine conditional sharpening — the theorem is off-the-shelf, but the hypothesis (standard pair + covariance at finite N with error control) is real research. Emergence-safe.

**Route 2 — S1/S2 hybrid via warped convolution of half-sided inclusions.** HIGH. Lechner–Scotford, "Deformations of half-sided modular inclusions and non-local chiral field theories", arXiv:2111.03172 (verified via arXiv API record): warped convolution — a deformation built from commutators with position-like self-adjoint operators — deforms second-quantized HSMIs and *preserves* half-sidedness, with modular data under control. This is structurally the exact operation NAMCG's dictionary performs ([X̂,·]-type deformations of an algebra that already has geometric modular flow). If NAMCG can anchor on a sector with known geometric HSMI, warped convolution gives a published mechanism to track what the commutator deformation does to modular data. Sub-lemma to attempt: identify when the [X̂,·]-deformed matrix net is unitarily equivalent / close to a warped-convolution deformation of the free net. Feasibility: moderate; conditional sharpening; does not by itself cross the interacting gap.

## Published no-gos / obstructions inside NAMCG's stated hypothesis class

1. **Associativity ⇒ Poisson.** For any associative star product on C^∞(R^4) whose order-Θ term is the bracket of a variable bivector Θ(x), the order-Θ² associator vanishing forces [Θ,Θ]=0 (Schouten). This is the BFFLS/Kontsevich integrability condition (DOI 10.1016/0003-4916(78)90224-5; q-alg/9709040), and the 058 integrated text already states it correctly. Consequence: for non-Schouten-closed Θ(x), *no* associative continuum product with that classical limit exists; the only published escapes are (a) correction of the leading bivector itself (S3: select Poisson classes from the state), (b) twist/quasi-Hopf deformation (Route 10), or (c) abandoning the classical-limit reading (nonassociative products). The researcher should decide explicitly which branch Assumption ass:dictionary commits to.
2. **No single-operator HSMI construction exists and the naive statement is false in general**: modular covariance/HSMI requires a *pair* of standard subspaces with semigroup covariance (Borchers; Wiesbrock equivalence); spectral positivity of one generator K, even with cyclic separating vector, does not make an inclusion half-sided. Any NAMCG lemma claiming this without the Borchers-type hypotheses would be wrong. Koot's criterion (arXiv:2503.18036) is the correct published replacement.
3. **Weinberg–Witten-type no-gos:** I checked nothing in the searched material that bears on Problem A directly (Problem A is a map-derivation problem, not a massless-spin-2 compositeness problem); no relevant conflict found. Not further pursued.
