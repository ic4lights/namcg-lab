## Direct answer to the scoped question

The literature DOES supply induced-profile-measure mechanisms — the scoped question is not a no-go — but with one sharp structural caveat the researcher must absorb first: every canonical induction that starts from an EXCHANGEABLE / label-blind microscopic weight produces a relabelling-SYMMETRIC profile density (Wigner Jacobian, collective field, character expansion, Sanov). By the integrated 088 Lemma-4 dichotomy (appH L3189) such weights are exactly the "symmetric" case: uniform among minimisers or degenerate relabelling orbits. A profile weight that can select a non-uniform profile requires ONE of: (i) a non-convex induced log-density (interaction-generated; uniform becomes unstable), or (ii) label-asymmetric microscopic data pushed through a label-sensitive profile map. Routes R-A and R-B below instantiate (i); R-C instantiates the quotient reading of (i); R-E is the literature for (ii) (partly unverifiable). None of the eight routes touches the frame law, so the avenue-(a) closure (post-18) and the 087 measure-blindness theorem are untouched by all of them.

## Ranked routes

**R-A. Integrate-out / collective-field induced profile action (strongest form answer to "produced by the core").** Verified (arXiv API feeds, 2026-11-18): hep-th/9112045v2 (Lechtenfeld, "Semiclassical Approach to Finite-N Matrix Models" — "Jacobian arising by changing variables"); hep-th/9201037v1 (Feinberg, "String Field Theory for d≤0 Matrix Models via Marinari-Parisi" — "Jacobian from matrix coordinates to collective modes"); hep-th/9312124v1 (Lechtenfeld, "Collective Field Theory for D=0 Matrix Models"); hep-th/9202019v1 (Ben-Menahem, "D=0 Matrix Model as Conjugate Field Theory"); and the dynamical non-convex instance arXiv:0905.2406v1 (Bringoltz, "Large-N volume reduction of lattice QCD with adjoint Wilson fermions at weak-coupling" — feed abstract states explicitly: "one-loop calculation of the free energy as a function of the holonomy", stable phase identified by the Z_N-symmetric configuration). Mechanism: change of variables in the microscopic matrix measure from configurations to profile variables (eigenvalue density; in EK, holonomy profile) — the profile functional is then EXACTLY a pushforward: Jacobian of the map times the integral over the fibre; integrating out massive/hidden modes gives a one-loop effective potential on profile space, which is non-convex in the phase where the uniform profile is unstable (EK center-broken phase). This is the published template for "core weight on configuration space -&gt; derived weight on profile space" by fibre integration, at fixed N (Lechtenfeld) as well as large-N.
Transfer to the block-cut profile map: **YES in form** — the missing ingredient named in the integrated audit (a weight on profile space produced by the core) is, mechanistically, exactly the fibre integral of a core weight over the fibre of the block-cut profile map; classical matrix models perform this for the eigenvalue/conjugacy-class maps. **PARTIAL in substance** — nobody has performed it for a block-row-variance-type map (not the invariant spectrum): no published Jacobian or fibre integral for a map that is coarser than eigenvalues and label-sensitive; and the EK one-loop is a perturbative holonomy potential in gauge data, proxy-level for NAMCG edge data. Difficulty MEDIUM-HIGH; the Jacobian of a non-invariant profile map plus the fibre contraction is real work, and success would be a conditional sharpening for NAMCG unless the resulting stationary set is shown non-uniform. Rank 1.

**R-B.
