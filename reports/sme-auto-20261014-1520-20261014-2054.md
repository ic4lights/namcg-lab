## Item assessments

### 002: Problem N ghost-pole criterion (eecd202; appH, ch09)
1. What it does: in the truncated heat-kernel identity, the induced Weyl term gives a spin-2 pole with m_2^2 = -Upsilon Lambda^2/L, Upsilon = pi/(G_eff W_C Lambda^2). In a leading-log form-factor model, no zero lies in |k^2| < Lambda^2 iff Upsilon > Upsilon_*(delta_W), with Upsilon_*(0) = pi/2.
2. Classification: correctly labelled conditional (the closing text says "not closure", "Problems F and N remain open"). I spot-checked the scalar coefficients: c_C = 1/120 per real scalar and c_R = (1/2)(1/6 - Q)^2 are the standard a_2 values. With A_1 = N_0/6 and W_C = N_0/120, Upsilon = A_1/(2W_C) = 10 follows. The Lambert-W zero formula and the Upsilon_*(0) = pi/2 crossing at x = ±i are consistent with F(x) as written.
3. Impact: mixed. It gives N a concrete, checkable target. It also shows that with the stated added continuation convention the induced Weyl sign is the ghostly one, and that Upsilon = 10 for minimal scalars. That is a negative-leaning hint for the unmodified scalar content, since the pole lies where the fixed-L form is invalid. The text correctly says this is a property of the form, not a prediction.
4. Emergence: preserved. The metric is not introduced fundamentally; it is the composite metric.
5. Field context: induced Weyl and R^2 terms with the Stelle spectrum are standard (Stelle 1977 is already cited in the manuscript). I did not independently verify Stelle. Nonlocal one-loop form factors are in Barvinsky-Vilkovisky (verified, see below). I found no published treatment of the Upsilon criterion for a matrix-model induced action, so this is new within a conditional model.
6. Concerns:
   - Major (presentation): the criterion rests on three added inputs, namely the "curvature invariants continued without change of sign" convention, a constant delta_W that is not computed, and the assumption that the Weyl form factor decays for s k^2 >> 1. The text flags all three, but the ch09 sentence "a truncated induced Weyl term has this sign" will be read by referees as a stronger statement than the continuation convention supports.
   - Minor: the 2 - gamma_E form-factor illustration is a numerical result for a different (potential-insertion) kernel. It is not the Weyl kernel and is correctly described as an illustration.
   - Minor: the ch17 alpha gloss lengthens one already-dense sentence.

### 003: prop:scaffold correction, ass:emergent-op, scaffold no-go (85f49d0; appH, ch03, ch04, ch05, ch08, appB, appG)
1. What it does: it retracts the claim that the scaffold action induces EH for the composite metric (the conformal-mode counterexample shows the claim false). It records the operator-on-emergent-metric premise as Assumption ass:emergent-op. It adds Lemma 1 (no Lambda^4, Lambda^2 or ln Lambda terms with derivatives of Phi at fixed A_mu) and a Lemma 2 no-go for linearized diffeomorphism invariance of h within H1-H4.
2. Classification: accurate. This is a correction of a materially false claim plus a conditional no-go. The no-go statement carefully lists the relaxable hypotheses (Theta-built regulator, non-invariant vacuum, curved scaffold, non-linear composite map). The sentence "the opposite-sign term of Z-sigma is not produced in this calculation" is honest.
3. Impact: mixed, and net positive for integrity. It is a setback for the idea that Presentation B generates G_eff by itself, so ass:emergent-op becomes load-bearing and is now in the ch16 inventory. It clarifies where the content must come from (Presentation A / a Theta-built structure), which is the structural direction 008 and 009 explore.
4. Emergence: preserved. The scaffold metric g^(0) is non-dynamical and the physical metric is not it. The correction removes a place where the scaffold was being treated as physical.
5. Field context: the no-go has the same logical shape as the standard fact that a Lorentz-covariant theory of massless spin 2 needs gauge invariance, which is the Weinberg-Witten setting. Lemma 2 does not use Weinberg-Witten and does not prove its applicability. Weinberg-Witten and its loopholes (emergent gauge invariance, emergent manifold, holography) are documented in the literature, but I did not verify the original paper. Steinacker's IKKT work obtains EH from the one-loop action on a brane with extra fuzzy dimensions (arXiv:2303.08012, verified), which is consistent with NAMCG's finding that the four-coordinate scaffold sector lacks this ingredient.
6. Concerns:
   - Major: Lemma 2's reasoning "Q_p depends only on p^2 hence cannot be invariant" is correct for the stated class, but the class (h a fixed linear image of Phi) is narrow. A referee will read "no-go" as broader than it is. The text does restrict the claim; keep that restriction prominent in any abstract or Chapter 1 summary.
   - Minor: the Delta Z computation relies on a particular exponential-integral regulator; the text says "within one-loop" and conditions it accordingly.

### 005: Presentation-A Poisson-limit induced-curvature computation (9c68802; appH)
1. What it does: in the Poisson limit it shows sqrt|G|(R_G/6 - E) = rho R[g_rho]/6 for scalar fluctuations. For the connection sector it finds (4/6) rho R + rho^2 X with ghosts -2/6 rho R. About constant Theta the quadratic eigenvalues are +1, -1/4, -3/4, and the net TT coefficient is 1 - N_tot/24. It also gives a numerical cubic-order test that rho^2 X is not a combination of the two covariant densities.
2. Classification: labelled conditional, not closure, with an explicit list of what is not covered (Moyal terms, fermions, gauge dependence, Phi-a mixing). Accurate. The cubic non-covariance statement is correctly limited to the sampled class: eta-using rewritings are untested. I did not re-run the numerical test.
3. Impact: mixed. Positive: the scalar identity is a clean demonstration that the heat-kernel curvature coefficient of a Theta-built operator is the curvature of g_rho. Negative: the connection sector contains a non-covariant X term, and the net TT sign requires N_tot > 24, an input-dependent condition. The text does not claim G_eff or its sign.
4. Emergence: preserved. G is built from Theta, and no fundamental metric is introduced; eta enters only as the matrix-coordinate contraction, which the text states.
5. Field context: this is the same class of computation as Steinacker's one-loop IKKT work (arXiv:2303.08012, verified at abstract level: EH induced at one loop, with G_N set by a Kaluza-Klein scale of fuzzy extra dimensions). That paper obtains the EH coefficient with a compact fuzzy K and the full 10D model including fermions and supersymmetry, which NAMCG's bosonic 4D sector does not have. I did not check how Steinacker handles the analogue of the X term. This is a real point of departure that I could not resolve from abstracts. NAMCG is therefore behind the field in content (no fermions, no extra dimensions) but is more explicit about the Poisson-limit obstruction.
6. Concerns:
   - Major: the use of eta contracted with matrix indices (and the derivative index of X contracted with eta rather than G) means an implicit background tensor eta is present in the connection sector. The text says so. Whether this counts as a fundamental metric in the emergence sense is a conceptual question the author should decide. I do not call it a violation because eta is the matrix-coordinate (target-space) metric, as in IKKT, not the spacetime metric.
   - Minor: the statement "net coefficient has the sign of a positive induced EH term only if N_tot > 24" depends on a Euclidean sign convention flagged in the text. A referee will want it cross-checked against the Euclidean/Lorentzian sign used in ch08.

### 008: four-matrix conditional obstruction (3ab80c9; appH)
1. What it does: for four matrix coordinates in the Poisson limit it shows the map Theta -> G has image of dimension 6 and codimension 4 in the ten metric components, with a quadratic relation. The linear response about constant Theta is 3-dimensional. The linearized Einstein operator has rank 3 versus 6 conserved source components, so E[h] = T fails for generic conserved T without an extra derived stress. K is not proportional to g^-1 pointwise for real Lorentzian g, and the tree-level quadratic action is algebraic in (dG, d rho).
2. Classification: correctly an obstruction within H_A (stated list of conditions). The nonlinear 3-versus-7 count is labelled a counting argument. The Cayley-Hamilton-type relation (K eta)^2 - (1/2) tr(K eta) K eta - Pf^2 I = 0 follows from the characteristic polynomial quoted (eigenvalues -N^2 doubly degenerate), which I checked by hand for consistency. I did not re-run any script.
3. Impact: mixed. It is a negative result for the four-coordinate class, but it points to what is needed (N >= 8 coordinates or a derived extra stress) and so narrows the search. The rank-6-versus-10 statement fits the observation that a generic metric has four more degrees of freedom than a single Theta can supply.
4. Emergence: preserved. The obstruction concerns how G is built from Theta; the "assumed dictionary" for D >= 4 coordinates is explicitly labelled as outside the class.
5. Field context: IKKT/Steinacker already use 10 matrix coordinates with a 4D brane and fuzzy extra dimensions (arXiv:2303.08012, 2312.01317, 2601.08031, all verified at abstract level), so the N >= 8 route is the field's own setting. The four-matrix obstruction therefore fits with published structure: the 4D-only sector is too small. The Kumar-Steinacker modified Einstein equations (anharmonicity tensor, dilaton and axion contributions) are an example of an extra stress in the one-loop theory. Whether that "extra derived stress" is the same object as NAMCG's T_ent is a possible comparison I did not check beyond abstracts. NAMCG is consistent with the field here, and its obstruction is a new, explicit statement of a known structural limit rather than a conflict.
6. Concerns:
   - Major: the abstract-level framing "E[h] = T fails" could be misread as an unconditional no-go for emergent gravity from matrix models. The closing paragraph restricts the statement correctly (four coordinates, Poisson limit, constant eta, linear order about constant Theta, generic p); keep it so.
   - Minor: the Poisson limit and constant-Theta linearization drop Moyal and non-constant-background effects, which could in principle change the rank count. The text lists this.
