## Route R1 — rank 2: permutahedron interior identification (feasibility: easy; value: consumes the rank, provenance not novelty)
The target statement — the image of the diagonal map on the unitary orbit of a fixed non-scalar Hermitian spectrum equals the Schur–Horn permutahedron {d ≺ λ} cut by the trace hyperplane — is the classical Schur–Horn theorem (I. Schur, 1923; A. Horn, Pacific J. Math. 4 (1954) 620–626 — classical, **not independently verified** this pass; no open arXiv record). Two modern fibre-side papers I did verify and which the researcher should read:
- Augustin-Liviu Mare, "Connectivity properties of the Schur-Horn map for real Grassmannians", arXiv:2309.11596v2 (2023/2024). Fibre-connectedness of the Schur–Horn map; the real-Grassmannian analogue where connectedness is nontrivial. NAMCG's 098 already exploits unitary-orbit connectedness (trivial in U(D), nontrivial in O(D)) — this is the published home of that argument, and Mare's fibre-structure results are the template for the "interior" strengthening.
- Hengzhun Chen; Yingzhou Li, "On the Continuity of Schur-Horn Mapping", arXiv:2407.00701v2 (2024, rev. 2026). Continuity/saturation behaviour of the diagonal map including boundary points — directly relevant to the "interior" wording of the rank.
The general D proof under the no-citation discipline is one page once majorization and the doubly-stochastic/Birkhoff parametrization are stated (the frontier's own 096/097 fibre facts already give the containment directions pointwise). Classification warning: the honest output is "specialization of the classical Schur–Horn theorem, self-contained", not a new identification; the review-095/096/097/098 flag "Schur-Horn literature not citable without a self-contained proof" should survive as the package's own framing.

## Route R2 — alternate venue 1: exact general-D conditional density of P2 (feasibility: medium; value: sharpens, never closes)
The conditional density of the diagonal given the spectrum is the Duistermaat–Heckman measure of the U(D) coadjoint orbit pushed by the diagonal moment-map coordinate: a piecewise-polynomial function of the target diagonal given by a type-A Kostant partition (vector-partition) function; the P2 density is its integral over the intersection of the permutahedron with a quadric. Closest verified contact: Hariharan Narayanan; Piyush Srivastava, "Deterministically approximating the volume of a Kostka polytope", arXiv:2503.06459v3, Int. Math. Res. Not. 2026(6) rnag036 — volumes of exactly this moment-polytope class via partition functions (Kostka numbers = type-A weight multiplicities = DH values). The standard DH/Kostant-partition statements themselves I could **not** verify as open records this pass (mark not independently verified). At D=3 an exact piecewise form is by-hand feasible from 096's patch Jacobian; general D is the piecewise-polynomial theorem. Consistent with venue 1's own label: sharpens G.moments, closes nothing.

## Route R3 — MI((tau,u);x) vs I(u;x) full-spectrum conditioning (feasibility: easy; no-new-mechanism verdict applies)
Literature check is negative and I state it: an arXiv full-text search pairing "mutual information" with "eigenvalues"+"eigenvectors" (15 records reviewed, 2000–2021) returns only MIMO capacity and VQE papers (e.g. Florian Dupuy; Philippe Loubaton, "On the Capacity Achieving Covariance Matrix for Frequency Selective MIMO Channels…", arXiv:1007.0875v2) — none computes spectrum-versus-eigenvector-layer information for any random-matrix ensemble. So there is no published mechanism and no published number. Mathematically: in the constant-variance class x is a function of the eigenbasis layer only, which 099 proved exactly independent of the spectrum, so MI((tau,u);x) = I(u;x) = 0 there — the strict inequality lives only in the hetero class, where 097's exact tilted fibre law rho = beta·tau·(p1-p2) makes tau informative about x beyond u; the inequality itself is the mutual-information chain rule, strictness is a non-degeneracy check on tau|u, and the values are computable in closed form or one-dimensional quadrature from the 097/099 conditional laws. Honest outcome: exact numbers/bounds plus the mandatory sentence that MI never supplies the state-side weight and closes nothing. Cheap package; good sequencing right after 099.
