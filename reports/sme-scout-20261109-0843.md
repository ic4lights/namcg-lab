SUMMARY. The literature has a mature dynamical-friction programme for condensed-phase dark matter (superfluid DM, BEC/fuzzy DM) in which drag coefficients are computed from an EFFECTIVE medium description (phonons, Gross-Pitaevskii wavefunctions, Chandrasekhar-type linear response over a given halo), not microscopically from the underlying quantum theory of the medium. The one genuinely field-theoretic drag derivation I found (Berezhiani-Elder-Khoury 2019) works entirely in the superfluid phonon EFT and crucially relies on a medium-modified (gravity-tachyonic) sound cone to get any drag at all at T=0 subsonic speeds. Nothing in the searched corpus derives a drag/dissipation coefficient for rotation-curve phenomenology from a matrix model, fuzzy space, or noncommutative geometry — NAMCG's target is unoccupied, which is good for novelty and bad for external support. The standard physics constrains the target hard: at T=0 a Galilean- or Lorentz-invariant ground state gives Im chi^R(omega)/omega -> 0 for sub-critical motion (Landau 1941), so linear friction requires (i) a medium that picks a frame, (ii) an open low-frequency excitation channel, and (iii) for the d^-4 form a mechanism tying the response kernel to separation. For NAMCG, the sharpest honest moves are definitional: on bare Presentation-A data the very operator -dH/dr_i in the ch14/appH Kubo formulation is not defined, because separation d is emergent, not a parameter of the matrix Hamiltonian — exactly the 061/062 pattern. A bounded T=0 no-go (sub-critical reference state => Gamma_ij = 0) is also within reach and is publishable-grade obstruction, not weakness: it converts the working law from "unexamined" to "constrained", naming precisely what the reference state must contain (gapless channels or medium-modified dispersion, BEK-style).

VERIFIED ROUTES.

R1. Definitional obstruction route (recommended, rank 1; 061/062 precedent).
- What the literature says it needs: a friction computation requires a Hamiltonian with the separation as an external parameter and a force operator its derivative; this is standard in all verified dynamical-friction works (Berezhiani-Elder-Khoury, arXiv:1905.09297, JCAP 10 (2019) 074, DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2019/10/074; Berezhiani-Cintia-De Luca-Khoury, arXiv:2311.07672, JCAP 06 (2024) 024, DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2024/06/024; Hartman-Winther-Mota, arXiv:2011.00116, A&A 647 A70 (2021)). In every one, the medium (phonons, condensate wavefunction) is a PRE-GIVEN geometry/field on which the mover's potential is defined.
- What it establishes for NAMCG: in NAMCG the metric and separations are emergent (ch01/ch03; the conjugate-coordinate class in appH Problem F), so at fixed "r" there is no microscopic H(r) on bare matrix data; the appH L3038-3048 and ch14 L113-118 formulas are well-defined only after an identification of matrix degrees of freedom with spatially separated regions (dictionary, Problem A, or a fixed classical background with a distance notion). This is a bounded definitional obstruction of the same character as 061 P1 and 062 P3: the Kubo formulation of I is conditional on a coordinate/separation parameter that Presentation-A does not supply.
- What it does NOT establish: that the computation is impossible; exits are (i) compute on the conjugate-coordinate/Presentation-A background once a background with Theta-built distance is fixed, (ii) Presentation B (inherits ass:dictionary, cannot discharge the matrix-derived label — same exhaustion logic as 061 P1), (iii) an explicitly reduced model that states the separation is imported.
- Feasibility: high; it is a careful scoping derivation of exactly what H(r) and F_i are required to be, plus an exhaustive-exits argument. Sharpening, not breakthrough, but it protects the falsifiable-phenomenology claim.

R2. Bounded T=0 sub-critical no-go route (recommended, rank 2).
- Literature: Landau, Phys. Rev. 60, 356 (1941), DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.60.356 (verified via Crossref): the criterion that a superfluid at T=0 shows no dissipation below a critical velocity. BEK 2019 (verified above) proves the standard statement and its loophole: with the gravity-modified (tachyonic-sound) dispersion, subsonic Cherenkov friction becomes allowed but small; they also show first-order derivative treatments are usually insufficient. Martone-Shlyapnikov, arXiv:1805.12552, J. Exp. Theor. Phys. 127, 865 (2018): drag direction structure (longitudinal plus sideways components) in a supersolid — relevant to NAMCG's Gamma_L vs Gamma_T requirement (appH L3083-3095). Buehler-Desjacques, arXiv:2207.13740: for wave halos, circular-orbit wakes are infrared-divergent/transient rather than steady drag — a caution that a steady isotropic Gamma even exists only conditionally.
- NAMCG application: state a bounded class (reference state = stationary ground/quasi-free state of the specified microscopic dynamics; excitation spectrum gapped, or gapless linear with sub-critical relative velocity; reciprocal passive state) and show Im chi^R_(ij)(omega)/omega -> 0, i.e. Gamma_ij = 0 in that class, by the same spectral-support argument (delta-function support requires omega = v·q >= min gap/phonon threshold). Consequence: the -eta mu v/d^4 law, if it is to be derived, requires the NAMCG cores' reference state to carry an open low-frequency channel — e.g. the massless tensor/scalar sectors — or a medium-modified dispersion. This is the precise sub-lemma a researcher could attempt with sympy/numerics verification (a finite chain: two heavy sites + bath; compute Im chi at omega = v·q). Reproducible no-go, not failure: it names the missing ingredient.
- Conflict constraints: none of the papers is a no-go theorem in the formal sense; the loophole (BEK) must be stated. Do not present Gamma=0 as universal.
- Feasibility: high for the toy-class version; conditional sharpening.

Hence a NAMCG conditional benchmark (e.g. two localized impurities in a free Bogoliubov gas: derive Gamma_ij(r; l_A,l_B), its longitudinal/transverse split, the separation dependence, and show explicitly which ingredient produces powers like d^-4 vs constants vs 0) would be genuinely informative, and it is the only route that touches the actual missing-step content of appH L3139-3147 (finite-size factors ell_A, ell_B, tensor structure).
- What it does not establish: anything about NAMCG matrix dynamics. It must be labeled conditional/benchmark, not "the NAMCG response".
- Feasibility: moderate; a few pages of careful linear response.
