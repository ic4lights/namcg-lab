## 1. What changed since the 2026-10-25 scout

Two things matter for the gap:

1. 046 turns the F question into a regime question. On CP^{1,2} x K, the fibre levels contribute with power-law weights ((2J+1) tau_2/mu_J), so beta_X = W_X^(2)/W_X^(0) tends to 1 as n grows. The averaged-frame c_A = c_T therefore sits outside the controlled expansion. In the trace reading there is no single Einstein coefficient on the five traceless polarisations, except possibly at tuned K data. The remaining open items are the torsion expansion beyond first order, the cone at torsion order, the fibre-flux S^2-M first term (power counting only), and the normalisation mismatch J(J+2) vs Steinacker's m_s^2 = 3s.
2. 044/045 left the replica side (c_A - c_T) with uncomputed first-term Lambda^2 and log pieces, plus the mixed-term pieces 2 W_X, 4 W_X and -W_R/4 + 4 W_X, depending on the replica prescription.

The new literature supports a reframing of F that is consistent with NAMCG's emergence requirements. This is an observation, not a recommendation for a label change.

**Headline observation (MY REASONING, grounded in the abstracts and in 2411.02598 Sec. 3.3).** In Steinacker's published induced-gravity result, the one-loop term is a torsion-quadratic Weitzenboeck term H_grav. The abstracts and text I read say this term equals the Einstein-Hilbert term plus an axial-torsion quadratic piece plus a total derivative: R = -(1/2)H_grav - (1/2)T_mu T^mu + total derivative (eq. 3.31 in the text I read). The extra torsion piece is then interpreted as "mirage matter" and as extra non-Ricci-flat modes (arXiv:2601.08031, abstract; the "mirage matter" gloss on the 2601 abstract is from a third-party summary and was not checked against the paper).

This looks like the published counterpart of the 044 "axial-torsion mismatch" and the 046 non-Einstein coefficient. The published programme does not claim a single G on all polarisations, and it expects extra modes. If that reading is right, the F target "one Einstein coefficient with c_A = c_T on the polarisations" may be sharper than anything the IKKT literature has established, and the 046 no-go may already be the NAMCG version of a known structure. This needs checking against 2303.08012 and 2411.02598 in the body text. I did not do that comparison.

### R1. Problem F (and B): compare the 046 fibre trace with the Manta-Steinacker-Tran exact character formula

Verified:
- Manta-Steinacker-Tran, "hs-extended gravity from the IKKT matrix model", arXiv:2411.02598 (Nov 2024). A search result lists JHEP 02 (2025) 031; not independently verified.
  - Abstract: the one-loop action is written through an SO(1,9) character that evaluates the internal-mode traces explicitly. Higher-order contributions in the internal flux F_IJ are "generally suppressed and UV finite". Explicit expressions are given for the effective Newton constant G_N and for the KK scale dynamics.
  - Text read: 1/G_N is proportional to Tr_hs of H_grav, to leading order in F_IJ, which is K[2] = Tr_g F^2/(6 + J^2) (the text says "6 + J^2" in its extracted form; the symbol rendering is imperfect). Higher terms K[4] are separately suppressed. The "hs scale" J (Appendix C: positivity of hs masses on modified backgrounds) and footnote 14 ("the number of hs modes whose mass terms can be neglected in the loops") are how the hs tower enters. The geometric trace formula (3.34) gives K[2] explicitly, with G_N proportional to J^2 dK^{3/2} m_K^2 for |K| = 4.
  - Section 5 states that the mixed M-K and pure K higher-order terms (n >= 4) are UV finite and subleading by power counting.
- Steinacker, arXiv:2303.08012 (JHEP 05 (2023) 129): the detailed one-loop derivation. The 046 proposal read Sec. 7 (eq. 7.5, m_s^2 = 3s). I did not reread it.
- Steinacker, arXiv:2601.08031 (Jan 2026): the combined YM plus induced EH action leads to modified Einstein equations with a geometrical tensor interpreted as "mirage matter", with non-Ricci-flat modes and a non-standard dispersion.
- Fredenhagen-Steinacker, arXiv:2101.07297 (JHEP 05 (2021) 183) and Steinacker, arXiv:2002.02742: the frame arises from the Poisson structure on the S^2 bundle over spacetime; the Weitzenboeck torsion obeys YM-type equations; vacuum solutions are Ricci-flat up to an effective energy-momentum tensor quadratic in torsion.
- Sperling-Steinacker, arXiv:1806.05907 (fuzzy H^4_n as an S^2 bundle with SO(4,1)-equivariant structure; abstract level).

Precise sub-lemma for the researcher (hypotheses, expected form, NAMCG objects):
- *Question.* Is the 046 level weight sum_J (2J+1)/(M + mu_J) and its limit beta_X -> 1 (n -> infinity) the same object as the Steinacker trace Tr_g[F^2/(6 + J^2)] at the same K-scale and hs-scale data, including the role of the hs scale J (cutoff or mass)?
- *Hypotheses to match.* Product M x K (H1); IKKT content supplied (H2); one loop (H4); K and fibre levels from the Lie-Poisson operator (H10, H11).
- *Expected form of the result.* One of three outcomes:
  (a) agreement: the Steinacker "hs masses neglected" regime is exactly the beta_X -> 1 trace regime of 046, so 046's no-go reads "the induced action is EH plus the axial-torsion/mirage structure and no single G on the traceless polarisations", consistent with the published statement;
  (b) disagreement in the fibre-level normalisation (J(J+2) vs 3s), which fixes the open normalisation item and may change M*;
  (c) a regime where the published formula controls the higher-order flux terms better than 046's single-scale model H13.
- *NAMCG objects needed.* The 046 level spectrum mu_J, the Duhamel weights, tau^K and v^K - tau^K, the cone values (044), and the n -> infinity statement.
- *Feasibility.* Medium. Most of it is a dictionary between two published/accepted calculations. It needs the 2411.02598 trace formulas in full, which I read only in part.
- *Value.* Conditional sharpening or reconciliation. It would be a mild breakthrough in the reformulation sense if the dictionary shows that the target "single G_T" cannot be the published claim. It would NOT be a derivation of the F criterion.
- *Emergence.* Preserved: the frame and metric stay composite (E E-bar, G = Theta Theta^T). The published works are explicit that the effective metric is induced from the frame.

Constraint: NAMCG's polarisation-level criterion (five traceless polarisations with c_A = c_T) is stricter than anything published. Do not cite Steinacker as having derived "one coefficient on six polarisations". The abstracts say the graviton content is present and that extra modes exist; they say nothing about equal coefficients.

### R2. Problem F: torsion resummation and BV form factors (046 avenue 1)

Verified (abstract level):
- Hanisch-Pfaeffle-Stephan, "The Spectral Action for Dirac Operators with skew-symmetric Torsion", arXiv:0911.5074, Commun. Math. Phys. 300 (2010) 877: for totally antisymmetric torsion in four dimensions, torsion becomes dynamical and couples to the traceless part of the Riemann tensor. This is the closest published analogue of the 046 "non-Einstein coefficient from fibre-trace leakage". It is evidence that a Weyl-type coupling from torsion in the heat-kernel coefficient is a known structure, not an artefact.
- Pfaeffle-Stephan, arXiv:1203.5898: spectral action with arbitrary (also vectorial) torsion; Holst term and Barbero-Immirzi prediction. Relevant to the vector/axial split in the 044 class.
- Agricola-Friedrich, math/0305233 (Casimir operator with skew torsion, from a search result): not fetched; not independently verified.
- Barvinsky-Gusev-Vilkovisky-Zhytnikov, gr-qc/9404063 (J. Math. Phys. 35 (1994) 3543): heat-kernel trace to third order in curvature in a basis of nonlocal invariants with form factors, including the "commutator curvature" (flux) and the potential. Companion: BGVZ 0911.1168; Gusev, arXiv:0811.1063 (Nucl. Phys. B807, 566): second-order form factors for general second-order operators; Codello-Zanusso, arXiv:1203.2034 (J. Math. Phys. 54, 013513): the same via a diagrammatic derivation.
- No source I found gives an exact resummation of the heat kernel for constant skew torsion on a homogeneous space; the search tool said so too. I found nothing on torsion inside the BV nonlocal framework.

What this gives (MY REASONING for the mapping):
- The 046 Duhamel weights m_1(x) = int a(1-a) e^{-xa}, m_2(x) = int (1-2a)^2 e^{-xa} are the standard BV second-order form factors with a mass gap Delta. Cross-check them against the Gusev/Codello-Zanusso form-factor functions. This is cheap, independent, and removes a verification burden. It is a consistency check, not new physics.
- The flux operator Z in the NAMCG model H = -box + kappa Sigma Z is a commutator curvature and a potential in the BV language. The BV/Codello-Zanusso framework should give the second-order (in Z) result including the first-term P_2 with correct nonlocal dependence on q, beyond the frozen-symbol approximation. It does NOT resum to all orders in torsion.
- The exact resummation on CP^{1,2} = SO(4,1)/(SO(3,1)xU(1))-type homogeneous space (the 046 background torsion coth tau) is a group-theory problem (Plancherel decomposition of tr e^{-sH}) rather than a perturbative one. That is the avenue 1 relaxation in the 046 fork table. I found no published solution.

Sub-lemma: (i) verify the m_1, m_2 weights against the published form factors, including the mass-gap generalisation; (ii) state which of the 046 uncomputed pieces (order-(2,2) forms, first-term torsion piece) are the BV second-order terms, and which need the third-order BGVZ terms.
Feasibility: high for (i); medium for (ii). Value: conditional sharpening and a verification of the interpolation. Emergence preserved (no fundamental metric: the operators are built from the frame).

### R3. Problem B/A: the replica/cone coefficient c_A with maximal SUSY and the same regulator

Verified:
- Dvali-Solodukhin, "Black Hole Entropy and Gravity Cutoff", arXiv:0806.3976: with N species the universal gravity cutoff is Lambda = M_Planck/sqrt(N); the two entropies (Bekenstein-Hawking and entanglement) are shown equal in explicit examples (KK towers) in which the relation between M_Planck and Lambda is known from the fundamental theory (abstract level).
- Nesterov-Solodukhin, arXiv:1007.1246 (Nucl. Phys. B842:141, 2011): the induced gravitational couplings and the entanglement entropy are both sensitive to how the theory is modified in the UV; the entropy is S = A/(4G(epsilon)) with the induced G(epsilon). The message is: compute G_T and G_A with the same UV modification. (Abstract level.)
- Solodukhin, Living Rev. Relativ. 14 (2011) 8, arXiv:1104.3712 (review).
- Susskind-Uglum (hep-th/9401070), Kabat (hep-th/9503016): the Kabat contact term is proposed to account for non-renormalisation of Newton's constant in some SUSY theories (abstract-level facts from a search snippet; Kabat verified in the earlier scout, Susskind-Uglum verified in the earlier scout).
- Mertens-Verschelde-Zakharov, "String Theory in Polar Coordinates and the Vanishing of the One-Loop Rindler Entropy", arXiv:1606.06632, JHEP 1608 (2016) 113: for type II superstrings the correct procedures give a vanishing one-loop Rindler entropy, S = 0, and the QFT UV divergences of the spectrum disappear in the Euclidean free energy (abstract level).
- Donnelly-Wall 1506.05792 (PRD 94, 104053): Maxwell via KK reduction onto a 2D base; the contact term is one negative scalar degree of freedom on the entangling surface. David-Mukherjee, arXiv:2005.08402 (hyperbolic cylinder and the KK tower of massive spin-2 for graviton EE) and 2201.06043 (graviton edge-mode EE): edge contributions are not captured by the hyperbolic-cylinder partition function alone (abstract level).

Mapping to NAMCG (MY REASONING):
- NAMCG's 044/045 chain has c_A as a heat-kernel cone coefficient with Lambda^2 and log first-term pieces uncomputed. The IKKT content (6,2) is finite and supersymmetric; the G_T side has vanishing Lambda^2 and log terms at (6,2). The literature indicates that the same SUSY content should constrain the cone side (Susskind-Uglum/Kabat suggest non-renormalisation involves the contact term; Mertens et al. indicate the full SUSY Rindler entropy can vanish in the string case). The sub-lemma is to evaluate the cone Lambda^2 and log first-term parts for the (6,2) content with the same Duhamel/heat-kernel machinery used for G_T, so that G_A and G_T are computed with the identical regulator, the Nesterov-Solodukhin and Dvali-Solodukhin lesson.
- Expected form: either the Lambda^2/log pieces cancel for (6,2) (then only finite-part replica pieces 2W_X, 4W_X, -W_R/4 + 4W_X remain and the prescription dependence is isolated), or a residue remains that is a NAMCG-specific replica term.
- Constraint: Mertens et al. concerns the string spectrum; it does not prove vanishing for the IKKT field content with a fixed noncommutative brane. Do not cite it as a result for NAMCG. Susskind-Uglum is a proposal, not a theorem.
- Feasibility: medium. Value: this is the gap that tells F and B whether c_A - c_T can be zero by SUSY content. A cancellation would be a positive structural result for G_A = G_T. A conditional sharpening, or a bounded no-go if a residue remains.

## 5. Concerns for the researcher

- Do not present 046 as contradicting Steinacker. The published programme does not claim single-G on the polarisations. The comparison in R1 may show the 046 no-go reproduces a known feature (extra torsion modes) rather than a new obstruction. Conversely, it may show a new, sharper statement. The claim must be checked before wording.
- The J(J+2) vs 3s mismatch is an unresolved normalisation issue that could matter for M*. 046 says the findings do not depend on it; R1 can test that claim.
- Do not use Mertens et al. or Susskind-Uglum as support for G_A = G_T in NAMCG without the (6,2) cone computation; they concern other regulators and other field content.
- Presentation: "no open range gives a single coefficient" is a bounded statement under H1-H13 and inert amplitudes zero; the literature does not alter that scope.
- Emergence check: all routes keep the metric composite. The SO(1,9) character route uses the Weitzenboeck frame built from Poisson brackets, not a fundamental metric. Watch that any use of the "Ricci scalar of the effective metric" identification does not smuggle in an independent metric (the effective metric is G = Theta Theta^T-type, as in the published work).
