### Chain 2: D1.torsion-T-nonzero

**F3. PLAN-HAZARD, major (my judgement; `conjecture` per rule 6). A non-derivative term at T≠0 is not by itself a mass or a removal of torsion modes.**
- appH:1464: "a commutant label therefore acquires no term without a derivative of f in its linearised equation, background torsion included. Such a term can come only from a mode that stops being commutant."
- appH:1402: local rotations with six arbitrary functions are implemented by "a unitary operator, so (e, ω) related by a local rotation are one gauge orbit". This holds on every background of the class.
- Break: the six-parameter gauge directions stay in the kernel at T≠0 by construction. A non-derivative coefficient on a non-commutant Lorentz mode can then be the inhomogeneous gauge transformation of a fluctuation about a non-invariant background. It does not by itself lift a mode.
- Smallest test: on the null-rotation background, report the rank of the linearised operator on the non-commutant Lorentz modes modulo the gauge directions `[½λ^{cd}S_cd, X_a]` with base-dependent λ, not the non-derivative coefficient alone.

**F5. NO-GO-APPLIES, bounded, conditional. Published ghost and constraint results for Poincaré gauge theory bind any covariant, local reading of a T≠0 norm.**
