## 0. Headline

1. The literature supports the diagnosis of the assessment (the B residue is an edge/improvement term) and gives published mechanisms for fixing this kind of term. Two of them (candidates 3 and 4, the modular-Hamiltonian boundary term) look more promising than candidate 1 taken alone.
2. MY REASONING on candidate 1: published gauge-covariant Noether-Wald constructions (momentum maps, Prabhu bundle formalism) add a "momentum map times flux" term that acts as a potential (Phi_H-type work term). In the check recorded in the assessment, eps and d(eps) vanish on Sigma, which predicts that the gauge-covariant correction vanishes on Sigma and the 042 indeterminacy persists. Candidate 1 is still worth one cheap package: a negative result is a sharp reproducible statement and it tests the D1 relation. I would not expect it to fix r.
3. The kinematic lead of candidate 1 (rigid eta-rotation = Hamiltonian flow of eps) is, as far as I can tell, the standard "global symmetry realised as inner automorphism" property of symmetric matrix-model backgrounds (fuzzy sphere, covariant quantum spaces of Steinacker). It gives isotropy (global stabiliser) rotations, not local Lorentz symmetry. Calling it a D1 seed needs an explicit argument that it yields local frame rotations. I did not verify this against full text.
4. Candidate 2: the published induced Einstein-Hilbert term in the IKKT literature (Steinacker 2021-2023) is obtained on M^{3,1} x K with compact fuzzy K and maximal SUSY, with G set by the KK scale of K. That is the "KK or mass-type scale" entry point NAMCG already cites, not an embedded brane with only a second fundamental form. I found no paper computing an area (replica) coefficient G_A on any matrix-model brane.
5. The ranking changes modestly; see section 3.

## 1. Routes, by assessment candidate

### R1. Gauge realisation of the compensating rotation; gauge-covariant Noether-Wald charge (cand. 1; targets B, D1)

Verified (abstracts read):
- Prabhu, "The first law of black hole mechanics for fields with internal gauge freedom", arXiv:1511.00388, CQG 34, 035011 (2017). Principal-bundle formulation for tetrad gravity, Einstein-Yang-Mills, Einstein-Dirac; horizon potentials and charges; Wald-like entropy.
- Jacobson-Mohd, arXiv:1507.01054, PRD 92, 124010 (2015). Entropy = Noether charge of combined diffeomorphism + local Lorentz symmetry, since diffeos alone fail at the bifurcation surface for frames. Closest published template for "stationary up to a compensating internal transformation".
- Elgood-Meessen-Ortin, "The first law of black hole mechanics in the Einstein-Maxwell theory revisited", arXiv:2006.02792 (2020): momentum maps act as generalised thermodynamic potentials. (The journal reference JHEP 09 (2020) 026 comes from a search result, not from the arXiv record.)
- Elgood-Ortin-Pereniguez, arXiv:2012.14892, JHEP 05 (2021) 110: manifestly gauge- and Lorentz-invariant Wald entropy for heterotic gravity with Chern-Simons terms, via momentum maps and restricted generalised zeroth laws. Companion: Elgood-Mitsios-Ortin-Pereniguez, arXiv:2012.13323, JHEP 07 (2021) 007 (the formalism selects one of several definitions of the conserved charges).
- Ortin, "A note on Lie-Lorentz derivatives", hep-th/0206159, CQG 19 (2002) L143.
- Tachikawa, hep-th/0611141, CQG 24:737 (2007): Noether charge repaired for gravitational Chern-Simons terms.
- Wall-Yan, "Linearised second law for higher curvature gravity and non-minimally coupled vector fields", arXiv:2402.05411, PRD 110, 084005 (2024): entropy invariant under JKM ambiguities; explicit formula for f(Riemann) with vectors. Bears directly on improvements of type f.Riem(gamma).
- Visser-Yan, arXiv:2403.07140 (2024), and Hollands-Wald-Zhang, arXiv:2402.00818 (2024): improved Noether charges unambiguous to first order in a perturbation.
- Speranza, arXiv:1706.05061 (JHEP 02 (2018) 021) and arXiv:2202.00133 (JHEP 07 (2022) 029); Donnelly-Freidel, arXiv:1601.04744 (JHEP 09 (2016) 102): edge-mode extended phase spaces fix surface charge ambiguities with extra surface fields. Geiller papers were named in the task; I did not fetch any; not verified.

What it establishes: in published cases the compensating gauge or Lorentz parameter enters the charge through its momentum map, and the JKM improvement ambiguity is removed either by (a) requiring the charge to be built from the gauge-covariant (Lie-Lorentz) derivative, which annihilates fields that are Lie-dragged up to gauge, or (b) edge-mode extension.

Relevance to 042: the obstruction is that Y(phi, L_xi X) with L_xi X = BX does not vanish. Mechanism (a) would kill it if Y is required to depend on the fields only through the gauge-covariant derivative. MY REASONING, to be checked: (i) that restriction is a choice of admissible Y; the Iyer-Wald identity does not force it; it must be justified from gauge invariance of the matrix action. (ii) In the Poisson limit the gauge group acts on (gamma, omega) as symplectic diffeomorphisms of the chart, so the "gauge" statement may collapse to ordinary Lie dragging along the Hamiltonian field of eps: xi is itself that Hamiltonian field, so L_xi X = {eps, X} may hold by construction. Check this before the result is called new. (iii) eps and d(eps) vanish on Sigma, so the momentum-map term vanishes there.

Constraint: Einstein-aether (Foster, gr-qc/0509121, verified in the previous scout) is the warning for frames not Lie-dragged at the bifurcation surface; Jacobson-Mohd is the published remedy. No published result concerns a compensating transformation that is an inner automorphism of a matrix algebra.

Suggested question: (a) show whether eps is a momentum map in the Jacobson-Mohd / Elgood-Ortin sense and evaluate it on Sigma for all stationary backgrounds in both signatures; (b) if it vanishes on Sigma, record that the gauge-covariant charge equals the 042 charge there and Y survives (reproducible no-go); (c) only otherwise test whether the Wall-Yan JKM-invariant prescription selects r.
Feasibility: high for (a),(b); low-medium for r. Value: conditional sharpening or no-go; breakthrough only if r is fixed from matrix gauge data (unlikely).

### R2. Rank-6 class, induced Einstein form and area coefficient (cand. 2; targets F, B)

Verified (abstracts read):
- Steinacker, "Gravity as a quantum effect on quantum space-time", arXiv:2110.03936, PLB 827 (2022) 136946: EH at one loop on noncommutative branes in IIB/IKKT; compact fuzzy extra dimensions K and maximal SUSY essential; G set by the KK scale of K; for covariant quantum spacetimes the perturbative physics includes all gravity degrees of freedom and a higher-spin tower.
- Steinacker, arXiv:2303.08012, JHEP 05 (2023) 129: detailed one-loop derivation, brane M^{3,1} x K in R^{9,1}; vacuum energy does not act as a cosmological constant.
- Steinacker, arXiv:2601.08031 (Jan 2026): modified gravity, extra non-Ricci-flat modes, "mirage matter"; no EH coefficient in the abstract.
- Sperling-Steinacker, arXiv:1901.03522, JHEP 07 (2019) 010: tower of higher-spin modes with a massless graviton and a scalar; analogue of linearised EH.
- Steinacker review arXiv:1003.4134, CQG 27 (2010) 133001; Grosse-Steinacker-Wohlgenannt, arXiv:0802.0973, JHEP 04 (2008) 023 (UV/IR mixing as induced EH).
- Area versus Newton coefficient: Jacobson gr-qc/9404039; Frolov-Fursaev hep-th/9703178; Susskind-Uglum hep-th/9401070; Casini-Mazzitelli-Teste 1412.6522 (previous scout); Jacobson, "Entanglement equilibrium and the Einstein equation", arXiv:1505.04753, PRL 116, 201101 (2016); Casini-Galante-Myers, arXiv:1601.00528, JHEP 03 (2016) 194; Speranza, arXiv:1602.01380, JHEP 04 (2016) 105; Bueno-Min-Speranza-Visser, arXiv:1612.04374, PRD 95, 046003 (2017).

What it establishes: (i) induced EH from IKKT is a one-loop result tied to the KK scale of fuzzy extra dimensions with maximal SUSY, consistent with the vanishing of the Lambda^2 and log terms at (6,2). (ii) For covariant quantum spacetimes the abstracts claim the full graviton content; they do not show a computed six-polarisation, single-coefficient statement. (iii) For non-conformal matter the identification of area and Newton coefficients needs an extra assumption on entanglement variations, with reported counter-indications (Casini-Galante-Myers; Speranza): the published form of the NAMCG G_T vs G_A tension. (iv) No paper found computes a replica/area coefficient on a matrix-model brane.

Constraint: the "embedded brane, D >= 7, non-zero second fundamental form" class of the assessment is not the class in which the published induced EH arises. Formulate candidate 2 with the KK/mass-type scale as expansion parameter, in a class with the (6,2) content. I did not verify that the NAMCG CP^{1,2} leaf is the S^2-bundle structure of the covariant quantum spacetime of Steinacker; check before importing results.

Suggested question: does the one-loop EH term of Steinacker, as a heat-kernel/spectral quantity of the fuzzy-K operators, give one G on all six polarisations in the Poisson limit, and does the same K-dependent term set the replica area coefficient? (This is 026 avenue 1, the mixed quartic a_3-type coefficient.)
Feasibility: medium-low. Value: the only route that can join B and F in a full-GR class; a polarisation-dependent G_T would be a genuine bounded no-go. Breakthrough potential if both numbers are obtained in one class.

### R3. Edge terms in the entanglement first law (cand. 3; target B)

Verified (abstracts read):
- Herzog-Nishioka, "The edge of entanglement: getting the boundary right for non-minimally coupled scalar fields", arXiv:1610.02261, JHEP 12 (2016) 138: the modular Hamiltonian of a curvature-coupled scalar needs a specific boundary term.
- Chen, "The generalized first law for more general matter", arXiv:2202.00680, JHEP 04 (2022) 076: modular Hamiltonian = integrated bulk Noether current + boundary term; the generalised first law then includes the Wald-Dong terms from non-minimal coupling.
- Bueno-Min-Speranza-Visser, 1612.04374: non-minimal coupling gives surface-localised Wald-like entropy and modular-Hamiltonian corrections.
- Donnelly-Wall, arXiv:1206.5831 (PRD 86, 064042) and arXiv:1412.1895 (PRL 114, 111603): Kabat contact term as gauge-dependent ambiguity / edge-mode entropy. Solodukhin 1502.03758, Blommaert-Colin-Ellerin 2405.12276 (previous scout).
- Faulkner et al. (1312.7856) and Casini-Huerta-Myers were named in the task; I did not fetch them; not independently verified.

What it establishes: this is the closest published precedent for the 042 reformulation (G_A = G_T iff the edge term supplies -4 kappa_X). For non-minimal couplings the discrepancy between the bulk expectation and the entanglement entropy is carried by a boundary term of the modular Hamiltonian equal to the Wald-Dong term. The NAMCG improvement r kappa_X sqrt(G) R_W is structurally the xi R phi^2 analogue.
Suggested question: in the 015 mode set, find the boundary term required for the first law to hold with Noether current T_eta + improvement, keeping r free; report the r for which no extra edge term is needed and compare with -4 kappa_X (Chen-type consistency).
Feasibility: high, but 2 polarisations in the rank-3 class only. Value: conditional sharpening; a positive result would make r a prediction. I rank it above R1 alone because the published mechanism is directly the same kind of gap.

### R4. Modular Hamiltonians and edge entropy of one-loop / matrix states (cand. 4; targets A, B, G)

Verified (abstracts read):
- Frenkel-Hartnoll, "Emergent area laws from entangled matrices", arXiv:2301.01325, JHEP 05 (2023) 084: matrix wavefunctions near a fuzzy sphere; the SU(N) Gauss law ("Gauss law entanglement") gives an entropy proportional to the bounding area with coefficient set by an inverse emergent Maxwell coupling.
- Fliss-Frenkel-Hartnoll-Soni, "Minimal areas from entangled matrices", arXiv:2408.05274, SciPost Phys. 18, 171 (2025): relational subsystem definition in matrix quantum mechanics; RT-like minimisation in states with semiclassical emergent geometry.
- Karczmarek-Sabella-Garnier, arXiv:1310.8345, JHEP 03 (2014) 129: entropy on the fuzzy sphere not proportional to boundary length.
- No paper found on the modular Hamiltonian or Bisognano-Wichmann structure of a matrix-model state.

Relevance: Frenkel-Hartnoll is the one published result where the matrix gauge group itself (not a field-theory heat kernel) generates an area-proportional entropy, with coefficient fixed by an emergent Maxwell coupling. The Poisson-limit a^a sector of NAMCG is Maxwell-type (041), and the Donnelly-Wall edge modes already used in 036 are the same type of object. This is evidence that the edge modes of the symplectic gauge sector (ledger 042 avenue 5) carry a classical area term invisible to the one-loop heat kernel; it is a candidate for part of the G_A - G_T difference, to be tested and not assumed.
Suggested question: in the Poisson-limit connection sector with emergent Maxwell coupling from the 041 operator, compute the Gauss-law/edge-mode area coefficient and compare it with the Donnelly-Wall edge density of the 036 bookkeeping. Is it the same object (no new term) or additional (shifts c_A)?
Feasibility: low-medium. Value: conditional sharpening; the same ingredient seeds G-derived.

## 3. Ranking, and whether the literature changes the assessment ranking

1. R3 + R1 as one package: modular-Hamiltonian edge-term consistency (Herzog-Nishioka/Chen logic) together with the cheap gauge-covariance check (momentum map of eps on Sigma). Both ask what fixes Y or r by a published mechanism, and share the edge-term algebra. Feasibility high; breakthrough only if r is fixed from matrix data; otherwise conditional sharpening or a no-go. Class-specific (rank 3).
2. R2: rank-6 class transfer reformulated through the fuzzy-KK route. Feasibility medium-low; the only route that can be a breakthrough for F and joins B.
3. R4: Gauss-law/edge-mode entropy and modular edge term of the one-loop state, first for the free connection sector. Feasibility low-medium; breakthrough potential for A and G.
4. R5: signature probe with the numerical IKKT benchmark (medium, conditional).
5. R6: Wall-Yan hypothesis check (cheap).

Does the literature change the assessment ranking? Partly. (a) It does not contradict the strategic point that the 4-coordinate class cannot carry full GR; the published mechanism (fuzzy K, KK scale) is consistent with the need for a different class. (b) It lowers my confidence that candidate 1 alone fixes r, because published gauge-covariant charges add a momentum-map term that vanishes on Sigma for the eps of the assessment. (c) It raises candidate 3 relative to candidate 1: a published, directly analogous mechanism exists. (d) It sharpens candidate 2: use the fuzzy-K/KK scale, not only a second fundamental form. (e) Candidate 4 gains a concrete published sub-object (Gauss-law entropy). The recommended order of the assessment (1, 3, 2+5, 4) is acceptable if 1 and 3 are done together; I would not fund 1 as a stand-alone multi-round effort.

## 4. Concerns and constraints for the researcher

- Classification: nothing here closes any critical-path problem. A fixed r or a G_A = G_T match in the rank-3 class is class-specific and conditional.
- Do not present {eps, X^a} = xi^a as new: global symmetry realised as inner automorphism is a defining feature of symmetric matrix-model backgrounds. Do not describe the rigid rotation as local Lorentz symmetry.
- Emergence: all routes keep the metric composite (gamma = X*eta, G = Theta Theta^T). The matrix gauge group belongs to the model; momentum-map and edge-mode constructions use a surface Sigma defined by the flow, not a fundamental metric. Risk low. Watch that an admissible-improvement argument does not smuggle in a background metric (for instance Riem(gamma) with gamma treated as independent).
- Published warnings: Einstein-aether (Foster); Jacobson-Satz on identifying the remainder of the entropy with entanglement; Casini-Galante-Myers and Speranza on the area/Newton link for non-conformal matter.
- Source quality: the momentum-map papers were verified at abstract level only; the statement that a covariant-derivative prescription removes Y-type terms is the published design principle, not a verified theorem for the NAMCG class.
