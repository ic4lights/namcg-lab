\emph{Transport of the profile coordinate along the pushforward family and its moment coordinates.} Within the named coupled gaussian slot law of the seventh preceding paragraph, at a general profile \(g=(g_{1},\dots,g_{D})\) of strictly positive entries and total weight \(W\), with no constancy or uniformity restriction, the profile coordinate of the pushforward weight of the third preceding paragraph admits two exact transports. First, the group of positive profiles modulo common scaling acts on the interior of the profile simplex by \(\tau_{g}(p)_{i}=g_{i}p_{i}/\sum_{j}g_{j}p_{j}\): composing gives \(\tau_{g}\circ\tau_{h}=\tau_{gh}\) with inverse \(\tau_{1/g}\), the action is free, since \(\tau_{g}\) fixes an interior point only when \(g\) is constant, and its Jacobian on the simplex is \(\prod_{i}g_{i}/(\sum_{j}g_{j}p_{j})^{D}\); consequently the member of the family at profile \(g\) is exactly the pushforward of its flat member under \(\tau_{g}\), the closed density of the third preceding paragraph being the flat density \((D-1)!\) times the Jacobian of \(\tau_{1/g}\) at the image point, \((D-1)!\big/\bigl(G(\sum_{i}p_{i}/g_{i})^{D}\bigr)\) with \(G=\prod_{i}g_{i}\), so the whole family is a single transport orbit of the flat member, which is also the uniform-profile and Haar-frame member of that paragraph, and the profile is recovered from any member as the unique group element carrying the flat member to it: this is the identifiability of the third preceding paragraph in transport form, a reframing of the label data as a coordinate of the family, and it produces no profile from the core. Second, write \(m_{2p}=2\,\mathbb{E}\,\ell^{2p}\) for the averaged charge moments of the tenth preceding paragraph at its leading-order convention; then \(m_{2p}=2\,(4s^{2}W)^{p}\,\rho_{p}\), with \(s^{2}\) the slot variance parameter, and \(\rho_{p}=p!\,h_{p}(g)/W^{p}\) in terms of the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomials \(h_{p}\), so that the combinations \(\rho_{p}=2^{p-1}m_{2p}/m_{2}^{p}\) are free of \(s^{2}\) and recover exactly the obstruction factors of that paragraph, and the profile is carried by them: the power sums \(s_{k}(g)/W^{k}\) follow from the \(\hat h_{k}=\rho_{k}/k!\) by the inverted Girard recursion \(\sigma_{k}=k\hat h_{k}-\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}\sigma_{i}\hat h_{k-i}\) with \(\sigma_{1}=1\), the elementary symmetric functions of \(g/W\) follow by Newton's identities, and \(g/W\) is the ordered root set of the resulting monic polynomial of degree \(D\); the power-sum map from the profile to \((s_{1}(g),\dots,s_{D}(g))\) has the exact Jacobian \(\prod_{k=1}^{D}k\cdot\prod_{i<j}(g_{i}-g_{j})\), nonzero exactly off the coincidence walls \(g_{i}=g_{j}\), and \((\rho_{2},\dots,\rho_{D})\) is a triangular reparametrisation of \((s_{2}(g)/W^{2},\dots,s_{D}(g)/W^{D})\) with diagonal entries \((k-1)!/W^{k}\), so it is itself a coordinate on the ordered chamber \(g_{1}>\dots>g_{D}>0\), singular exactly where profiles collide: at a general rank-one profile the ordered profile, up to relabelling and up to the scale fixed by \(W\), is carried by the averaged charge-moment sequence rather than supplied to it, while the scale datum \(s^{2}W\) is not supplied by the sequence, exactly as in the preceding paragraphs. At \(D=2\) the transport is already visible in the first moment of the pushforward measure: with rates \(\lambda_{i}=1/g_{i}\) and \(L=\lambda_{1}/\lambda_{2}\), \(\mathbb{E}_{\mu_{g}}(p_{1})=L(\log L+1/L-1)/(L-1)^{2}\), strictly decreasing from \(1/2\) at \(L=1\) to \(0\) as \(L\to\infty\), so the mean of the pushforward measure is a faithful chamber coordinate there; the analogous injectivity statement for mean or symmetric-moment maps of the measure at \(D\ge3\) was not obtained. The slot coordinate is the normalised diagonal of the Gram matrix of the slot vector \(w\), whose spectrum is a single atom at the vertex of the simplex, so within the rank-one class the only nondegenerate Gram-type coordinate on configurations is this diagonal one. These statements are exact coordinate and transport statements within the named coupled gaussian slot law at strictly positive profile, with the moment statement at the leading-order convention of the tenth preceding paragraph; they evaluate no matrix integral of the core action, they produce no state and no selection mechanism, they do not fix the state that sets \(W\), \(g\), \(s\) or the frame, they leave the missing steps (i)-(iv) of the Missing step paragraph unchanged, and they close nothing in Problem~G.
