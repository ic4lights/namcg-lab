## Question 1 — is the pointwise dichotomy already in the literature? Is it "routine order-2 formality-obstruction"?

**Direct answer: the dichotomy as literally stated (fixed 1-jet, order ≤2 per slot, exact 4-dimensional span computation) is not, as far as I could find, recorded verbatim as a theorem anywhere; but every ingredient of it is textbook-standard, and a deformation-quantization referee will read it as a correctly done, routine order-2 instance of standard obstruction theory — not as a new theorem.**

What is standard, and what I verified it against:

- **Necessity side (associativity forces [Θ,Θ]=0), stated in Kontsevich's own paper.** I extracted the full text of arXiv q-alg/9709040 and read §1.1–1.2 directly. It states: a star product is `fg + ħB₁ + ħ²B₂ + ...` with Bᵢ bidifferential operators (§1.1); associativity forces B₁ to be a Hochschild 2-cocycle (the displayed equation is exactly the package's δ=0 formula); the antisymmetric part is given by a bivector π via B₁⁻(f,g)=⟨π,df∧dg⟩; and "The second term O(ħ²) in the associativity equation ... implies ... [π,π] = 0 ∈ 𝒳³(X), where the bracket is the Schouten–Nijenhuis bracket", followed by "gauge equivalence classes of star-products modulo O(ħ²) are classified by Poisson structures on X". This is the package's "only if" and the κ-relation in conceptual form, at the level of smooth fields rather than a fixed 1-jet.
- **Sufficiency side (closed ⇒ a B₂ with A₂=δB₂ exists):** this is Kontsevich's construction — the formality quasi-isomorphism produces U₂ with A₂=δU₂ when [π,π]=0; the abstract's theorem is exactly the existence statement (verified on the abs page and §1.2's closing lines).
- **"Obstruction functionals span exactly the Schouten 3-forms":** this is the standard identification of the Hochschild cohomology of C^∞(M) with polyvector fields carrying the Schouten bracket (HH³ ↔ 𝒳³, Gerstenhaber/HKR-type). I verified a modern expository statement of this identification in Esposito, "Lectures on Deformation quantization of Poisson manifolds", arXiv:1207.3287 (eq. (49): the bracket induced by the Gerstenhaber bracket becomes the Schouten bracket), whose PDF I grepped. Kontsevich §3.4/§4.6 contains the same complex-theoretic identification.
- **δ(A₂)≡0 (the package's C4)** is the automatic Hochschild-cocycle property of a truncation associator; routine.

What is *not* in the literature as far as I could find (and I searched: INSPIRE, Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv UI/API as available): a theorem stated at a **fixed 1-jet** with the primitive **B₂ constrained to order ≤2 per slot with pointwise-free coefficients**, or the explicit **linear-algebraic span computation** (witness space = Schouten 3-forms, dim 4 in d=4). Those are the package's own finite-model verifications of the standard fact. The hep-th nonconstant-θ associativity literature (Aschieri–Blochte-type, Meier–van Tongeren — records verified in my 2026-11-20 scout pass) uses the same obstruction in words, not as a pointwise dichotomy.

**Presentation consequence (advice only):** the paragraph is safe and correctly classified, but a one-line attribution that this is the order-Θ² instance of the Kontsevich/BFFLS obstruction would pre-empt a referee reading it as a novelty claim, and is consistent with the manuscript's own 058 dichotomy sentence (which my scout already classified as "published theorem class"). That the result is standard is not a defect — it is why the paragraph functions as reliable model bookkeeping.

## Question 2 — is "the natural-universal question remains open" safe? Reference verification

**Direct answer: No — the scoped snippet is still not safe without an acknowledgement sentence. Kontsevich formality settles the general question positively: for every smooth Poisson bivector there is a natural, universal, jet-dependent star product, so its U₂ is a jet-universal B₂ with A₂=δB₂. "Remains open" is legitimate only for (a) the existence inside the five tested constant-coefficient structures (the C6 scope), (b) selection of any particular B₂ by the matrix theory, and (c) non-formal (norm/convergence) versions. The manuscript needs one sentence recording the formality result as the settled general analogue; the corrected snippet's "beyond this class ... remains open" will be read by any DQ-aware referee as claiming the general problem is open, which it is not.** The package itself already knows this (findings §5: "formality shows universal formulas exist for smooth Poisson Θ") — the risk is purely at the manuscript-wording level. Note also that C6 is in no tension with Kontsevich: formality's U₂ has coefficients depending on jets of Θ with contraction types outside the five-structure span, which is precisely the class the C6 no-go excludes.
