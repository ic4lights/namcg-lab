## Findings
1. **Severity: MAJOR. Location:** `proposals/105-a-map-order2-associator-correction/insert-r1.tex`, the sentence "In the five-structure natural ansatz built from contractions of the products ΘΘ and Θ∂Θ of order ≤2 per slot, no jet-universal B₂ solves A₂=δB₂ at closed jets, so the natural-universal question remains open." **Finding:** the descriptive clause "built from contractions of the products ΘΘ and Θ∂Θ" denotes a strictly larger span than what was tested. The tested class is the five explicitly enumerated structures {P1, M, div1, U1, U2} in `s1b_order2_coboundary.py` (`col_struct`) with **fixed, jet-independent constant coefficients**; my independent construction confirms e.g. the factorized pairing Θ^{p₁p₂}Θ^{q₁q₂}(∂∂f)(∂∂g) is a ΘΘ-contraction of order ≤2 per slot that is **not** in the tested span. A reader will therefore take the no-go as covering a class wider than proved. Additionally, "the natural-universal question remains open" is defensible only when scoped to the model/class analysed; read as the general mathematical question (smooth Poisson bivectors, arbitrary jet dependence) Kontsevich formality is the obvious related result and the bare "remains open" could mislead. **Reason:** the package's own text (proposal Result and Special instructions: "C6 is bounded to the five enumerated structures with universal jet-independent coefficients; the .out shows rank 3 vs 4") states the correct scope; only the insert sentence is loose. **Required action:** replace that one sentence with the corrected snippet below (or equivalent wording that (a) ties the no-go to the explicitly enumerated five structures with fixed jet-independent coefficients, (b) does not describe the span as all ΘΘ/Θ∂Θ contractions, (c) scopes "remains open" to beyond the tested class).
