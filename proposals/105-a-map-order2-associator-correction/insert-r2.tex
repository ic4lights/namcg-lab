\paragraph{Order-\(\Theta^2\) coboundary dichotomy.} For the naive truncation \(f\star_1 g=fg+\tfrac{i}{2}\Theta^{ab}(x)\,\partial_af\,\partial_bg\), the order-\(\Theta^2\) associator \(A_2(f,g,h)=B_1(B_1(f,g),h)-B_1(f,B_1(g,h))\) depends on \(\Theta\) only through its 1-jet at the origin, vanishes when any argument is constant, satisfies \(\delta A_2\equiv0\), and its slot-antisymmetric part equals exactly \([\Theta,\Theta]^{abc}\,\partial_af\,\partial_bg\,\partial_ch\). At a fixed 1-jet, the equation \(A_2=\delta B_2\) admits a bidifferential \(B_2\) of order \(\le2\) per slot with arbitrary pointwise coefficients if and only if \([\Theta,\Theta](0)=0\): for generic 1-jets no such \(B_2\) exists and the obstruction functionals span exactly the Schouten 3-forms, while along the closure family the system is solvable at every closed jet. For constant \(\Theta\), the correction \(B_2(f,g)=-\tfrac{1}{8}\Theta^{ab}\Theta^{cd}\,\partial_a\partial_c f\,\partial_b\partial_d g\) satisfies \(A_2=\delta B_2\) identically. Closure alone does not make the naive truncation associative: at closed jets with \(\Theta(0)\neq0\) the associator is still nonzero, and removability requires adding a correction operator. Within the explicitly enumerated five-structure ansatz of bidifferential operators of order \(\le2\) per slot built from fixed \(\Theta\Theta\) and \(\Theta\partial\Theta\) contraction types with coefficients independent of the \(\Theta\)-jets, no \(B_2\) in this class solves \(A_2=\delta B_2\) at all closed 1-jets; Kontsevich's formality theorem supplies a natural jet-dependent correction for every smooth Poisson bivector, so what remains open here is not the existence of a general natural correction but its selection by the matrix theory. These are exact algebraic facts for polynomial test functions of degree \(\le2\) at the 1-jet level; they do not address orders \(\Theta^3\) and higher, operator-norm estimates, or convergence, do not select a correction from the matrix theory, and this paragraph does not derive the dictionary assumption or the half-sided inclusion and does not close Problem~A.
