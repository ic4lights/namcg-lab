\paragraph{Chart-blindness of the constant-\(\Theta\) symbol map and the reach of matrix selection at order \(\Theta^2\).} Within the constant-\(\Theta\) Abelian class the Weyl symbol map and its finite-\(N\) Weyl-ordered realization carry no semiclassical-chart datum: the map's own datum is the constant bivector plus an ordering convention, and the ordering is a gauge. Concretely, the Weyl-ordered mode product of the finite-\(N\) torus pair differs from the left-ordered mode product by the coboundary of the quadratic mode phase \(e^{-i\Theta m n/2}\), its commutator coefficient is the exact \(2i\sin(\Theta\sigma/2)\) in the symplectic pairing \(\sigma((m,n),(p,q))=mq-np\), equal to the constant-\(\Theta\) Moyal bracket and differing from the Poisson bracket first at order \(\Theta^3\), and its structure constants depend on the mode labels and \(\Theta\) only. Since the coordinate commutator \([\hat X^I,\hat X^J]=\mathrm i\Theta^{IJ}\mathbb 1\) of the class is central, every normalized state of the fixed Heisenberg algebra has the same constant effective bivector, so states and condensates of the fixed algebra induce no leaf dependence. Combining with the pullback selection: at a fixed closed bivector 1-jet the reachable solutions form the image of the 20-dimensional fiber of the embedding 2-jet; its first variation along the fiber spans a 20-dimensional subspace of the 36-dimensional kernel freedom, and the complementary 16 kernel directions are invisible to every first variation of the order-\(\Theta^2\) datum along the fiber. Hence within the stated class no matrix-side object -- symbol map, ordering convention, trace pairing, state, or condensate -- resolves the semiclassical-chart selection; chart-resolving data must come from outside the class, namely from noncentral commutator (condensate) backgrounds or the Presentation-B vacuum correspondence. These are exact algebraic facts for polynomial test data of degree \(\le 2\) at order \(\Theta^2\) within the class stated here; they do not cover noncentral commutator backgrounds, \(D>4\) submanifold embeddings, non-Abelian configurations, or orders \(\Theta^3\) and higher, do not derive the dictionary assumption, and this paragraph does not close Problem~A.
