\emph{Pushforward weights on the profile simplex from the named slot and frame measures.} Within the named coupled gaussian slot law of the fourth preceding paragraph, slots \(w_{i}\) with \(\mathbb{E}\,w_{i}w_{j}^{*}=s^{2}g_{i}\delta_{ij}\) at profile \(g\) of total weight \(W\), and under the Haar frame measure of the third preceding paragraph, the fibre-integration route from a configuration-space measure to a weight on profile space is executed exactly for the two named measures of these paragraphs. On the slot side the canonical profile-space coordinate built from the configuration is the fibre map \(p(w)=(\lvert w_{1}\rvert^{2},\dots,\lvert w_{D}\rvert^{2})/\sum_{j}\lvert w_{j}\rvert^{2}\) on the simplex; writing \(G=\prod_{i}g_{i}\), the pushforward of the slot law under \(p\) is exactly \((D-1)!\big/\bigl(G(\sum_{i}p_{i}/g_{i})^{D}\bigr)\): in polar coordinates \(w_{i}=\sqrt{E_{i}}\,e^{\mathrm{i}\theta_{i}}\) the squared magnitudes \(E_{i}\) are independent exponentials of means \(s^{2}g_{i}\); the change of variables \(E_{i}=T\,p_{i}\) contributes the Jacobian \(T^{D-1}\), integrating out the total gives \(\int_{0}^{\infty}T^{D-1}e^{-T\Lambda}\,dT\=(D-1)!/\Lambda^{D}\) with \(\Lambda=\sum_{i}p_{i}/(s^{2}g_{i})\), and the scale \(s^{2}\) cancels. Two structural properties are exact. The weight is symmetric under joint relabelling of \((g,p)\), and it determines the input profile up to the scale fixed by \(W\), so reading the pushforward as a weight on profile space re-imports the profile that already indexes the slot law rather than producing one: at uniform input the weight is relabelling-symmetric and flat, and the relabelling asymmetry of every non-uniform member of the family is label data of the input \(g\) passed through the map. And the reciprocal logarithm of the weight, \(\Phi(p)=D\log\sum_{i}p_{i}/g_{i}\), is convex on the simplex, strictly so along every segment along which \(\sum_{i}p_{i}/g_{i}\) is not constant, has no interior stationary profile unless \(g\) is uniform, and has its unique minimum at the vertex of the largest slot variance: so far from selecting the uniformity locus of the third preceding paragraph, this weight selects the single-slot profile, at which the obstruction factor of the sixth preceding paragraph attains its largest value, and it selects it by the input labels. On the frame side the Haar measure pushes forward under \(u\mapsto q=(\lVert u_{1}\rVert^{2},\dots,\lVert u_{D}\rVert^{2})\) to the flat exchangeable weight on the simplex, the fully degenerate symmetric case of the dichotomy of the preceding paragraph with every profile a minimiser, so its profile content is zero and the unordered quotient of the simplex inherits the same flat weight; the frame-weight variance of the second preceding paragraph is a statistic of this same coordinate, not a further weight. For the wider class of product slot laws in which the squared slot magnitudes are independent and the law of each is fixed by \(s^{2}\) times the slot variance, the same two properties hold by the same equivariance and identifiability argument: at uniform input the pushforward is relabelling-symmetric, and every relabelling-asymmetric member determines, and so re-imports, the input profile indexing the law. The frame side is thus flat and the slot side is the input profile in measure form, so within these named measures and these named fibre maps the fibre-integration route yields no weight on profile space beyond the profile data already supplied; the matrix integral itself is not evaluated here, these statements supply no state and no selection mechanism, they do not fix the state that sets \(W\), \(g\), \(s\) or the frame \(u\), they leave the missing steps (i)-(iv) of the Missing step paragraph unchanged, and they close nothing in Problem~G.
