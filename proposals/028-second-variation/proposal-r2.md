# Proposal 028-second-variation, round 2

## Result
Bounded structural result plus bounded no-go for the A-dependence of the one-loop weight-two coefficient in the CP^{1,2} four-matrix class with the supplied IKKT content. Nothing closes F or E.

1. **Exact supertrace lemma (all X, not only A=0).** For arbitrary functions X^a on the leaf, every species operator is H_i = (-Box_X)(x)1 + Sigma_i^{ab}(x){F_ab,.} + m_i, with the same Box_X and the same {F_ab,.} for all species (Hessian, ghost and Dirac square checked symbolically for random polynomial X; `arbitrary_X_identities.py`). The species tensors satisfy sum w d = 0 and sum w tr(Z...Z) = 0 for n = 1,2,3 (all index tuples, non-symmetrised; `tensors_lemma.py`); n = 4 gives the s^4 character (f_e^2+f_m^2)^2. Hence in the Duhamel expansion of any regulated trace in (m_i, Z_i) each term is a species constant times a species-independent operator trace (no commutation of chi, Box_X, {F,.} is used). A toy check with random non-commuting matrices confirms c_0 = c_1 = c_3 = 0 and c_2 = -M_str Tr[chi e^{-H}] (`duhamel_toy.py`).
2. **Answers to the three questions of the target, within the class.**
   - *Nonzero?* Exactly zero for every A are only the count term, the spin term (quadratic in the Zeeman operator) and the cubic term. What remains without Z insertions is +M_str Tr[chi Phi'(H_X)] (for Phi = exp(-.): -M_str Tr[chi e^{-H_X}]), M_str = 4 mu^2 + n_ex mu_i^2; at the leading symbol it is proportional to M_str times the moment of 1/(sigma_X - i0). Nothing is claimed about the next-to-leading symbol of that term.
   - *Reduces to mass supertrace times j(a)?* Yes, generalised: j(a) is replaced by the moment of 1/(sigma_X - i0), with sigma_X built from dX^a (first jet only). This leading-symbol term depends on A only through its first derivatives, so its second variation has no second-derivative term and no EH-form term; the next-to-leading symbol (second jet of X) is not computed, and no K^{mu nu} or metric-fluctuation reading is used. The Hessian of the real (PV) part for the Gaussian window of Delta_{3/4} is numerical, at frozen coefficients and phi = pi/2: indefinite in 40 of 59 draws (5 positive, 14 negative definite), numerically nonzero along alpha ~ q in all draws with non-null q (`mass_moment_hessian.py`, `mass_moment_stats.py`).
   - *Contains the s^4 Zeeman character?* Not at that order. It is the next order, together with M_str (mass insertion into the scalar a_1), the vector mass times tr Sigma Sigma, and sum w d m^2. These are the only species constants that can multiply an EH-form term.
3. **Bounded no-go for a sign at the next order.** At A = 0 the order-Z^4 integrand in the Gaussian window grows as s^2 (log-slope 2.00 at four orbit points, `z4_large_s.py`), because the flux is nonzero on the kernel plane of sigma(Box). The Schwinger coefficient of the s^4 term is divergent, so no finite EH-type coefficient and no sign follows. The non-expanded leading-symbol logarithm is given in closed form (`logdet_leading.py`); its second variation is not computed.
4. **Covariance (SME question).** At the phase phi = 0 (first-jet terms vanish) and leading q, the flux shift is F_ab -> F^0_ab + eps c (xi.Y_q)(q_a alpha_b - q_b alpha_a); at general phase the sin(phi) term is also present. At fixed proper time and kappa = 3/4 the Gaussian averages in the order-F^4 integrand depend on q.y at fixed q.q (factor > 2 over rapidity 1.2), through y^mu and the window (not separated by the diagnostic). Proposal only: the non-invariant part (|phi|^2 = E^2+B^2 type) is 2 to 180 times the invariant part at eight orbit points (`lorentz_diag.py`; norms of different tensors, one proper time). The M_str piece is the mass insertion of 026 / the Pauli-Sakharov term and is expected to be covariant in (g_rho, rho) before windowing; it is not computed on the leaf. Proposal only: this resembles the time-like-vector-field Lorentz violation noted by Steinacker; no equation-level match is claimed and it is not in the LaTeX.

## Research and reasoning
**Setting.** Poisson limit, leaf CP^{1,2}, X^a = T^a + A^a, bilinear term mu^2 = -3, background gauge D_aA^a; species: vector (4, w=1), n_ex = 6 scalars (mass mu_i^2), ghost (-2), 16 real fermion components (w = -1/2, no bilinear mass); one species-independent window chi(Delta_kappa). Supplied input: the fermion content only. The relation Dslash^2 = Box + (1/2)Gamma^{ab}{F_ab,.} holds for arbitrary X as identity (ii) below.

**Operators at arbitrary X.** With D_a = {X_a,.}, F_ab = {X_a,X_b}: (i) [D_a,D_b] = {F_ab,.} (Jacobi); (ii) (Gamma^a D_a)^2 = eta^{ab}D_aD_b + (1/2)Gamma^{ab}{F_ab,.}; (iii) the Euler-Lagrange operator of the second-variation density (1/4)[(D_a e_b - D_b e_a)^2 + 2F^{ab}{e_a,e_b} + 2mu^2 e^2] equals L e with L_a = -Box e_a + D_aD_b e^b - 2{F_ab,e^b} + mu^2 e_a. All three verified symbolically (`arbitrary_X_identities.out.txt`). Gauge fixing removes D_aD_b; the ghost operator is {X_a,{c,X^a}} = -Box c. The fermion operator compared with -Box is -Dslash^2, so Z_D = -(1/4)[Gamma^a,Gamma^b] and Z_V = -Sigma_V; the relative sign matters for n = 3 (with the opposite sign T_3 is nonzero; verified).

**Lemma.** The Duhamel expansion of Tr[chi Phi(H_i)] in (m_i, Z_i) gives terms (sum_i w_i m_i^p tr_i(Sigma...Sigma)) x (common operator trace). Constants: T_0 = sum w d = 0; T_1 = T_2 = T_3 = 0 as tensors; first mass constant M_str = 4 mu^2 + n_ex mu_i^2 (= -12 + 6 mu_i^2 at mu^2 = -3; zero at mu_i^2 = 2). Correction (F6): this is the condition str(m^2) = 0, Visser eq. 33; it is not eq. 35, str(k_1 m^2) = 0, because k_1 differs by species (Visser Table 1: 1/6 for a minimal scalar, -1/3 for a Dirac spinor). Neither equation is used in the LaTeX; next: sum w d m^2, c_12 = mu^2 tr_V(Sigma Sigma), and T_4 (the character coefficient). The statement is for the formal expansion; convergence is not claimed.

**Relation to 026.** 026's weighted a_1 = 0 for every Poisson bivector is the n <= 3 statement here, with Z_i playing the role of the connection b of 026; its a_2 = (1/2) S_4 (quartic in b) is the T_4 term; its mass-insertion formula (curvature part -(sum w n m/6) rho R[g_rho], connection part -(sum w kappa m) rho^2 X) is the M_str and c_12 constants, kappa_i being proportional to tr Sigma Sigma (Dirac/vector ratio 1/2, verified). 026's flat massless class has M_str = c_12 = 0 and zero background flux, so the second variation of the T_4 term about it vanishes, consistent with "no EH term at A = 0". In the CP^{1,2} class mu^2 = -3 supplies M_str and c_12 and the background flux is nonzero; that is the only way an EH-form term can arise, and it is not covered by the lemma (it needs the next-to-leading symbol on the six-dimensional leaf).

**Leading symbol and A-dependence.** sigma_X(xi) = eta_ab xi(D^a) xi(D^b), xi(D^a) = xi.W^a, depends on dX^a only. For A^a = alpha^a cos(phi), phi = q.y: W^a(eps) = W^a - eps sin(phi) alpha^a Y_q, and Ham(F^{ab}) = s c_{ab} + eps s (alpha^a q^b - alpha^b q^a)[sin(phi) Ham(M^{45}) + m cos(phi) Y_q] by the chain rule (checked against the Poisson algebra for linear A, `mass_moment_hessian.py` part A). At leading q only the cos term survives: the flux shifts by lambda(xi) Fq_ab, lambda = xi.Y_q. So the q^4 block of any leading-symbol functional is a quadratic form in the Maxwell field strength Fq, with coefficients fixed by the background flux, Y_q and the window.

**Mass-moment Hessian.** N(eps) = (4pi)^-3 det(P)^{-1/2} M(lambda), lambda = spec(eta B_eps), B_eps = What_eps P^-1 What_eps^T, What_eps = What - eps alpha Y_q^T. B_eps > 0, so Re N > 0 for every eps (Gaussian lemma of 027). The Hessian of the real (PV) part along alpha is indefinite in the sampled draws (numerical, frozen coefficients, phi = pi/2).

**Obstruction.** At A = 0 the order-4 integrand (4pi)^-3 det(P+isS)^{-1/2} s^4 <|C|^2> (C = sum_k z_k^2, z = E + iB, |C| = f_e^2+f_m^2, identity verified) scales as s^2 at all four tested points, so the order-by-order Schwinger series is not an expansion in a small parameter for the kernel-plane directions (the flux has hypoelliptic weight 2). A resummed object exists: sum_i w_i ln det(sigma + Z_i) = ln(sigma^2-4f_m^2) + ln(sigma^2+4f_e^2) + 4 ln sigma - 2 ln[(sigma+f_m)^2+f_e^2] - 2 ln[(sigma-f_m)^2+f_e^2], with series -6 (f_e^2+f_m^2)^2/sigma^4 (`logdet_leading.py`).
