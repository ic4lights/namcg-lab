\emph{Variational rigidity of the frame-averaged decoupling locus.} Within the named coupled gaussian slot law of the second preceding paragraph, at fixed \(D\) and \(W=\sum_{i}g_{i}>0\), and in the frame-law notation of the preceding paragraph, the frame-averaged decoupling functional \(Q_{\pi}(g)=\mathbb{E}_{\pi}\Gamma=g^{T}\Sigma_{\pi}g\) is variationally self-identifying: the fixed-weight stationarity equation reads \(2\Sigma_{\pi}g=\lambda\mathbf{1}\), and since \(\Sigma_{\pi}\) annihilates the constant profiles, pairing with \(\mathbf{1}\) forces \(\lambda=0\); the stationary profiles are therefore exactly the kernel of \(\Sigma_{\pi}\), which is exactly the set of profiles constant on the coincidence-graph components, which by positive semidefiniteness is exactly the zero locus of \(Q_{\pi}\) and, at fixed weight, exactly its minimiser set; on the mean-zero directions the second variation of \(Q_{\pi}\) is at least \(2\lambda^{+}\) times the squared norm, with \(\lambda^{+}\) the stiffness of the preceding paragraph, and it is flat along the kernel directions. This stationarity is rigid across the decoupling cone: for every strictly monotone \(C^{1}\) reparametrisation the stationary locus is unchanged, new stationary profiles arising only at stationary values of the reparametrisation; and for every positive combination of frame laws the stationary locus is the piecewise-constant locus of the union of their coincidence graphs, so every functional in the class of those built from frame-covariance data of profile-independent frame laws through positive combinations and monotone reparametrisations has a purely combinatorial stationary locus, none selects profile values within a component, and whenever a coincidence graph is disconnected the minimiser set is a continuum, of which the two-block datum of the preceding paragraph is the minimal case; no repackaging of the averaged decoupling datum within this class turns the frame law into a profile selector. The flat kernel directions localise the missing ingredient: when a functional is the sum of \(Q_{\pi}\) and a \(C^{1}\) term symmetric under slot relabelling, the uniform profile is stationary for the sum, and when that sum is strictly convex on the fixed-weight slice the uniform profile is its unique stationary profile; a unique non-uniform stationary profile requires an extra term that either distinguishes the slots, as the integrated fuzzy variance proxies do through their label dependence, or, together with a relabelling-asymmetric frame law, lifts the flat kernel family, while a symmetric non-convex term with a relabelling-symmetric frame law leaves only degenerate relabelling orbits of minimisers that themselves require a further selection input. No functional on profile space is produced for this role by the core action: the core supplies weights on configurations, and states only under the positivity proviso of the state and coarse-graining paragraph, no weight on profile space, while the identification of the profile with block-row variance data belongs to the state model — the variational form of the selection question is thus settled inside the named class, and the remaining demand is a weight on profile space produced by the core and non-degenerate along the kernel directions, which would be a derivation on the state side rather than a refinement of the frame average. These statements are for the named coupled gaussian slot law at fixed \(D\) and \(W\) with profile-independent frame laws, and for the decoupling-cone class just defined; they establish a stationarity identity and variational rigidity within the named class, they supply no state, no selection mechanism and no weight on profile space, they do not fix the state that sets \(W\), \(g\), \(s\) or the frame \(u\), they leave the missing steps (i)-(iv) of the Missing step paragraph unchanged, and they close nothing in Problem~G.
