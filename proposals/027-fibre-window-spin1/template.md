# Proposal 027-fibre-window-spin1, round 1

## Result
Bounded, conditional results for Problem F; no sign of G_eff, no closure.
1. **Gaussian lemma (proved).** For any Gaussian window the Lorentzian principal-value mass moment M = i int ds prod(1+i s lam_k)^(-1/2), lam=(-c,l1,l2,l3), has Re M = (2/c) int_0^1 dv prod(1+l_k(1-v^2)/c)^(-1/2) > 0 and Im M > 0 (contour rotation s=-iu; the factor (1-cu-i0)^(-1/2) is real for u<1/c, imaginary after). This turns the 025 sampling (4000 spectra) into a theorem for Gaussian windows.
2. **Fibre-bounded cone window (Avenue 1).** At 60 orbit points (1e-9): in whitened coordinates (|u|^2=alpha q_b, |z|^2=beta q_f) S_uu has eigenvalues (-1/2, sinh^2(tau)/2 x3), sigma=Q_L(u+wz), w conformal with |w z|=coth(tau)|z|, image spacelike, so the cone boundary |u|=|z| is tangent to the null cone of Q_L. Apex-inside shell formula (proved): PV int_ball = (R^2-|p|^2)I_0/2 + p^T J p, I_0=8 pi^2/cosh^2(tau). Extension to the tangency distance: argued (domain of dependence for the wave operator) and checked by quadrature to 1e-3; then Re<1/(sigma-i0)>_W = (1/2) J_c pi Q_f I_0 int chi > 0, independent of the cutoff shape and of w. Finite; Im>=0. Electric growth: closed forms for A,B on three planes (base, vertical, centre); the fibre-driven rate is 2 s_f sinh(tau) (bounded by the fibre bound, independent of Lambda), the base-driven rate is linear in |u|; log det finite; growth is a property of the Schwinger representation and is not removed by the fibre bound.
3. **Vector and ghost sectors, invariant sector (Avenues 2/3, exact in s=0).** tr(E+R/6) = 4 D_1 + 6 s^2(1+a^2)/(t+i s a^2) - 4 i s mu^2 (matrix Seeley-DeWitt, verified to ~120 digits at 3 points; the SD route reproduces the exact 025 kernel D_1 to 125 digits). Closed forms Q_mu=-mu^2 J, J=2(1+i/a)/(1+a^2); Q_sp = -8/(1+a^2) - i 4(3a^2+1)/(a^3(1+a^2)). Bosonic Re Q = (2+n_ex) Re Q_sc + (16-2 n_ex mu_i^2)/(1+a^2) plus the scale-dependent mode-count term. **Not sign-definite**: the Q_sc piece is indefinite and the mode-count constant has the sign of a logarithm; the full-leaf spin (F^2) term has a purely imaginary large-s log tail with coefficient prop. to (1-a^2)/(1+2a^2), changing sign at |y4|=ell.
4. **Supertrace (Avenue 4).** With the supplied IKKT content (16 real fermion components, weight -1/2, n_ex=6) the component count, the index (Dirac index = 1/2 vector index, checked) and the F^2 term cancel; the Zeeman character is 4(cos sB - cosh sA)^2 = s^4 (A^2+B^2)^2+..., a sum of squares (no self-dual zero in Lorentzian signature). What remains of the weight-two term at A=0 is the mass supertrace (4 mu^2 + n_ex mu_i^2) times the positive mass moment. The species-independent R/6+E part does not carry the Newton coefficient for this content; it must come from the next order or from the A-dependence. This overlaps in spirit with the merged 019 count/index rules (Theta-built class, Euclid flux); new here are the conjugate-coordinate class, the Lorentzian (A,B) structure and the explicit remainder.

## Research and reasoning
**Conventions.** As in 025: Delta_H4 unit-radius Laplacian, Box=-(Delta_H4-Delta_H3) on fibre-constant functions, exponent t Delta_H4 - i s H_V, x=s/t, a=sinh tau=y4/ell. Symbols are the 023 forms (helper modules copied).

**Method check.** sd_scalar_vs_exact.py: the formal Seeley-DeWitt first coefficient of P=t Delta_4 + i s Box (complex symmetric metric G^-1 = t(ell^2 eta + y y) + i s m^2 eta) equals the exact H^4 x H^3 kernel D_1(z0=t-is, w0=is) of 025 for generic (t,s) to 125 digits. This validates the route before it is used on the vector.

**Vector.** sd_vector_spin.py (a): the sign of Sigma is fixed by requiring J+Sigma to represent so(3,1); the cross term eta eta J Sigma equals -2 s J_ab A^b exactly, so H_V0 = -Box + cross = C_G - C_41 - C_Sigma (an independent re-derivation of the 022 identity in this sector). (b) The 4x4 connection is omega_0 + omega_M with omega_M traceless, so tr E = 4E_0 - G tr(omega_M omega_M); analytically tr(X^a X^b)=2 g3^{ab} (g3=sigma(Delta_H3)), giving E_spin = 2 s^2 tr[G g3] = 6 s^2(1+a^2)/(t+i s a^2), because the pencil eigenvalues of (v,u) are (-1,a^2,a^2,a^2) and g3=u+v kills the time direction. Verified with exact rationals incl. an off-axis point and mu^2=1,-3. Dirac: sd_dirac_spin.py gives exactly half the vector spin term, for both signs of the coupling.

**6D form.** The derivation uses only the Gram of the Hamiltonian fields: E_spin = 2 s^2 tr[G (-sigma(C31))], with -sigma(C31)(xi) = A^2-B^2 of F(xi)=xi(J_ab) (checked). spin_term_6d.py: on the base block -K31_uu = I/2+S_uu; the large-s tail coefficient C is purely imaginary and proportional to the sum over ker S of (-K31) = 2 q_c = (1-a^2)/(1+2a^2) (spin_tail_6d.py, centre_flux_kernel.py, flux_closed_forms.py). The contour-angle dependence of the real part (-0.7390/-0.7261/-0.7132 at three angles, one orbit point) is this tail; the rotated (angle pi/2) real part is negative at all 8 points, which is one scheme only.

**Cone window.** cone_window_geometry.py, tangency_identity.py (60 points): c=1/2, c/lambda=ell^2/y4^2, |w|^2=1+ell^2/y4^2, image orthogonal to the time vector. shell_apex_lemma.py / cone_window_moment.py: the quadratic formula checked to 1e-4 at |p|<r; brute-force PV quadrature for |p|>r shows the quadratic formula holds for spatial p up to R sqrt(1+c/lambda) (tested P=0.5..1.30 against threshold 1.2991, differences <=8e-3 on values ~10) and fails at P=1.35 (diff -0.23). The cap formula was unconverged and is not used. Delta = int delta dtheta is O(1e-3) against I_0~30 at 10 orbit points. Domain-of-dependence argument: a causal line through p at spatial distance P with speed v_c=sqrt(c/lambda) passes at distance P/sqrt(1+v_c^2) from the ball centre, so every causal line meets the ball iff P<r sqrt(1+v_c^2) = tangency. This is a plausibility argument for Holmgren-John continuation, not a proof. I had expected an O(Q^2) displacement correction; the tangency identity removes it.

**Mass moment lemma.** gaussian_pv_lemma.py: formula vs contour integral 8e-11 over 300 spectra; 4000 samples all Re,Im > 0.

**Flux.** flux_window_growth.py, flux_closed_forms.py: closed forms to 1e-13 (centre, vertical), base maximum within 6e-5 (3000 directions). This is the full linear symbol F(xi)=xi(J_ab), not only the centre part. On the centre plane F_abF^ab/2 = B^2-A^2 = |z|^2 (a^2-1)/(2(1+2a^2)), positive for a>1; the 025 sign statement (sample-dependent threshold in y4^2) is consistent in kind but I did not reproduce its numbers.

**Real versus imaginary part.** Which part of sum ln(lambda - i0) is the physical action depends on the identification S_eff=(i/2) sum ln; both parts are reported. The apex log of the F^2 term is purely imaginary (large-s phase -i of the Gaussian product); the mass principal value is real.

**Literature.** Nothing new relied on. That the Newton constant arises at finite order in the maximally supersymmetric model is from the SME scout (2405.09804, not independently verified); it is not used as input.
