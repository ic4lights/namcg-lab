\paragraph{Matrix-side selection of the order-\(\Theta^2\) correction on the pullback class.} In the semiclassical coordinate construction with constant nondegenerate \(\Theta^{IJ}\), a local diffeomorphism \(x(y)\) defines the leaf bivector by \(\Theta^{IJ}=\theta^{ab}(y)\,\partial_ax^I\,\partial_bx^J\); pulling the operator product back to leaf coordinates gives an exactly associative product whose order-\(\Theta\) term is the naive truncation with \(\Theta(x)=\theta(y)\) and whose order-\(\Theta^2\) term is the explicit matrix-selected correction \(B_2(F,G)=-\tfrac{1}{8}\Theta^{IJ}\Theta^{KL}(M^a_IM^b_K\,\partial_a\partial_bF+N^a_{IK}\,\partial_aF)(M^c_JM^d_L\,\partial_c\partial_dG+N^d_{JL}\,\partial_dG)\), where \(M\) is the Jacobian of the inverse map and \(N\) its second-jet tensor. The induced 1-jets satisfy \([\theta,\theta](0)=0\) identically in the embedding 2-jet, so this class lies on the coboundary-solvable side of the dichotomy, and on the polynomial 2-jet model \(A_2=\delta B_2\) holds exactly for the selected correction; the correction lies outside the enumerated five-structure ansatz because it contains \(\partial\theta\,\partial\theta\) and \(\theta\,\partial\theta\) channels. The selection is not a function of the bivector 1-jet: fixing \(\theta(0)\) and \(\partial\theta(0)\) leaves a nontrivial fiber of embedding 2-jets whose selected corrections differ by kernel elements, so pinning down a single correction within the pointwise freedom requires semiclassical-chart (embedding) data beyond the 1-jet. These are exact algebraic facts for polynomial test functions of degree \(\le2\) within the pullback class at order \(\Theta^2\); they do not select the semiclassical chart from the matrix-model dynamics, do not cover \(D>4\) submanifold embeddings, non-Abelian configurations, or orders \(\Theta^3\) and higher, and this paragraph does not derive the dictionary assumption and does not close Problem~A.
