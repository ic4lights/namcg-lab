\paragraph{Covariant variables, the joint flow and the Noether charge of \(\rho^2X\) on the bifurcation surface (conditional; not closure).}
Keep the setting of the paragraph on Moser variables and of the paragraph on the order \(\Lambda^2\), with \(n_D=0\): four matrix coordinates, the Poisson limit, \(\omega=\Theta^{-1}\) closed, the density \(\tfrac{N_{\mathrm{tot}}}6\rho R[g_\rho]+\kappa_X\rho^2X\) with \(X=T_{\lambda\mu\nu}T^{\lambda\mu\nu}\), and \(c_W(r)=N_{\mathrm{tot}}/6+\kappa_Xr\). Let \(\xi\) be the rotation of the \((x^1,x^2)\) plane (the boost for \(\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)\), with \(x^1\) the time coordinate), \(\Sigma=\{x^1=x^2=0\}\), and \(B^a{}_b=\partial_b\xi^a\).

\emph{Covariant variables.} Let \(X^a\) be the matrix coordinates as functions on the leaf and \(\gamma_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{ab}\partial_\mu X^a\partial_\nu X^b\), so that \(\gamma=\eta\) in the matrix chart and the curvature \(R[\gamma]\) vanishes. The Poisson-limit operators are built from \(\{X^a,\cdot\}=\Theta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu X^a\partial_\nu\) and from contractions of matrix indices with \(\eta\), so their local invariants are unchanged by the global translations and \(\eta\)-rotations of the \(X^a\); since a local diffeomorphism onto flat space is fixed by \(\gamma\) up to such an isometry, they are functionals of \((\gamma,\omega)\) covariant under changes of chart, with \(e^\alpha{}_\mu=\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\omega_{\mu\beta}\), \(G=\omega\gamma^{-1}\omega^T\), \(\rho=\lvert\operatorname{Pf}\omega\rvert/\sqrt{\lvert\det\gamma\rvert}\) and the torsion of \(e\) taken with the Levi-Civita connection of \(\gamma\). Densities carry the factor \(\sqrt{\lvert\gamma\rvert}\), which is \(1\) in the matrix chart, so that \(\sqrt G=\rho^2\sqrt{\lvert\gamma\rvert}\). The invariants \(X\), \(T_{\lambda\mu\nu}T^{\mu\lambda\nu}\), \(V_\mu V^\mu\), \(\mathcal T\), \(\nabla_\mu V^\mu\) and \(R_G\), computed in a random polynomial chart with \(\gamma\) the pull-back of \(\eta\), equal those of the matrix chart (exact arithmetic, one closed \(\omega\) and one chart per signature). The field \(\gamma\) is the contraction \(\eta\) of the matrix action written in another chart; it is not the metric \(G\) and adds no field. In this sense the dependence on \(\eta\) noted in the paragraph on Moser variables is tensorial.

\emph{Joint flow.} With both indices transformed, \(\mathcal L_\xi\omega=0\) holds for the family \(\Theta^{12}=1/f(r)\), \(\Theta^{34}=1\) and for constant \(\bar\omega\) in the span of \(e_{12}\) and \(e_{34}\), and fails for a generic constant \(\bar\omega\) (symbolic, both signatures); this is the invariance under the joint flow of the paragraph on the order \(\Lambda^2\). Then \(\mathcal L_\xi\gamma=0\), the fields \(e\), \(G\), \(\rho\) and \(\vartheta\) are Lie-dragged and regular, and \([e,B]=0\) on \(\Sigma\). The matrix coordinates are not Lie-dragged: \(\mathcal L_\xi X^a=B^a{}_bX^b\), the rigid rotation of the matrix index. In the variables \((\gamma,\omega)\) the hypothesis \(\mathcal L_\xi\phi=0\) of~\cite{IyerWald1994} holds; in the variables \((X^a,\omega)\) it fails by a global, not a local, transformation.

\emph{Charges on \(\Sigma\).} For a density depending on \((\gamma,\omega)\) and their first derivatives the Noether current contains at most first derivatives of \(\xi\), so its charge on \(\Sigma\), where \(\xi=0\), vanishes up to an exact form; this covers \(\rho^2X\), \(\rho^2\mathcal T\) and \(\rho^2V_\mu V^\mu\), and \(\rho^2\nabla_\mu V^\mu\) is a divergence. For a density \(\sqrt G\,\ell(R)\), with \(R\) the curvature of a torsion-free connection, the charge on \(\Sigma\) is \(2P_\alpha{}^{\beta\mu\nu}B^\alpha{}_\beta\) with \(P=\partial(\sqrt G\,\ell)/\partial R^\alpha{}_{\beta\mu\nu}\), whatever the connection, since \(\xi=0\) there. At random jets of a non-flat \(\gamma\) (exact arithmetic, three per signature)
\[
  R_G+\mathcal T+2\nabla_\mu V^\mu=R_W:=G^{\sigma\nu}(e^{-1})^\mu{}_\alpha\,R[\gamma]^\alpha{}_{\beta\mu\nu}\,e^\beta{}_\sigma ,
\]
the curvature decomposition of the Weitzenb\"ock connection of \(e\); for flat \(\gamma\) this is the identity \(R=-\mathcal T-2\nabla_\mu V^\mu\) of the paragraph on the order \(\Lambda^2\). With \([e,B]=0\) the charges of \(\sqrt G\,R_W\) and \(\sqrt G\,R_G\) on \(\Sigma\) coincide pointwise. Hence, for every real \(r\), the density \(L_r=L_0+r\kappa_X\sqrt G\,R_W\), with \(L_0\) the induced density in which \(X\) is the torsion invariant, equals \(L_0\) on the class, and its charge on \(\Sigma\) has the coefficient \(c_W(r)\). The density \(L_0\) gives \(r=0\), the coefficient \(c_A=N_{\mathrm{tot}}/6\) of the fixed-state replica, on which \(\rho^2X\) is bounded.

\emph{Compensation.} A term \(f^{abcd}R[\gamma]_{abcd}\) shifts the response \(T_\eta^{bd}\) of the density to \(\gamma\) at \(\gamma=\eta\) by \(E^{bd}=-2\partial_a\partial_cf^{abcd}\), which is identically conserved. For a Killing field \(\xi\) of \(\eta\), \(\partial_bQ^{ab}=2\xi_cE^{ac}\) with \(Q^{ab}=-2f^{abcd}\partial_c\xi_d-4\partial_df^{abcd}\xi_c\) (exact polynomial identity at sampled \(f\) with the symmetries of a curvature tensor, both signatures), and \(Q^{ab}=-2f^{abcd}\partial_c\xi_d\) on \(\Sigma\) is the charge of the added term there. By Stokes' theorem on the Cauchy surface of the wedge, the boost charge of the shift of \(T_\eta\) equals the shift of the charge on \(\Sigma\) minus the flux of \(Q^{ab}\) at spatial infinity. For variations of \(f\) of compact support the first-order change of the charge on \(\Sigma\) is therefore the boost charge of the change of \(T_\eta\), and the relation of~\cite{IyerWald1994} between the charge on \(\Sigma\) and the boost charge of \(T_\eta\), with its term at infinity, holds for every \(r\); for constant \(f\) the improvement vanishes and the charge on \(\Sigma\) is carried by that term. In the variables \((X^a,\omega)\) this freedom corresponds to the ambiguity \(\Theta\to\Theta+\mathrm dY\) of the symplectic potential, which leaves the charge on \(\Sigma\) unchanged only when \(\mathcal L_\xi\phi=0\)~\cite{JacobsonKangMyers1994}; here \(Y(\phi,\mathcal L_\xi X)\) with \(\mathcal L_\xi X^a=B^a{}_bX^b\) does not drop out.

\emph{Consequence.} Within these hypotheses the Iyer--Wald identity for the induced density does not select \(r\); the response \(T_\eta\) to \(\gamma\) is not identified here with \(T^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mu\nu}\) of Equation~\eqref{eq:first-law}. Each of the coefficients \(c_W(-1)\) and \(c_W(-4)\) of the paragraph on Moser variables, \(c_W(-2)\) of the paragraph on local frame rotations and \(c_W(0)=c_A\) is the charge on \(\Sigma\) of a density \(L_r\) equal to \(L_0\) on the class, and their differences are edge terms of the boost generator of \(T_\eta\), of the type of the superpotential of Equation~\eqref{eq:Top-FP}. For \(n_D=0\) the fixed-state replica has \(c_A-c_T=4\kappa_X\), so the requirement \(G_A=G_T\) of the paragraph on the normalisation holds if and only if the edge term that accompanies the replica contributes the coefficient of \(r=-4\), that is \(-4\kappa_X\) in units of \(c\) relative to \(L_0\). It is a property of the modular Hamiltonian of the state at \(\Sigma\), or of a local symmetry under which the compensating rotation would be a gauge transformation, and not of the induced density. The two readings of the paragraph on the comparator are related in the same way: a density of the bundle curvature, taken as a function of the curvature of the connection \(\Gamma[\gamma]-\tfrac12V\,\mathbb I+b\) with the fibre index identified with a tangent index by \(\partial X\), has the charge \(2(\partial L/\partial\Omega^\alpha{}_{\beta\mu\nu})B^\alpha{}_\beta\) on \(\Sigma\), the reading with \(H=\mathcal J\); taken as a function of \(\Omega-R[\gamma]\) it has none, the other reading; the two differ by a term of the form \(f^{abcd}R[\gamma]_{abcd}\). For the constraint \(b=F[\Theta]\), with \(F\) the covariantised composite built from the first jets of \((\gamma,\omega)\), a multiplier term depends on first derivatives of \((\gamma,\omega)\) only and has no charge on \(\Sigma\); this is the charge that the paragraphs on the comparator and on the order \(\Lambda^2\) list as not computed, and it is computed only in this setting.

These statements are for the Poisson limit, four matrix coordinates, the bosonic sectors with \(n_D=0\), the \(\LUV^2\) density, backgrounds Lie-dragged by the joint flow and regular on \(\Sigma\), and local charges; the identities are exact at the sampled jets and polynomials stated. They do not determine the edge term, the modular Hamiltonian, the replica or the Dirac sector, do not identify \(T_\eta\) with \(T^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mu\nu}\) of Equation~\eqref{eq:first-law}, use no metric other than the pull-back of the matrix contraction \(\eta\), and give no value or sign of \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\).
