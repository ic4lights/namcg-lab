1. (Established numerically, with the structural reason.) Including the Zeeman insertion eps P cos(phi) N (N = 2iK eta for the vector, (i/4)[Gam^a,Gam^b]K_ab for a 4x4 Dirac block, K_ab = kappa_a alpha_b - kappa_b alpha_a) the coefficient of ds/s changes: 16 pi^2 c_V = 4A - F1, 16 pi^2 c_D = 4A - F1/2 (F1 = y^2((kappa.alpha)^2 - kappa^2 alpha^2), F2 = kappa^2 (alpha.y)^2, A = (F1 - 2F2)/24; fit g1 = -0.8337, g2 = -0.3333 vs -5/6, -1/3; Dirac -0.33346, -0.33333 vs -1/3, -1/3). The extra term is the term quadratic in N, proportional to tr(Sigma Sigma). The weighted sum over the supertrace content vanishes (1e-5, three points): the massless resummed character term has no UV logarithm at this order, so its finite part is free of the logarithm-scheme ambiguity. The mass-linear logarithm is M_str A - mu^2 F1: not proportional to A for mu^2 != 0, and still y^mu-dependent (both F1, F2 contain y).
2. (Numerical, one orbit point.) The Zeeman-dressed vector response splits into spin blocks (E+-, B+-; diagonal and pair blocks). Blocks containing E+ grow on rays theta < theta* = arctan(f_e/f_m), blocks containing B+ grow for theta > theta*, all grow polynomially at theta*: no ray gives a convergent proper-time integral (this extends the leading-symbol statement of 032 to order eps^2). A continuation is a hypothesis. Validation of the two continuation prescriptions on the leading-symbol integral at (1.6, 0.9): branch-group rays (-4.007-9.981i, -4.015-9.973i) vs regulator e^{-i s m^2} on theta* extrapolated m^2 -> 0+ (-4.007..-4.013, -9.984..-9.992i): agree to ~0.01. This is a check of the prescription in a solvable case, not a derivation of it.
3. (Numerical, one orbit point, regulated, tetrad part.) With e^{-i s m^2}, m^2 = 0.4..1.3 max(f_e,f_m): gauge invariant (<= 6e-6); vector form NOT proportional to its log form 4A - F1 (relative defect 0.91-0.98) nor to A (0.79-0.82); massless supertrace form not proportional to A (0.87-1.0; scheme independent since no logarithm). Sign structure: Re of the vector form positive definite on the quotient by kappa for every m^2 tried; Im positive definite for m^2 <= 0.6, indefinite for >= 0.8; scalar form indefinite (Re); Re(x Q_S - 3 Q_V) definite for x in an interval containing [0,21] at every m^2. These sign statements move with m^2, with Re/Im, and the extrapolation m^2 -> 0 is unstable, so no sign statement at m^2 = 0 is made. The indefiniteness of 034 is therefore neither confirmed nor removed in the Zeeman-dressed term; proportionality to A is not restored.
4. (Correction, PRIOR-CLAIM-ERROR, non-conclusion-changing.) 034 `l3_fin.integrate` adds -c ln(s1) where Fin = lim[int_{s0} T2 - c ln(1/s0)] gives +c ln(s1) (test `t6`: c e^{-s}/s gives 5.41+1.20i instead of -gamma). Fin values shift by 2c ln s1, a multiple of A. The scheme-invariant defect |Q - lam A|/|A| is unchanged (`t7`): 0.19/0.075, 4.5e-3/4.4e-3, 2.8e-4/8e-8 (Re/Im); the normalised residuals quoted in the 034 block become 0.63/0.82, 0.49/0.99, 0.011/0.94. The "both signs" statement survives (quotient spectra of Re yy remain mixed). Sign-definiteness of a quotient spectrum is not invariant under the scheme shift c ln(lambda) because A is indefinite: only the defect is meaningful for forms with a logarithm.

## Research and reasoning
Operators (derived here, not copied): with Omega_a = omega_a - eps alpha_a P sin(phi), [omega_a, phi] = -i kappa_a: [Omega_a,Omega_b] = iF_ab + i eps P cos(phi) K_ab. The vector operator is eta Omega Omega + Z_V + mu^2, Z_V = -2 Zcal eta, Zcal = [D,D] = -[Omega,Omega] (D = i Omega). The coefficient -2 is checked by the longitudinal identity -Box D_a lam - 2 Zcal_ab D^b lam = -D_a Box lam - (D^b Zcal_ba) lam (first-derivative terms cancel only for -2). So Z_V = 2i(F + eps P cos(phi) K) eta, e^{-isZ0} = e^{2sF eta} = M(s) (the 034 block matrix), eigenvalues e^{+-2 s f_e}, e^{+-2 i s f_m}. Dirac: Z_D = (i/4)[Gam,Gam](F + ...), tr e^{-isZ0} = 4 cosh x cos y. Gauge invariance alpha -> alpha + lam kappa does NOT fix the sign of N (t2 passes for both signs); the sign is fixed by the identity above.

Engine: `engine_z.py` extends the 034 double-Duhamel engine. Node weights (sum over sig of e^{i sig phi(tau1)} e^{-i sig phi(tau2)}): nVV = pref wick4/4 (034), nVd = pref wick3 (i sig/4), ndV = pref wick3 (-i sig/4), ndd = pref wick2/4; spin factors tr M(s), tr[M(s) N], tr[M(s-tau) N M(tau) N]. Reductions checked: scalar species reproduces 034 engine_e_mp (1e-13, `t1`); gauge invariance of vec, Dirac, and the supertrace combination (`t2`); sum of spin blocks = vector total (3e-14, `t3`); the supertrace log coefficient cancels (nontrivial check of the relative normalisation of the VV and N N terms).

Continuation. Mass-linear term is i int ds Theta (n_ex mu_i^2 + mu^2 chi_V-type); chi_V e^{...} gives growth e^{+2 s f_e} (real axis) and e^{+2 i s f_m} (rays below theta*), exponent of a block on ray theta: -(f_e cos + f_m sin) + 2 f_e cos (E+) or + 2 f_m sin (B+). Branch-wise rotation and the Abel-type regulator are equivalent when each branch is analytic across m^2 = 0 (checked at leading symbol). For the 4D integrand the branch-wise decomposition is blocked by the pair blocks (interpolating phase), so the regulator was used; the m^2 -> 0 limit of the 4D regulated integral is not reached (tail e^{-m^2 rho sin(theta*)} needs rho_max ~ 45 and the integrand grows ~ rho^2 at theta*, quadrature degrading above rho ~ 50, hence m^2 >= 0.4). The Nielsen-Olesen imaginary part is kept.

Comparison with the original target: no value or sign of G_eff results; the Zeeman term does not restore proportionality to A or covariance. Hypotheses of the no-go and what can relax them: see Remaining open step.

## Remaining open step
Class of the no-go: Poisson limit, CP^{1,2}, nilpotent symbol with central seam, tetrad part, one orbit point, regulated continuation, vector and 4x4 Dirac content with massive vector only. Original target (a derived finite sign-definite covariant EH coefficient) is not reached. Ranked avenues, each relaxing a stated hypothesis:
1. Exact (analytic) u-integration of the Duhamel integrals so that spin-block exponents separate and the continuation m^2 -> 0 is taken branch-wise without a regulator (relaxes: regulator, quadrature); would turn the regulated sign data into definite ones.
2. Window average over the centre variable zeta of the full Fin (yy + cc + cross) with the corrected sign, using the scheme-invariant defect and the now-finite massless supertrace form (relaxes: per centre variable, tetrad only). The supertrace form has no logarithm, hence is the best-posed object for a sign test.
3. More orbit points (3.4 h each; the strongly electric limit f_e/f_m >> 1 where 034 saw near proportionality to A is the cheapest to analyse analytically).
4. Weight-one terms and gradients (relaxes nilpotent order), non-perturbative masses, a spectral (Landau-level) definition of the vector sector with ghost subtraction, positive-mass vacuum.
5. The y^mu-dependence (non-covariance of A and of F1, F2) is untouched by the Zeeman term and by the sign of G_eff; a covariant EH form needs another ingredient (connection-sector term X, or fibre structure), which is the real location of the gap.
