## Result
The localization step behind the beta-family integrability dichotomy of the integrated 093 paragraph (appH L3199) is proved as a unconditional real-analysis theorem for the named family. For the fixed-trace Dirichlet-beta weights \(\prod_i p_i^{a-1}\prod_{i<j}\lvert p_i-p_j\rvert^{\beta}\) (a>0, beta>=0) on the unit-trace simplex, on the ordered chamber the substitution y_k = p_k - p_{k+1} sends the coincidence walls to faces of the gap orthant; a face where exactly a run of s consecutive gaps vanishes kills exactly binom(s+1,2) pair factors, and the transverse integral is equivalent to the model integral of \(\prod_{1\le i\le j\le s}(y_i+\cdots+y_j)^{-(1-\beta)}\) over the vanishing-gap box, which by a Tonelli polar split plus induction over vanishing runs is finite exactly for \(1-\beta<2/(s+1)\). Hence, with depth m = s+1: the inverse-Vandermonde moment \(\int\lvert V\rvert^{-1}d\mu\) converges near an interior stratum of deepest coincidence depth m exactly for \(\beta>1-2/m\) and diverges for \(\beta\le1-2/m\), for every m and every chamber dimension D, all divergence being stratum-local, and over the whole interior chamber finiteness is \(\beta>1-2/D\). The transverse polar exponent satisfies the exact identity \(e+1=\binom{m}{2}(\beta-(1-2/m))\), so the thresholds 0, 1/3, 1/2 at m=2,3,4 are exact. At an r-fold zero coincidence at the simplex boundary p=0 the same bookkeeping with the Dirichlet factor carried through yields exactly \(1-\beta<\min(2/r,\,2a/(r-1))\); for a>=1 the interior strata are the binding ones, and the computed member (a,beta)=(|N-2M|+1,2) of the 091 paragraph lies strictly above every coincidence threshold. This upgrades frontier stanza derived_subresults_093_post24 item (vi) from CONDITIONAL ("via standard local-radial / hyperplane-arrangement localization") to a proved lemma for the named family. ## Research and reasoning
Setup. Fix D and the ordered chamber p_1>...>p_D>0 of the unit-trace simplex. The substitution (p_1,...,p_D) -> (y_1,...,y_{D-1}, t) with y_k = p_k - p_{k+1}, t = p_D is linear unimodular, p_i = t + sum_{k>=i} y_k, so pair differences are p_i - p_j = y_i + ... + y_{j-1}, consecutive gap sums, and t plus the nonvanishing gap data are integrable longitudinal variables bounded away from the walls on a patch. The combined integrand for \(\int\lvert V\rvert^{-1}d\mu\) is \(\prod_i p_i^{a-1}\prod_{i<j}\lvert p_i-p_j\rvert^{\beta-1}\).
Face bookkeeping (established). A face of the gap orthant is a set S of gap indices set to zero; the vanishing pair factors are exactly the intervals [i, j-1] contained in S, so their number is sum over maximal runs of length l in S of binom(l+1,2). Verified exhaustively by the run-length identity for D=2..8 in s1 v6 PART A and, independently and with different code, over all subsets for D=7 in spot v2 check 2.
Model integral and criterion (established). On a face patch where exactly a run of s consecutive gaps vanishes, the nonvanishing pair factors and the Dirichlet factor are bounded above and below by positive constants on the patch, so integrability on the patch is equivalent to finiteness of M_s(p) = int over (0,eps)^s of prod over intervals I in the run of (sum_{k in I} y_k)^{-p}, p = 1-beta. This is the braid (consecutive-sum) product-of-linear-forms model, and it is self-similar: faces of the box are shorter runs with the same family of forms. Write y = rho*omega and apply Tonelli (integrand >= 0): M_s = (int_0^{eps c} rho^{s-1-p*binom(s+1,2)} drho) x (angular integral over the gap simplex). The radial factor is finite exactly for p < 2/(s+1). The angular integral is positive (integrand positive on an open set) and finite for p < 2/(s+1): near a boundary face of the simplex where a run of l <= s-1 consecutive omega's vanishes, the transverse integral is again the model M_l at the same p, finite by induction on s since p < 2/(s+1) < 2/(l+1) for l <= s-1; base case s=1: p<1. Hence M_s finite iff p < 2/(s+1); and for p >= 2/(s+1) Tonelli gives M_s = infinity times a positive factor = infinity (the same induction covers angular divergence only when the radial factor already diverges). Depth m strata in the chamber are exactly runs of s = m-1 vanishing gaps, so convergence near every interior stratum point of deepest depth m is beta > 1-2/m and beta <= 1-2/m diverges; the binding stratum over the whole interior chamber is s = D-1, i.e. beta > 1-2/D; off the arrangement the integrand is continuous, so boundedness on compact sets is immediate.
Exponent identity (established). In transverse polar coordinates at depth m the radial exponent is e = (m-2) - (1-beta)*binom(m,2), so e+1 = binom(m,2)*(beta - (1-2/m)) exactly, and e+1>0 iff beta > 1-2/m; s1 v6 PART B verifies the symbolic identity, the threshold solve, and e=-1 iff beta=1-2/m for m=2..8; spot v2 check 1 re-verifies with independent sympy code and prints thresholds [0, 1/3, 1/2] at m=2,3,4.
Boundary strata (established, named family). At an r-fold zero coincidence p_1,...,p_r -> 0 with the remaining slots bounded away, cross differences are bounded, and the r-cluster bookkeeping (r coordinates, Dirichlet factor rho^{r(a-1)}, polar rho^{r-1}, binom(r,2) vanishing differences) gives radial exponent (beta-1)*binom(r,2) + r(a-1) + r - 1, integrable iff 1-beta < 2a/(r-1) (s1 v6 PART C, symbolic solve r=2..7); the sub-clusters at small positive values impose the interior conditions 1-beta < 2/(l+1) down to l = r-1, and both families are monotone in the run length, so the exact boundary condition is 1-beta < min(2/r, 2a/(r-1)). For a >= 1 one has 2a/(r-1) >= 2/(r-1) > 2/r, so the interior strata bind; the computed member a=|N-2M|+1 >= 1, beta=2 has 1-beta = -1 below every threshold (all thresholds positive), so the moment is finite at every stratum of the family at that member.
Numerical witnesses (established, numeric). Interior two-sidedness at m=3 (s=2): truncated integrals of (y1 y2 (y1+y2))^{-p} over [e^{-L},1]^2: s1 v6 PART D1 (log-grid trapz, L in [20,24]) gives slopes 0.4002 (p=0.8, predicted divergence rate 3p-2=0.4) and 0.0054 (p=0.6<2/3, predicted 0); spot v2 check 3 reproduces 0.4002/0.0054 through an entirely different nested exact 1D quadrature (inner integral by global cumulative trapz in log variables). Boundary two-sidedness at r=2: spot v2 check 4, log-grid with cell-scale diagonal cutoff: (a,beta)=(1/16, 3/4) below the threshold beta > 1-2a=7/8 diverges with slope 0.1372 against the asymptotic 0.131 of eta^{2a+beta-1}=eta^{-1/8}; (a,beta)=(1, 3/4) converges, slope -0.0009. s1 v6 PART D2: mpmath angular integral 31.292 finite at v=1/2 (exponent -1/4) with radial exponent E+1 = -1/8 < 0, divergence eta^{-1/8} (5.6 at eta=1e-6).
Literature: none imported. The result is the log-canonical-threshold-type computation for the type-A consecutive-sum product proved here by explicit Tonelli-plus-induction; no external theorem or citation is relied on or cited, and no bib entry is proposed.
