\emph{Unconditional stratum localization of the beta-family wall integrals.} For the fixed-trace Dirichlet-beta family of the fourth preceding paragraph, with profile weight \(\prod_{i}p_{i}^{a-1}\prod_{i<j}\lvert p_{i}-p_{j}\rvert^{\beta}\) on the unit-trace simplex at \(a>0\) and \(\beta\ge0\), and for the inverse-Vandermonde moment \(\int\lvert V\rvert^{-1}\,d\mu\) of the preceding paragraph, the localization step used there is established without qualification. On the ordered chamber \(p_{1}>\cdots>p_{D}>0\) the substitution \(y_{k}=p_{k}-p_{k+1}\) turns every pair difference into a sum of consecutive gaps \(\sum_{k=i}^{j-1}y_{k}\), and the coincidence walls become faces of the gap orthant: on a face where exactly a run of \(s\) consecutive gaps vanishes, exactly \(\binom{s+1}{2}\) pair factors vanish and they are comparable, up to two positive constants, to \((\sum_{k}y_{k})^{\binom{s+1}{2}}\) in the vanishing gaps, while every remaining pair factor and the Dirichlet factor \(\prod_{i}p_{i}^{a-1}\) is bounded above and below by positive constants, so transverse polar coordinates in the vanishing gaps reduce integrability on that face patch to \(\int_{0}\varrho^{\,s-1-(1-\beta)\binom{s+1}{2}}\,d\varrho\), which converges exactly for \(1-\beta<2/(s+1)\), and Fubini over the finitely many face patches gives the rest; since the depth-\(m\) strata are exactly the runs with \(s=m-1\), \(\int\lvert V\rvert^{-1}\,d\mu\) converges near every interior point of the arrangement whose deepest coincidence has depth \(m\) exactly for \(\beta>1-2/m\), diverges for \(\beta\le1-2/m\), and is bounded on compact sets off the arrangement, so all divergence is stratum-local and, over the interior chamber, finiteness reduces to the deepest stratum, \(m=D\): \(\beta>1-2/D\). In transverse polar coordinates at depth \(m\) the radial exponent is \(e=(m-1)-1-\binom{m}{2}(1-\beta)\), so \(e+1=\binom{m}{2}(\beta-(1-2/m))\) and its positivity is exactly the convergence condition, which identifies the thresholds \(0\), \(1/3\), \(1/2\) at \(m=2,3,4\) as exact for every \(m\), every chamber dimension \(D\) and every stratum. At the simplex boundary \(p=0\) the same bookkeeping, with the Dirichlet factor \(\prod_{i}p_{i}^{a-1}\) carried through the polar scaling, replaces the condition at an \(r\)-fold zero coincidence by \(1-\beta<\min(2/r,\,2a/(r-1))\): for \(a\ge1\) the interior strata are the binding ones, and the computed member \((a,\beta)=(\lvert N-2M\rvert+1,2)\) of the third preceding paragraph lies strictly above every coincidence threshold of the family. These statements are exact integrability statements for the named family weights with the Dirichlet factor carried explicitly at boundary strata; they bound finiteness only, they select no profile, orbit or state, they evaluate no matrix integral of the core action, they leave the missing steps (i)-(iv) of the Missing step paragraph unchanged, and they close nothing in Problem~G.
