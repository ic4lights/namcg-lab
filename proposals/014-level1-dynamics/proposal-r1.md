## Result

Target: the dynamics of the twelve first-harmonic fibre amplitudes `b^{nu;mu rho}` of the CP^{1,2} conjugate-coordinate class (013), toward a derived field equation. Hypothesis class `H_dyn` used throughout: Poisson limit; leaf CP^{1,2}; `X^a = T^a + A^a`, `T^a = M^{a4}`, `R = 1`; base functions `y^mu = M^{mu5}`; the quartic term of `eq:Smatrix` with `C = 0` plus a bilinear term whose coefficient is fixed by stationarity (item 1); leading symbol in `1/(|q| ell)`, frozen coefficients, sheet `y^4 > 0`, tree level; matter coupled through the fibre-averaged operator tensor (013) to a conserved source. Nothing on the critical path is derived. What is established, with its exact scope:

1. **Background and exact gauge invariance (established, Poisson-algebra identities).** `{T_a,{T^a,T^b}} = -3 T^b` (both signs `s`). So `T^a` is NOT stationary for the quartic term of `eq:Smatrix` alone; it is stationary for `S + (mu^2/2g^2) int vol X_a X^a` exactly when `mu^2 = -3/R^2`. A constant `C_{mu nu lambda}` cannot replace this term (`C_{abc} M^{bc}` is linearly independent of `M_{a4}` on the orbit). The linearised field-equation operator is `L(A)_a = -Box A_a + D_a (D.A) - 2 s J_{ab} A^b + mu^2 A_a` (`D^a = {T^a,.}`, `J_{ab} = {M_{ab},.}`), with `S2 = (1/2g^2) int vol A^a L(A)_a`, and `L({Lambda,T}) = 0` exactly for every `Lambda` iff `mu^2 = -3`. The amplitude action is therefore gauge invariant exactly, not only at the leading symbol. This answers 013's remaining item 4 ("background as a solution") in the Poisson limit: it is a solution of `eq:Smatrix` plus a fixed bilinear term, not of `eq:Smatrix` as displayed.
2. **Leading symbol (established in the class; numerically cross-checked on the realisation).** The only part of `L` with two `y`-derivatives is `-Box A_a + D_a D.A` (because `D^a y^mu = s m eta^{a mu}`, `m = M^{45}` a function of `y` alone); `J_{ab}` acts on `y` as a Lorentz rotation (first order). Hence `S2 ~ (1/4g^2) int vol m^2 F_{ab}F^{ab}` pointwise on the fibre: every fibre function is an independent Maxwell field with the same coefficient; no mixing between harmonics, no mass, at this order. For the level-1 sector (`M^{mu rho} = sum_i e^i S_i^{mu rho}`, `int dmu e^i e^j = kappa delta^{ij}`) the 12 amplitudes are **three** Maxwell fields `c_i^nu`, kinetic matrix `delta_ij (q^2 eta_ab - q_a q_b)`; the Maxwell gauge transformation is the leading form of the matrix gauge transformation and acts on `h` as a divergence-free diffeomorphism (013). No relative ghost at this order; the common overall sign is opposite to `-1/4 F^2` in `eta` (the weight convention is not fixed by `eq:Smatrix`).
3. **Order mismatch, resolved at the leading symbol (established, exact-rational; bounded no-go).** With `h = H c` (`H`: 12 to 10, linear in `q`): (a) `E H` (cubic) does not factor through the Maxwell operator `M` (quadratic): no polynomial `P(q)` with `E H = P M`; none of the ten entries of `E[h(c)]` is divisible by `q^2`. So no local equation `E[h] = P(d) J` follows from `d_a F_i^{ab} = J_i^b`. (b) Free solutions: for `q^2 != 0` pure gauge, `E[h] = 0`; for null `q` (Lorenz gauge) six physical polarisations, three inert (`h = 0`), three visible in `h` mod diffeos: two transverse-traceless (annihilated by `E`) and one with nonzero trace, for which `E[h]_{mu nu} = (1/2) q_mu q_nu h^alpha_alpha != 0`. (c) With a conserved source coupled through `h`: `h = (N/q^2)(Q T + T Q)`, `Q^{mu alpha} = sum_i s_i^mu s_i^alpha = q_perp^2 P_perp - q_perp q_perp^T`; mod diffeos `h ~ N[2(1 + q_par^2/q^2) T + yhat tau + tau yhat]`: homogeneous of degree 0 in `q`, local for `q_par = 0` (static source at a base point with `y^i = 0`). Since `E` has degree 2, `E[h]` has degree 2 and cannot be a multiple of `T`. In words: second order in `c` is zeroth order in `h` after one derivative is removed; the Einstein operator is second order in `h`.
4. **Consequence (conditional on an assumed induced term).** If a linearised Einstein-Hilbert term (degree 2 in `h`) is added, the tree-level form (degree 0) dominates at small `q`; a Newton-type response `h ~ G T/q^2` appears only above the crossover set by the ratio of the coefficients. Cancellation of the tree-level coefficient would need a loop term of the form `sum_i F_i^2`; not examined.

**What is NOT derived:** masses of the level-1 amplitudes, subleading (`1/(|q|ell)`, `1/(|q|ell)^2`) terms, mixing of harmonics, the connection induced by `S_i(y)`, `G_eff`, any Einstein-type field equation, the matter coupling from the matrix action (input), identification of `G` with the emergent metric. The answer to the question "is an Einstein-type equation for `h` derived?" is NO within `H_dyn`: the tree-level equations give an algebraic response; the only Einstein-type structure comes from the induced term, which is an input here. This no-go is bounded by `H_dyn` (see "Relaxation routes").

## Research and reasoning

**Setting.** `S = -(1/4g^2) Tr [X,X][X,X]` with `[.,.] -> i{.,.}`, `Tr -> int vol` gives `S = (1/4g^2) int vol {X^a,X^b}{X_a,X_b}` (positive coefficient). With a bilinear term `(mu^2/2g^2) int X_a X^a`, `delta S = (1/g^2) int dX^a E_a`, `E_a = -{X^b,{X_b,X_a}} + mu^2 X_a`. At `X = T`: `E_a = (3 + mu^2) T_a`. Linearisation: `L(A)_a = -{T^b,{T_b,A_a}} - {T^b,{A_b,T_a}} - {A^b,{T_b,T_a}} + mu^2 A_a`. Using `[D^a,D^b] = s J^{ab}` (Jacobi) one finds `L(A)_a = -Box A_a + D_a(D.A) - 2 s J_{ab}A^b + mu^2 A_a`; this matches the structure of the connection-sector quadratic form already in appH (`K d a d a - 2 a{Theta,a}`).

**Scripts (exact, sympy).** `check_poisson_algebra_s2.py`: polynomial Poisson algebra on the 15 variables `M^{AB}` (no orbit constraint needed), `s = +1` and `s = -1`: (1) `Box T^b = -3 T^b`; (4) `[D^a,D^b] = s J^{ab}`; (2) the closed form of `L` for random degree-2 `A`; (3) `L({Lambda,T}) = 0` for random `Lambda` iff `mu^2 = -3` (fails for `0`, `1`). Only the fibre-independent `m^2` multiplies `d d`, so the quadratic form is diagonal in the fibre function.

\paragraph{Conjugate-coordinate class: tree-level quadratic form of the level-one amplitudes (conditional; not closure).}

\emph{Background.} The Lie--Poisson relations give \(\{T_a,\{T^a,T^b\}\}=-3T^b\), an identity of the polynomial Poisson algebra of \(\mathfrak{so}(4,2)\) that does not depend on the sign \(s\). \mathcal F_{ab}=\partial_aA_b-\partial_bA_a ,
\]
with \(\partial_a\) the derivative along \(y\) at fixed fibre argument. At this order each fibre function is an independent Maxwell field with the same coefficient, and there is no coupling between fibre harmonics. \paragraph{Conjugate-coordinate class: what the tree-level equations of the level-one amplitudes imply for \(\mathcal E[h]\) (conditional; bounded no-go).}

For these solutions \(\mathcal E[h]_{\mu\nu}=\tfrac12q_\mu q_\nu h^\alpha{}_\alpha\), which vanishes on the transverse-traceless polarisations and not on the polarisation with nonzero trace; the longitudinal diffeomorphism is not a symmetry of the class, so this trace is not removed by gauge.

\emph{No local relation.} The map \(c\mapsto\mathcal E[h(c)]\) is cubic in \(q\), and the Maxwell operator \(c\mapsto q^2c^b-q^b\,q\cdot c\) is quadratic. No matrix \(P(q)\) linear in \(q\) satisfies \(\mathcal E[h(c)]=P(q)\cdot(\text{Maxwell operator on }c)\), and none of the ten components of \(\mathcal E[h(c)]\) is divisible by \(q^2\). Hence no local equation \(\mathcal E[h]=P(\partial)J\) follows from the second-order equations \(\partial_a\mathcal F_i^{ab}=J_i^b\) for a source \(J_i\).

\emph{Response to a source.} Take as input a conserved source, \(q_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0\), coupled to the amplitudes through \(h\), that is \(J_{i\nu}\propto s_i^\mu T_{\mu\nu}\) (gauge invariance of the coupling requires the conservation). Then \(c_i\) is determined up to gauge, \(c_i^\nu\propto J_i^\nu/q^2\), and
\[
h^{\mu\nu}=\frac{N}{q^2}\bigl(Q^{\mu\alpha}T_\alpha{}^\nu+Q^{\nu\alpha}T_\alpha{}^\mu\bigr),\qquad
Q^{\mu\alpha}=\sum_is_i^\mu s_i^\alpha=q_\perp^2P_\perp^{\mu\alpha}-q_\perp^\mu q_\perp^\alpha ,
\]
with \(N\) a nonzero constant fixed by the couplings, \(P_\perp\) the projector on \(y^\perp\) and \(q_\perp=P_\perp q\), both restricted to the indices \(0,\dots,3\). Modulo \(q\circ\xi\) this is \(h^{\mu\nu}\simeq N\bigl[2(1+q_\parallel^2/q^2)T^{\mu\nu}+\hat y^\mu\tau^\nu+\tau^\mu\hat y^\nu\bigr]\) with \(\hat y=y/\ell\), \(q_\parallel=q_\mu\hat y^\mu\) and \(\tau^\nu=\hat y_\alpha T^{\alpha\nu}\). The response is homogeneous of degree zero in \(q\): \(h\) is algebraic in \(T\), local for \(q_\parallel=0\), in particular for a static source at a base point with \(y^i=0\), and otherwise carries the factor \(q_\parallel^2/q^2\). Since \(\mathcal E\) is of degree two, \(\mathcal E[h]\) is homogeneous of degree two in \(q\) and is nonzero whenever \(h\) is not a linearised diffeomorphism; it therefore cannot equal a multiple of \(T\). A relation of second order in \(c\) is zeroth order in \(h\) once the common derivative is removed, whereas \(\mathcal E[h]=T\) is second order in \(h\).

\emph{Induced term.} Suppose a term \((16\pi G_{\mathrm{eff}})^{-1}\int\sqrt{-G}\,R\), reported to arise at one loop in~\cite{Steinacker2023} and not computed here, is added as an input. Its quadratic form in \(h\) has degree two in \(q\) and the tree-level form has degree zero, so the tree-level form dominates at small \(q\), and an Einstein-type response \(h\sim G_{\mathrm{eff}}T/q^2\) can appear only above the scale set by the ratio of the two coefficients. Removing the tree-level term would require a loop contribution to the coefficient of \(\sum_i\mathcal F_i^2\) that cancels it; this is not examined.

This is a statement for the Poisson limit, the stated class, the leading symbol, tree level, the level-one sector coupled to a conserved source through \(h\), and the sheet \(y^4>0\). It does not exclude an Einstein-type equation from the induced term, from subleading orders in \(1/(|q|\ell)\), from other sectors or from the entanglement route of Assumption~\ref{ass:EE}, and it derives no value or sign of \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\). This changes no ledger label; Problem~F remains open.
