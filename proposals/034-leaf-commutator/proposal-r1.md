## Research and reasoning
**Why the commutator is not a perturbation.** Y = Ham(phi), phi = q.y a coordinate function of the Lie algebra; the Jacobi identity gives [W_a, Y] = Ham({T_a, phi}) = Ham(kappa_a) = sigma q_a Ham(y^4). In the frame (W_a, Z_k), Ham(y^4) is purely horizontal on the leaf (numerically exact), so the whole vertical (weight-two) part of [W_a, Y] vanishes: y^c S_ac^k + (W_a y^k) + y^l [W_a,Z_l]^k = 0. In the nilpotent model with first-jet-frozen coefficients the charge y^k is therefore not constant: its gradient cancels the flux term y^c F_ac. Equivalently, in the nilpotent group Y is a right-invariant field, commuting with all left-invariant W_a. The model with constant c retains only y^c F_ac, a spurious flux perturbation, which breaks the leaf gauge invariance alpha -> alpha + lambda kappa (the transformation by Lambda = lambda sin(phi) is U = exp(-i eps lambda cos(phi) Y), which conjugates H(alpha) to H(alpha + lambda kappa) exactly when Y is central).

**Evaluation.** In the Plancherel picture L^2(G) = int pi_zeta (x) pi_zeta^*, left-invariant fields act on one factor and Y on the other, so the local density at the origin is tr_pi[chain . P^2] with P = y^c omega_c + c0 (c0 = y^k zeta_k) at the seam (the cyclic position between the last and first propagator). Order: -i s <C><P^2> - s^2 int_{0<u2<u1<1} <V(s u1) V(s u2) P^2>, P^2 at the far right (`engine_e.py` docstring gives the exponentials for general times: [omega_a(t), phi(t')] = -i (M(t-t') kappa)_a; BCH commutator [phi(t1), phi(t2)] = i (a2 - a1).kappa + i a1 F a2^T). Validation: 2D Landau gauge, Euclid: omega_1 = p_1, omega_2 = k2 - B x_1, K = y2 k2 is an exact c-number; the exact local density by spectral diagonalisation (periodic grid, Fourier p; a first finite-difference attempt had fermion doubling, a factor 2, found and fixed by the Maxwell check against the a_2 coefficient, 0.9% = higher orders in t kappa^2, `toy2d_maxwell.py`) matches engine_e: 0.08489561 vs 0.08491501 (c0 = 0) and 0.08260391 vs 0.08262229 (c0 = 0.7). The earlier seam prescription gave 0.0862 - 0.0226i.

**Leaf pipeline.** `leafdata.py`: frame (W_a, Z_k), S_ab^k from the exact brackets, F_ab(zeta) = -S_ab^k zeta_k, c0 = y^k zeta_k, y^c, kappa_a; `canon.py`: Lorentz transformation to F_01 = a, F_23 = b; `engine_e_mp.py`: multiprecision engine; `l3_fin.py`: ray integral (theta = 0.6) of Theta(s) times the kappa^2 coefficient, Fin = lim [int_{s0}^inf T2 ds - c ln(1/s0)]. Fin/c shifts by a constant under s -> lambda s, so the proportionality test of the tetrad part to A (best fit lambda A) is rescaling-invariant.

**Interpretation of the numbers.** c is determined by y y and is F-independent: the logarithm is A. Fin_yy is not proportional to A except where the flux is strongly electric. The charge part (c0^2) is a Maxwell-type piece, much larger in magnitude (c0 up to ~100 in the units of the sampled frame), which is the enhancement heuristic of 032 now visible in the numbers (not a derivation).

**Not done.** (a) The vector-mass term with the Zeeman-dressed resolvent at order epsilon^2: the Zeeman operator is Sigma^{ab} [Omega_a, Omega_b]/i with [Omega_a, Omega_b] = i F_ab + i eps cos(phi) Y (alpha_b kappa_a - alpha_a kappa_b) (central Y), which needs a matrix-valued Gaussian engine with P at the seam; not built. That the leading exponential rate of the Zeeman-dressed integrand (032) is not altered by the perturbation (only polynomial prefactors change) is a conjecture. (b) The weight-one terms (item 5). (c) The window average over the centre variable. (d) Quadrature: 6-point panels, 14-point double Duhamel nodes; a convergence check at ray points (12 -> 14 -> 16 nodes changes the kappa^2 coefficient at 1e-4 relative at the largest |s|, `l3_conv.out.txt`); Fin values are quoted to two digits only.
