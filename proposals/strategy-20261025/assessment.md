## 0. Bottom line

1. **Top-ranked next proposal (candidate 1 below).** On backgrounds that the joint flow leaves stationary, the rigid η-rotation that 038 and 042 identify as the obstruction is a matrix gauge transformation.
   - It is generated by a Hamiltonian ε that is quadratic in the matrix coordinates and vanishes to second order on Σ. For the cone and the constant backgrounds this is checked in `check_hamiltonian_rotation.py`; it is a lead, not a result.
   - The proposal would compute the Noether charge for backgrounds that are "stationary up to a gauge transformation". This is the analogue of a charged black hole whose potential is stationary only up to gauge.
   - The question is whether gauge covariance removes the edge-term ambiguity Y of 042, and so fixes r.
   - It is the one step that could turn the B residue into a number using only matrix data.
   - It also gives D1 its first candidate: local frame rotations realised by the matrix (symplectic) gauge group, with no imposed local Lorentz symmetry.
2. **Strategic correction.** About 12 B proposals (031–042) work in the four-coordinate Poisson class.
   - That class is kinematically rank 3: per 008 the linearised Einstein operator reaches only 3 of 6 conserved sources.
   - The B normalisation test therefore uses 2 tensor polarisations in a class that cannot carry full linearised GR.
   - The classes with rank 6 are the CP^{1,2} conjugate-coordinate class (013) and an embedded brane with D ≥ 7 matrix coordinates and a non-zero second fundamental form (009).
   - The B and F matching (an induced Einstein form, G_T, and the area coefficient G_A on the same replica) has to move into one of those classes. Candidate 2 does this.
3. **Diminishing returns.**
   - The F windowing and resummation chain in CP^{1,2} (025, 027, 028, 030, 032, 034, 040) has given eight bounded results and no finite covariant Einstein–Hilbert coefficient.
   - The B contact and edge bookkeeping at log order (029, 031, 033, 035, 036, 037) reduced to identities between vanishing coefficients at (6,2), or to a comparator choice. 042 has now shown that this comparator choice is the same edge ambiguity as at Λ².
   - Both lines should pause until the shared missing ingredients in §2 are attacked.

## 1. Problem-by-problem status

The status lines are quoted verbatim from the Appendix H ledger. The critical-path set from `sec:ch17-done` is A, B, C, D1, E, F, G-derived and N. Chapter 17 and Appendix H agree on all of them; I found no discrepancy.

### A — matrix-to-modular map
- **Status.** "Status: OPEN. Priority: CRITICAL PATH for the matrix-derived label. Completion: PROGRAMME-CONDITIONAL."
- **Established.**
  - Exact commutator-to-derivative behaviour in the constant-Θ Moyal realisation.
  - A metric-matching obstruction for a real constant Θ.
  - Through B: conditional replica gluing for a quasi-free state with rotational modular flow (019).
  - Through 042: the edge term of the boost generator is now the open datum.
- **Missing step.** An error-controlled map from (X, Φ) to the diamond modular Hamiltonian H_D, or to the mutual-information functional, defined before the metric is.
- **Dependencies.** B (replica, gluing, holonomy α_v, edge term) and G-derived (the infrared entropy density comes from the replica) need it. So do F and B wherever they use Assumption~dictionary.
- **Live avenues.**
  - Modular flow of the one-loop quasi-free state on a joint-flow-stationary background, in the spirit of Bisognano–Wichmann.
  - Testing the Dorau–Much relative-entropy flux identity against a matrix-derived H_D (SME rank 2, ledger entry 012).

### B — replica Ward identity / entanglement stationarity
- **Status.** "Status: OPEN. Priority: CRITICAL PATH. Completion: PROGRAMME-CONDITIONAL. The first-law identity is established, but the stationarity condition for the tensor block is not derived."
- **Established (four-coordinate Poisson class, Λ²).**
  - The replica area coefficient c_A equals the Theorem Geff coefficient (015).
  - G_T ≠ G_A, with c_A − c_T = 4κ_X for fixed-state gluing. In terms of n_D: 4 − n_D with antiperiodic gluing, 4 − 2n_D with periodic gluing.
  - Gauge-parameter independence at 𝔍 = 0 (017).
  - Fermion gluing (019) and the classification of contacts (021, 024, 038).
  - The Noether coefficient r is presentation dependent (038).
  - With a stipulated local dressing, r = −2 (039).
  - In Moser variables r = −1 or −4 (041).
  - Under the conditions of 042, the Iyer–Wald identity cannot select r, because the residue is an edge term of the η-boost generator.
- **Established at log order.**
  - Content (6,2) is identically zero by SUSY counting (026, 031).
  - Off (6,2) the Weyl gaps are 4 − 2n_D, 4 − n_D and 2 − n_D (033).
  - The comparator (037) and the holonomy dictionary.
- **Missing step.**
  - The closure target: derive δS_D = 0 at fixed volume for the non-conformal tensor block, from the microscopic action, without Assumption~dictionary.
  - The current sub-target is the G_T = G_A consistency. It needs the edge term or replica selected by the matrix state, or a matrix symmetry that removes the ambiguity.
- **Dependencies.**
  - On A (the state).
  - On D1 (whether the compensating rotation is gauge).
  - On F (which class carries an Einstein form at rank 6).
- **Live avenues.**
  - Candidate 1, the gauge-covariant charge.
  - The Chapter 7 T^op superpotential (candidate 3).
  - The modular-Hamiltonian edge term (candidate 4).
  - The Lewkowycz–Maldacena dynamical replica.
  - Edge modes of the symplectic gauge sector.
  - The Dirac sector.

## 2. Shared missing ingredients

1. **The class question (F, B, C, E, N).**
   - Every Presentation-A computation must happen in a class that can carry full linearised GR: rank 6, an Einstein form on all six polarisations, and a conserved source.
   - The four-coordinate Poisson class fails the rank test (008).
   - The B normalisation work (015–042) and the 041 Moser covariance live in that class. Their structural lessons (edge ambiguity, covariant variables) should transfer, but their numbers (c_T = N_tot/6 − 4κ_X on 2 polarisations) may not.
   - The candidate classes are CP^{1,2} (013/014/016, rank 6, with Maxwell level-1 dynamics) and an embedded brane in D = 10 (009, with the IKKT content (6,2) as its natural content).
   - These are not yet joined. The CP^{1,2} F line never computed B, and the B line never left four coordinates.
2. **The state, its modular Hamiltonian and its edge term (A, B, G).**
   - The replica gluing (019), the holonomy α_v (035–038), the smooth versus fixed-state replica (021, 024) and now the η-boost edge term (042) are all the same unknown: the modular Hamiltonian of the matrix state at Σ.
   - G-derived needs the same object.
3. **The compensating rotation: global or gauge (D1, B, F's ghost content).**
   - 038 found the frame dragged only up to a rigid rotation. 039 stipulated local Lorentz. 041 showed the composite connection is not a rotation connection. 042 showed that the rigidity is exactly what leaves r free.
   - The new observation is that on stationary backgrounds the rigid rotation is also a matrix gauge transformation. This has the potential to unblock B and D1 at once without importing local Lorentz symmetry, and without touching emergence: the symplectic gauge group already belongs to the matrix model.
4. **Signature (E).** This is upstream of every Lorentzian statement. The useful action now is to record E's hypothesis classes and attach one dynamical-selection probe to whichever class is chosen in item 1.

## 3. Where effort has diminishing returns

- **F in CP^{1,2}, the windows and resummations (023, 025, 027, 028, 030, 032, 034, 040).**
  - Each one moves the divergence or non-covariance to another term, and none gives a finite covariant EH coefficient.
  - 040 itself found the log coefficient y-dependent and the mass-linear term not proportional to A.
  - Recommendation: pause until a covariant regulator, or a KK/mass scale derived from the matrix action, is available.
- **B at log order and in contact bookkeeping (029, 031, 033, 035, 036, 037).**
  - At (6,2) everything vanishes identically.
  - Off (6,2) the gaps depend on a comparator or holonomy choice that 042 has shown to be an edge ambiguity.
  - Recommendation: no further contact enumeration until the edge term is fixed by a principle (candidates 1, 3 and 4).
- **More presentations of r.** After 042 these add nothing. Any further r value is another extension or edge choice.

## 4. Ranked next proposals

### Candidate 1 (top): the compensating rotation as a matrix gauge transformation, and the gauge-covariant Noether charge on Σ
- **Targets.** B (normalisation residue), D1 (the first matrix-derived local frame rotation), A-lite.
- **Concrete question.**
  - Lead (checked in this folder): on joint-flow-stationary backgrounds, ι_ξω = dε with ε quadratic, vanishing to second order on Σ. Hence ΛX = {ε, X}, so the rigid η-rotation equals the Poisson-limit matrix gauge transformation generated by ε (unitary conjugation by exp(iε̂) at finite N).
    - Checked for constant ω in span{e12, e34}, ε = −(a/2)(x1² + x2²), and for two explicit cone profiles.
    - Lorentzian and generic stationary backgrounds were not checked.
  - Question: with the matrix gauge symmetry included, the background is "stationary up to gauge", as in Einstein–Maxwell, where Φ_H δQ arises. Does the gauge-covariant Iyer–Wald/JKM construction fix the ambiguity Y(φ, L_ξX) of 042? If so, what value of r follows: −4, −2, 0 or another?
  - Second question: does the stabiliser of a point inside the symplectic gauge group supply local rotations of the frame e (a D1 seed)? Here the frame index of e is the matrix index, and gauge transformations act on X^a.
- **Why it is decisive.** It is the only route that fixes r from symmetries the matrix model already has.
  - A positive result with r = −4 closes the 4κ_X part of the B normalisation gap in this class.
  - Any other definite r turns the indeterminacy into a definite mismatch, a reproducible no-go that sends B to the state (A).
  - Either way the D1 relation is clarified.
- **Feasibility and verifiability.** High for the kinematic lemma (symbolic, exact). Medium for the charge.
  - The gauge charge of ε is a surface term ∮ε·(flux). ε and dε vanish on Σ, so its contribution on Σ may vanish identically, which is itself informative.
  - Scripts would cover: the Hamiltonian check for all stationary backgrounds in both signatures, the gauge-covariant Θ, and the charge on Σ.
- **What the outcomes mean.**
  - Positive: B's Λ² residue becomes a computed number, and D1 gets a matrix mechanism.
  - Negative (the gauge charge does not constrain Y): the edge term is purely a property of the state, and candidate 4 becomes the only route.

### Candidate 2: an Einstein form on all six polarisations in a rank-6 class (the class transfer)
- **Targets.** F (primary), B, and later C and N.
- **Concrete question.** In the embedded-brane class of 009 (D = 10 matrix coordinates, Poisson limit, IKKT content, transverse scalars with a non-zero second fundamental form K):
  - Compute the induced one-loop quadratic form on the six gauge-invariant modes at the first non-vanishing order. At (6,2) the Λ² term vanishes, so this means the K-dependent and KK-type term, or, off (6,2), the Λ² term.
  - Test whether it is Einstein, i.e. one G_T on all six polarisations.
  - Compute the replica area coefficient on the same background.
- **Why it is decisive.** Without rank 6 and an Einstein form, the B matching cannot reach full GR. This proposal joins the B and F lines in one class.
- **Feasibility.** Medium to low. The a_1 bookkeeping with transverse backgrounds is standard. The rank-6 response map exists (009). The SUSY cancellation at (6,2) forces going to the next order, so the first package should treat Λ² off (6,2) as a computational parameter, as 033 did.
- **What the outcomes mean.**
  - Positive: the first matrix class with an induced Einstein term at full rank.
  - Negative (G_T depends on polarisation): a bounded no-go that selects CP^{1,2} or a fuzzy-fibre class instead.

### Candidate 3: the Chapter 7 T^op superpotential on the δρ = 0 modes
- **Targets.** B. This was the 042 gatekeeper's rank 1.
- **Concrete question.**
  - Define T^op as Chapter 7 does: the metric response of the tensor-block action on the background that defines the diamond.
  - Compute, in the class, the edge term of its superpotential, which Eq. (Top-FP) says is "absorbed into the area term".
  - Test whether it equals −4κ_X in units of c.
- **Why it matters.** It is finite and fixes the edge term by definition. It also tests a merged Chapter 7 sentence.
- **Feasibility.** High. It is quadratic algebra on the modes of 015.
- **What the outcomes mean.**
  - Positive: G_A = G_T holds once the edge term is included, and B's normalisation is consistent at Λ² in the class.
  - Negative: the Chapter 7 absorption does not supply the gap, which may be a correction (PRIOR-CLAIM) to Chapter 7's wording.
- **Limitation.** Only 2 polarisations, in the rank-3 class (see §2.1).

### Candidate 4: the modular Hamiltonian of the one-loop state on a joint-flow-stationary background
- **Targets.** A, B, G.
- **Concrete question.**
  - For the quasi-free fluctuation state (scalars, a^a with its gauge plus η-rotation, ghosts, Dirac), show that its modular flow is the joint flow combined with the gauge transformation of candidate 1.
  - Derive the edge term at Σ, and with it the gluing and α_v.
- **Why it is decisive.** It is the common unknown behind 019, 021, 024, 035–038 and 042. It also seeds G-derived.
- **Feasibility.** Low to medium for one package. A first package could cover the free connection sector only.
- **What the outcomes mean.** A positive result fixes the replica: c_A becomes a derived number and the B residue becomes a definite statement.

### Candidate 5: a dynamical signature-selection probe
- **Targets.** E.
- **Concrete question.** In the class chosen by candidate 2, for which η, or which presentation of the leaf, does a joint-flow-stationary background exist with a fluctuation Hessian free of tachyons and ghosts? The aim is to separate the 013 Lorentzian-base class from the 022 "never Lorentzian" presentations by a dynamical criterion.
- **Why it matters.** E is upstream of everything and has had no dedicated package.
- **Feasibility.** Medium for a bounded classification. A derivation of the signature is not expected.

**Recommended order.**
1. Candidate 1 (small, sharp, and it unblocks B and D1).
2. Candidate 3 in parallel, if capacity allows (cheap, and it tests Chapter 7).
3. Then candidate 2, the strategic class transfer, with candidate 5 attached.
4. Candidate 4 once candidate 1 has fixed the symmetry content of the modular flow.

C, N and G should not be funded until F (candidate 2) and the state (candidate 4) progress. Their missing steps are downstream.
