\paragraph{Slice bridge band and modulus obstruction.} On the slice \(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^0\in\mathbb R\), \(k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^i\in\mathbb R\), the insertion is bounded on the final contour, \(|O_k|\le2\), since \(k^\mu A_\mu\) reduces on \(\mathcal R_1\) to the Hermitian form \(r^\mu\tilde A_\mu\) and \(\rho_X^2=\sum_{a=1}^{3}(r^\mu\tilde A^a_\mu)^2\ge0\), so the \(k<14\) Pfaffian window applies with the \(k=0\) majorant. With the auxiliary factor at \(\delta=\lambda\epsilon\), the quadratic damping along \(\mathcal R_\theta\), \(\theta\in[u,1]\), is strictly positive on the whole slide if and only if \(\delta>\lambda_*\epsilon\), \(\lambda_*=\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}\): the spatial coefficient stays positive for all \(\delta\ge0\), and the temporal ratio \(-\cos(3\pi\theta/4)/\cos(3\pi(2\theta-1)/8)\) is strictly increasing with supremum \(\lambda_*\) at \(\theta=1\). For each fixed \(\lambda>\lambda_*\) the damped family at \(u=1\) converges to the right-hand side by dominated convergence within the window. Removing the auxiliary factor from either undamped side in modulus fails: on the rank-one corner \(\tilde A_0=\rho H\), \(\operatorname{Tr}H^2=1\), with transverse scale \(O(\epsilon)\), \(|O_k|\ge\mathrm e^{b_u\rho/2}/(2\sqrt2)\), \(b_u=\sin(3\pi(1-u)/8)|r^0|>0\) for \(u<1\), against regulator damping \(\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)\rho^2\), so the fixed-\(\epsilon\) modulus integral on \(\mathcal R_u\) obeys \(M_u(\epsilon)\ge\mathrm e^{c_0(u)/\epsilon}/\mathrm{poly}\), \(c_0(u)=\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(16\cos(3\pi u/4))>0\) for \(u<2/3\), and diverges for \(u\ge2/3\); on the flat contour \(|O_k|\) grows like \(\mathrm e^{c(k)\rho}\) and the corresponding bound grows like \(\mathrm e^{c(k)^2/(4\epsilon)}\). The linking band \(\delta\le\mathrm{poly}\cdot\mathrm e^{-c_0(u)/\epsilon}\) is therefore incompatible, as \(\epsilon\to0^+\), with the Stokes band \(\delta\ge\lambda_*\epsilon\): within the class \(N=2\), regulator (A), \(\gamma=0\), slice momenta with \(r^0\ne0\), and linking by absolute-value estimates along the family \(\mathcal R_u\), no rate \(\delta(\epsilon)\) and no contour slide bridges the undamped slice insertion (a fixed negative mass contributes only a phase at \(u=0\)). The displayed limit identity therefore remains unresolved on the slice at \(k\ne0\) beyond such bridges; closing it requires non-modulus (oscillation-based) estimates or an independent mechanism.
