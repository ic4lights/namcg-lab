Finding: three defects. (i) The model Laplace asymptotic drops the oscillatory branch's damping: with κ=k·ρ̂=a+ib the exact branch-sum exponent is (b²−a²)/(8cε) (my `lhs_model.py`); the researcher's model kept only +b². Counterexamples: k=u+icu with 0<|c|≤1 satisfies the stated condition on every ray but the mechanism gives no growth (decay if |c|<1); in particular every nonzero spatial anti-Wick-slice k has tan|Im/Re|=tan(π/8)<1 on the relevant rays.
