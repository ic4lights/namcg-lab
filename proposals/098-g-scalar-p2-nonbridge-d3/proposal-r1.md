## Research and reasoning
Pointwise step (self-contained, no literature import). For Hermitian G with spectrum lambda, trace tau, normalized diagonal d_i = G_ii/tau, scalar P_2 = sum d_i^2:
1. tau^2 P_2 = sum_j lambda_j^2 - sum_{j!=k} |G_jk|^2 follows from Tr(G^2) = sum_jk |G_jk|^2 (established identity; checked numerically to 2e-13 level in V0). Hence P_2 <= sum p_j^2 on the orbit, equality iff G diagonal.
2. P_2 >= 1/D by Cauchy (sum d_i = 1), equality iff d uniform.
3. Upper endpoint attained at the diagonal orbit point; lower at F^dagger Lambda F with F the cyclic character table / sqrt(D) (all squared moduli 1/D gives uniform diagonal). U(D) connected, orbit connected, P_2 continuous, so the image is the whole interval [1/D, sum p_j^2]; non-degenerate iff spectrum non-scalar. Numerics: V1 (endpoint attainment, interval filling to 0.02), V1b (flat-ish density). This is the dispatched "interval of the diagonal quadratic statistic"; the route replaces the frontier lead's "Newton finite-fibre" phrasing - both are admissible under the dispatch ("whichever is actually derivable"), the interval route was chosen because it is shorter and needs no Newton input.
Measure-theoretic step (conditional-in-class derivation). The disintegration facts are inherited from the integrated 096 (conditional Haar fibre reference; eigenvalue-collision nullity) and 097 (conditional eigenbasis law with RN derivative bounded above and below by positive constants against the Haar fibre reference under every strictly positive non-constant profile; collision nullity with Vandermonde weight). If a map F_0 of p recovered P_2 a.e., disintegration gives P_2 = F_0(p) fibre-a.e.; the fibre restriction is continuous and the conditional measure charges every nonempty orbit-neighborhood, so it is fibre-constantly equal to F_0(p) everywhere; the interval is non-degenerate at almost every spectrum - contradiction. Hence P(P_2 = F_0(p)) = 0 for every map, every D >= 3, both named classes.
D = 2: not re-derived. Inherited: 095 (constant-variance subclass) plus the D = 2 instance of 097's tuple nonexistence (profile class; at D = 2 the power-sum tuple is exactly (P_2), and 097's conditional non-atomicity applies). The V4 numerics (tilted conditional mean of P_2 against the exp(-beta G_11) pushforward quadrature, band tau ~ 4, p ~ 0.65; |z| ~ 1; 10 bins all nonzero) back the profile-class D = 2 non-atomicity used in that inheritance.
Positive contrast. At D = 2, (tau, P_2) pins the diagonal multiset via {d_1, d_2} = {(1 + sqrt(2P_2 - 1))/2, (1 - sqrt(2P_2 - 1))/2} (roots of x^2 - x + (1 - P_2)/2; V5 maxerr ~ 1e-13).
