\emph{A commutator-type lift: the dictionary bridge as an exact identity.} Take constant real \(\Theta^{\mu\nu}\), Hermitian matrix symbols \(A_\mu\), and the embedding ansatz \(\hat X^\mu=x^\mu\mathbb I+\Theta^{\mu\rho}A_\rho(x)\) as an input on the matrix coordinates (a Poisson-dressing identification, not a consequence of Equation~\eqref{eq:Smatrix}), and read the connection on \(\Phi\) in the adjoint (commutator) sense rather than the left action of Equation~\eqref{eq:Dmu-Phi}: for every matrix-valued Weyl symbol \(\Phi\) the Weyl calculus then gives \([\hat X^\mu,\Phi]_\star=\mathrm i\Theta^{\mu\nu}(\partial_\nu\Phi-\mathrm i[A_\nu,\Phi]_\star)\) exactly to all orders, so Equation~\eqref{eq:dictionary} of Assumption~\ref{ass:dictionary} is, conditional on these inputs, an identity at dictionary order with quantified even star-corrections \(O(\Theta^2)\) and beyond.
The printed left action is exactly incompatible with this class: for \(D_\mu\Phi=\partial_\mu\Phi-\mathrm iA_\mu\Phi\) the \(A\)-linear mismatch of the identity is \(-\Theta^{\mu\nu}\Phi A_\nu\), a right-action product no commutator produces, and the central \(u(1)\) part of \(A\) is invisible to commutators, so no commutator-type lift reproduces Equation~\eqref{eq:Smicro} with the connection as printed; the adjoint reading is a representation choice recorded as datum, with quartic gauge coupling of \(\operatorname{Tr}([A_\mu,\Phi][A_\nu,\Phi]^\dagger)\)-type.
The quadratic content of the class is bound to \(K\)-type: the shift-symmetric lift \(-(\kappa/2)\int\mathrm d^4x\,g^{\mu\nu}\operatorname{Tr}([\hat X_\mu,\Phi]_\star[\hat X_\nu,\Phi]_\star)\), with normalization \(\kappa\) a datum (of order \(\Theta^{-2}\) against Equation~\eqref{eq:Smicro}), has kinetic tensor proportional to \(K^{\mu\nu}\) of Definition~\ref{def:matrix-coords-ext}, hence never \(g\)-type for real nonzero Lorentzian data, since \(K=c\,g\) admits no real solution with \(\Theta\neq0\), the non-proportionality recorded for \(G=K^{-1}\) under Assumption~\ref{ass:emergent-op}; for purely spatial \(\Theta\) the tensor \(K\) is degenerate, so \(\Phi\) carries no time-derivative term.
The class is exactly blind to the frame mode: writing \(\Phi=\phi_0\mathbb I+\tilde\Phi\), star-commutators with \(\phi_0\mathbb I\) collapse to \(\mathrm i\Theta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi_0\)-type terms, so every appearance of \(\phi_0\) in a commutator word carries a derivative: no quadratic term and no \(X\)-dependent potential exists at any order, in particular neither the mass nor the locking direction of Equation~\eqref{eq:V} lies in the class, the frame couples only derivatively through \(K\), and the gauge-quartic coupling vanishes at \(\Phi=v\,\mathbb I\), so no gauge-boson mass arises from the condensate.
