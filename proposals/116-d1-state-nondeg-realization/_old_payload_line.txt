\emph{State-route realization of the pointwise bound.} Write \(r(\Phi):=\min_{U}\|\Phi-vU\|\) for the operator-norm distance from \(\Phi\) to the vacuum manifold \(v\,\mathrm U(4)\) of Equation~\eqref{eq:vacuum-manifold}, the minimum running over unitary \(U\). By the polar decomposition and Weyl's singular-value inequality, \(r=\max_i|\sigma_i-v|\) exactly, the minimum attained at the polar factor, and on \(r\le v\) the function~\eqref{eq:V} obeys the sharp two-sided estimate \((\lambda/4)\,r^2(2v-r)^2\le\mathcal V\le\lambda\,r^2(2v+r)^2\), the upper bound attained at the uniform radial deviation \(\Phi=(v+r)\,\Omega\) with \(\Omega\) unitary and the lower bound at singular values \((v-r,v,v,v)\). Hence \(\mathcal V\le\varepsilon\le\lambda v^4/4\) forces \(r\le r_\varepsilon:=v-\sqrt{v^2-2\sqrt{\varepsilon/\lambda}}\), which equals \(v\) at \(\varepsilon=\lambda v^4/4\) and re-derives the threshold \(\inf\{\mathcal V:\det\Phi=0\}=\lambda v^4/4\) as the statement that defect points lie at distance at least \(v\) from the vacuum manifold, equality being attained at \((0,v,v,v)\), and it gives \(|\det e|\ge(1-r/v)^4\ge(1-r_\varepsilon/v)^4\), the last factor equal to the recorded \((1-\sqrt{4\varepsilon/\lambda}/v^2)^2\). Moreover \(\sup\{\mathcal V:r\le\delta\}=\lambda\,\delta^2(2v+\delta)^2\), attained at uniform radial deviation, and this is below the defect threshold if and only if \(\delta<\delta_*:=(\sqrt{6}-2)\,v/2\), with equality of the supremum and the threshold at \(\delta=\delta_*\); consequently every configuration field with \(\sup_x r(\Phi(x))<\delta_*\) realizes the pointwise bound \(\mathcal V<\lambda v^4/4\) everywhere, with \(|\det e|\ge(1-\delta/v)^4\). In measure form, for any field on any measure space with \(E_{\mathcal V}:=\int\mathcal V\,d\mu<\infty\), Markov's inequality and the threshold give \(\mu\{x:\det e(x)=0\}\le\mu\{\mathcal V\ge\lambda v^4/4\}\le 4E_{\mathcal V}/(\lambda v^4)\), an identity for a set of singular values \((0,v,v,v)\), and \(\mu\{|\det e|<(1-\sqrt{4\varepsilon/\lambda}/v^2)^2\}\le E_{\mathcal V}/\varepsilon\) for every \(0<\varepsilon<\lambda v^4/4\); consequently every configuration of finite potential energy, in particular every finite-potential-energy classical solution of Equation~\eqref{eq:Phi-eom} on a slice, satisfies \(\mathcal V<\lambda v^4/4\) and the non-vanishing determinant of Assumption~\ref{ass:nondeg} for \(\mu\)-almost every point, with an explicit bound on the measure of the complement.
