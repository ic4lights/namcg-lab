\paragraph{The \(\gamma\to0^{-}\) link at fixed \(u\): reduction and modulus obstructions on both sides.} Let \(I_\epsilon^{(\gamma)}[O]\) denote the regularized integral (A) with the mass \(S_\gamma=-\tfrac{\gamma}{2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}\operatorname{Tr}A_\mu A^\mu\) added, computed on \(\mathcal R_u\) with \(u\in(0,2/3)\) fixed and \(\epsilon\to0^+\) last, and \(\Delta(\epsilon,\gamma)=I_\epsilon^{(0)}[O_k]-I_\epsilon^{(\gamma)}[O_k]\) for slice momenta with \(r^0\ne0\). On \(\mathcal R_u\) the mass exponent has real part \((\gamma/2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)[\sin(3\pi u/4)\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sin(\pi u/4)\sum_{i=1}^{9}\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2]\le0\) for \(\gamma<0\), and \(|\mathrm e^{z}-1|\le|z|\) holds for \(\operatorname{Re}z\le0\); since \(|\operatorname{Tr}A_\mu A^\mu|\le\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2\) on \(\mathcal R_u\), expanding the mass exponential gives \(|\Delta(\epsilon,\gamma)|\le(|\gamma|/2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\,\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\), where \(\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\) is the \(\gamma=0\) modulus functional on \(\mathcal R_u\) with insertion \(\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2\). Along the rank-one ray with transverse scale \(O(\epsilon)\), the bound \(|O_k|\ge\mathrm e^{b_u\rho/2}/(2\sqrt2)\), \(b_u=\sin(3\pi(1-u)/8)|r^0|\), against the regulator damping \(\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)\rho^2\) gives \(\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\ge\mathrm e^{c_0(u)/\epsilon}/\mathrm{poly}\) with \(c_0(u)=\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(16\cos(3\pi u/4))>0\): \(\Delta\to0\) as \(\gamma\to0^-\) at each fixed \(\epsilon\), but no absolute-value estimate for \(\Delta\) is \(o(1)\) uniformly on any ladder \(\epsilon\to0^+\), so at fixed \(u\in(0,2/3)\) the \(\gamma\to0^{-}\) link by modulus bounds is obstructed by the same flat-direction divergence that obstructs the undamped slice left-hand side, and any bridge must control the \(\gamma=0\) slice integral by signed (oscillation-based) estimates uniform in \(\epsilon\). On the mass side the soft-mode scale is \(\epsilon\)-uniform but not \(\gamma\)-uniform: maximizing \(b_u\rho/2-[\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)+|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)]\rho^2\) gives \(b_u^2/(16\beta(\epsilon,\gamma))\) with \(\beta(\epsilon,\gamma)\ge|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\), and the ray region contributes at least \(\mathrm e^{\Gamma_0(u)/|\gamma|}/\mathrm{poly}\) with \(\Gamma_0(u)=g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(8\sin(3\pi u/4))>0\) for every \(u\in(0,2/3)\), strictly decreasing in \(u\) (\(\Gamma_0(1/3)\approx0.0884{(r^0)}^2\) at \(g_{\mathrm{YM}}=1\)): the mass-side modulus also blows up super-polynomially as \(\gamma\to0^-\), most strongly at small \(u\), so the two sides of the link are not simultaneously controllable in modulus. With the mass Gaussian the extension of the displayed limit identity at fixed \(\gamma<0\) to \(O_k\) for every \(k\in\mathbb C^{10}\) is a derivation: for fixed \(u\in(0,2/3)\) the damping of each Hermitian component is bounded below by \(|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\) on \(\tilde A_0\) and \(|\gamma|\sin(\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\) on \(\tilde A_i\), independent of \(\epsilon\), so the Stokes boundary term on balls times \([0,u]\) decays and both sides of the limit are dominated. In the one-dimensional branch model the coefficient of \(\tilde A_0\) in \(k^\mu A_\mu\) on \(\mathcal R_u\) at pure temporal slice momentum is \(\mathrm e^{3\pi\mathrm i(1-u)/8}r^0/2\), not its value at \(u=0\); with this contour-dependent coefficient the soft-mode growth exponent at \(\gamma=0\) is \({(r^0)}^2/(16\sqrt2\,\epsilon)\) and the fixed-\(\gamma\) saturation exponent is \(-g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2{(r^0)}^2/(8\sqrt2|\gamma|)\), both independent of \(u\), so the saturation factor \(\mathrm e^{{(r^0)}^2\sin(3\pi u/4-\pi/4)/(8|\gamma|)}\) and its growth as \(\gamma\to0^-\) for \(3\pi u/4-\pi/4>0\) are properties of the frozen-coefficient model, not of the contour family.
