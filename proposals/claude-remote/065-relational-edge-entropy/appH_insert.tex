\paragraph{Edge entropy of a matrix block on a compact fuzzy factor (conditional; bounded no-go).}
Let \(X^a\), \(a=1,\dots,d\), be Hermitian \(N\times N\) matrices of a compact fuzzy factor with the positive contraction \(\delta_{ab}\) of its directions, let \(\Theta\) be an orthogonal projector of rank \(M\), \(\bar\Theta=1-\Theta\), and \(X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}=\Theta X^a\bar\Theta\). In the matrix quantum mechanics of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} the subsystem attached to \(\Theta\) is the block \(\Theta X^a\Theta\) of the matrix configuration space, made invariant under \(U(M)\times U(N-M)\) and averaged over a subgroup of \(U(N)\) that moves the block. At leading semiclassical order its entropy is \(\log d_\mu\), with \(\mu\) the irreducible representation of \(U(M)\) carried by the block (the edge modes), minimised over the frames that the average retains. For the fuzzy sphere \(X^a=\nu J^a\), with the state cut off at angular momentum \(l_c\), the minimum is attained on a cap. That construction uses the configuration space, the gauge action and a state; the Hamiltonian enters only through the state.

\emph{Edge datum.} For every configuration and projector, exactly,
\[
\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}X^a_{\bar\Sigma\Sigma}\bigr)
=\tfrac12\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}\bigl([X^a,\Theta]^\dagger[X^a,\Theta]\bigr)
=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\Theta\,\Box\,\Theta\bigr),
\]
with \(\Box=\sum_a[X^a,[X^a,\cdot\,]]\), so the squared norm of the classical datum of the edge charge is the quadratic form of the projector in the kinetic operator of the fluctuations of the factor, with the contraction of the action. For \(X^a=A^a{}_bJ^b\) on the \(N\)-dimensional irreducible representation of \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\), with \(A\) real and invertible, and \(\Theta\) the spectral projector of \(n\cdot J\) on its \(M\) largest eigenvalues, every \(X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}\) is a multiple \(c^a\) of one matrix unit \(|u)(v|\), with \(u,v\) the eigenvectors of \(n\cdot J\) on either side of the edge, and
\[
s^2\equiv\sum_a\bigl\|X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}\bigr\|^2=\tfrac14M(N-M)\bigl(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2\bigr),
\]
with \(\sigma_{1,2}\) the singular values of \(A\) on the plane orthogonal to \(n\) (exact). In the Poisson limit, with \(x^a\) the symbol of \(X^a\) and \(M(N-M)\to(\tfrac12N\sin\theta)^2\), the boundary of the cap is mapped to an ellipse in target space, \(s^2=(4\pi)^{-1}\oint|\partial_\phi x|^2\,\mathrm d\phi\) with \(\phi\) the angle of the flow generated by the symbol of \(n\cdot J\), and the perimeter \(P\) of the ellipse satisfies \(P\le2\sqrt2\,\pi s\), with equality only for \(\sigma_1=\sigma_2\). So \(s\) is proportional to the length of the boundary in the induced tensor, defined as in Definition~\ref{def:matrix-coords-ext} for a two-dimensional leaf, only where the speed along that flow is constant. On such a leaf the inverse of the kinetic tensor of that definition is conformal to the induced tensor, and on the round fuzzy sphere both are constant multiples of the round metric, whose area form is a constant multiple of the symplectic form, so the example of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} does not distinguish them.

\emph{Row length.} If \(X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}=c^a|u)(v|\), the classicalised charge of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} (its Equation~(4.4)), \(Q=-2\mathrm i\sum_a(X^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}\Pi^a_{\bar\Sigma\Sigma}-\Pi^a_{\Sigma\bar\Sigma}X^a_{\bar\Sigma\Sigma})\) with Hermitian momentum matrices \(\Pi^a\), equals \(-2\mathrm i(|u)(w|-|w)(u|)\) with \((w|=\sum_ac^a(v|\Pi^a\) restricted to the block, and its nonzero eigenvalues are \(-2\operatorname{Im}\alpha\pm2(\operatorname{Im}^2\alpha+|w_\perp|^2)^{1/2}\), with \(\alpha=(u|w)\) and \(w_\perp=w-\alpha u\) (exact). For the two caps of one axis \(n\), the spectral projectors of \(n\cdot J\) on its \(M_1\) largest and its \(M_2\) smallest eigenvalues, with edges farther apart than twice the band width of \(\Pi^a\) in the eigenbasis of \(n\cdot J\), the nonzero spectrum is the union of the two single-cap pairs (exact). Take a Gaussian state in which the \(\Pi^a\) are independent and each has variance \(f(l)\) on the spin-\(l\) matrices for \(l\le l_c\) and vanishes above. Then every full row sum of \(\langle|\Pi_{ij}|^2\rangle\) equals \(N^{-1}\sum_{l\le l_c}(2l+1)f(l)\) (exact), and, with \(\operatorname{Im}\alpha\) neglected, the row length \(\ell=2|w_\perp|\) of a cap of the round sphere obeys \(\langle\ell^2\rangle=4s^2W\), with \(W\) the sum over the boundary row inside the block. For \(f=\nu l/2\), the variance of the Gaussian state with frequencies \(\nu l\), the ratio of \(\langle\ell^2\rangle\) to \(\nu^3l_c^3M(N-M)/3N\) is \(1.021\) at \(N=400\), \(M=N/2\), \(l_c=40\), against \(1.041\) at \(l_c=20\) (numerical); this is the row length of Equation~(3.14) of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} at leading order in \(l_c\). For \(f=l(l+1)/4\) the local exponent of \(W\) in \(l_c\) is \(3.93\) there and \(16NW/l_c^4=1.07\) (numerical), against \(2.98\) for \(f=\nu l/2\). So \(\langle\ell^2\rangle=4s^2W\) for every such \(f\), with \(W\) depending on \(f\), on \(l_c\) and, weakly at large \(l_c\), on \(M\) (numerical).

\emph{State and coarse-graining.} The matrix integral defines the configuration space and the gauge action, so the space of square-integrable functions of the Hermitian configurations and the factorisation map of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} exist for it, but it supplies no state on that space. The ground state of a Hamiltonian \(\tfrac12\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}(\Pi^a)^2+S\) needs an evolution parameter that the integral does not contain. Where the weight is positive and integrable, as for the Euclidean contraction on a compact factor, \(e^{-S/2}\), on the fluctuations transverse to the gauge orbits, is a state whose modulus squared is the weight; for a Gaussian weight \(e^{-\frac12\sum_l\kappa_lx_l^2}\) it has \(f=\kappa_l/4\), whereas the ground state with frequencies \(\kappa_l^{1/2}\) has \(f=\kappa_l^{1/2}/2\), and for \(\kappa_l\propto l(l+1)\) the two give different powers of \(l_c\) in \(\ell\). For the Lorentzian weight with a convergence regulator the weight is not positive, and neither construction applies as stated. The state, \(l_c\) and the subgroup of the frame average are therefore supplied inputs; none is fixed by the integral here.

\emph{Bearing on Assumption~\ref{ass:Sent-density}.} (i) The block is a region of the factor, so this is the entropy of a region of the factor, not of the emergent spacetime, and its edge is not the boundary \(\partial M\) of Definition~\ref{def:HBEV}. (ii) For a fixed polynomial diagram \(\mu\) the hook-content formula gives \(\mathrm d\log\dim_{U(M)}\mu/\mathrm d\log M=\sum_{(r,c)\in\mu}M/(M+c-r)\to|\mu|\) as \(M\to\infty\) (exact), so \(\log d_\mu\) contains \(|\mu|\log M\) and depends, at fixed boundary data, on the rank of the block; for the flat diagram this is the factor \(\log(eM/\ell)\) of \(2\ell\log(eM/\ell)\) in~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025}. For the two caps of one axis above, at that fixed frame, with rows \(\ell_1,\ell_2\) of one diagram of \(U(M_1+M_2)\), the value of \(\log d_\mu\) for a one-row diagram per cap exceeds the sum of the single-cap values by about \(\ell_1\log\frac{M_1+M_2}{M_1}+\ell_2\log\frac{M_1+M_2}{M_2}\), up to a correction of the order of a logarithm (numerical); the two-sided diagrams of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} are not computed. The cause is that the block of the union contains the blocks between the caps. So the quantity that is minimised over frames is not additive over components; this is a statement at fixed frame, not about the minimised entropy of a block of rank \(M_1+M_2\). The integral over \(\partial M\) of a local density in \(h_{ij}\) and \(\phi\) is additive over far-separated components and depends on \(\partial M\) only through those data; a counterterm built from \(h_{ij}\) alone can remove at most a part of \(|\mu|\log M\) that is fixed by them, such as one set by \(N\) at fixed diagram, and is not shown to remove its dependence on the rank. At this order, and with the leading-order entropy of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025}, the functional \(\log d_\mu\) therefore does not have the form of Assumption~\ref{ass:Sent-density} (conditional). (iii) The length that enters is \(s\), a functional of the configuration, of the contraction of the action and of the Poisson structure. It is not identified with a length in the metric of Equation~\eqref{eq:g-from-e}, whose relation to the matrix configuration rests on Assumption~\ref{ass:dictionary} and Problem~D.

\emph{Vacuum term.} The abstract of~\cite{Steinacker2023} states that the vacuum energy there does not act as a cosmological constant but stabilises the brane structure at one loop. Its relation to the sector sum of Equation~\eqref{eq:rho-debt} and to Equation~\eqref{eq:Leff} is not examined.

These statements are for a compact factor with positive contraction, a single projector at fixed frame for the deformed sphere and the two caps, the leading semiclassical order with one dominant representation, and Gaussian states with the stated variances; the frame average and the minimisation of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} are used for its state and not re-derived. They derive no entropy density on \(\partial M\), no value or scaling of \(S_{\mathrm{ent}}^{\mathrm{(IR)}}\), no boundary stationarity and no \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}\); Assumptions~\ref{ass:Sent-density} and~\ref{ass:bdy-EE} are not discharged, and Problem~G remains open and PROGRAMME-CONDITIONAL.
