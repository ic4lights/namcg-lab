## Result

The package tests route R2 of `reports/sme-scout-20261108-after60.md`. The route is the relational matrix-block entropy of Fliss–Frenkel–Hartnoll–Soni (arXiv:2408.05274, SciPost Phys. 18, 171 (2025)), proposed as the first half of G-derived: a boundary entropy density derived from the matrix theory. I read the paper's arXiv v2 (journal) source for Sections 1–3, 4.1–4.2 and the opening of Section 5.

**Bounded no-go.** On a compact fuzzy factor this route gives a matrix-derived boundary law. It does not give the entropy density of Assumption `ass:Sent-density`. Three independent mismatches block it, and two inputs remain supplied.

**Established (exact, finite N).**
1. **Edge datum = kinetic form of the projector.** For any Hermitian X^a, any projector Θ and any contraction G_ab,
   Σ G_ab Tr(X^a_{ΣΣ̄} X^b_{Σ̄Σ}) = ½ Σ G_ab Tr([X^a,Θ]†[X^b,Θ]) = ½ Tr(Θ □_G Θ).
   So the squared norm of the classical datum that sets the edge irrep is fixed by the action's own kinetic operator and contraction. No metric is introduced.
2. **Deformed fuzzy sphere, X^a = A^a_b J^b, cap Θ (spectral projector of n·J).**
   - Every X^a_{ΣΣ̄} lies on one matrix unit |u)(v|.
   - s² := Σ_a ‖X^a_{ΣΣ̄}‖² = ¼ M(N−M)(σ₁²+σ₂²), where σ_i are the singular values of A on n^⊥.
   - In the Poisson limit s² = (4π)⁻¹ ∮ |∂_φ x|² dφ, with x^a the symbol of X^a and φ the rotation angle about n (the flow of the symbol of n·J). The target-space perimeter obeys P ≤ 2√2 π s, with equality only if σ₁ = σ₂.
   - So the "area" of 2408.05274 is the target-space (induced) length only on the round sphere. There the induced tensor, the inverse kinetic tensor and the round metric are all proportional, so the paper's example cannot tell them apart.
3. **Rank-one charge.** The classicalised edge charge (2408.05274 Eq. (4.4)) is Q = −2i(|u)(w| − |w)(u|).
   - Its spectrum is −2 Im α ± 2(Im²α + |w_⊥|²)^{1/2}.
   - For the two coaxial caps of one axis n (the M₁ largest and M₂ smallest eigenvalues of n·J), with edges separated by more than twice the band width of the momenta in that eigenbasis, the spectrum is the union of the two single-cap pairs.
4. **Rank dependence.** For a fixed polynomial Young diagram μ, d log dim_{U(M)} μ / d log M = Σ_boxes M/(M+c−r) → |μ|. So log d_μ contains |μ| log M and depends on the rank (the "volume") of the block at fixed boundary data. It is also not additive over two separated caps.

**Numerical.**
- In a Gaussian scalar-proxy state cut off at l ≤ l_c, the row length obeys ℓ ∝ s.
- For the variance ν l/2 (the paper's state type) the coefficient reproduces the paper's ℓ² = ν³ l_c³ M(N−M)/(3N) at leading order in l_c: ratio 1.021 at l_c = 40 and 1.041 at l_c = 20.
- For the square-root-of-weight state (variance κ_l/4) the power of l_c changes: local exponent 3.93 against 2.98.

**Consequences.**
- **(a) The state is not supplied by NAMCG.** NAMCG's integral supplies the configuration space and the gauge action, not a state. The paper's ground state needs an external evolution parameter. That is excluded here, because spacetime stays emergent. The within-core option, e^{−S/2} on a positive (Euclidean, compact-factor) weight, gives a different coefficient. The state, the coarse-graining scale l_c and the subgroup of the frame average are therefore supplied inputs.
- **(b) Not a density on ∂M.** The block is a region of the internal factor, not of emergent spacetime.
- **(c) Not local (conditional, fixed frame).** At fixed frame the functional log d_μ that is minimised over frames depends on the rank and is not additive over two coaxial caps, so it is not of the local form α₀ L³ + α₁ L R^{(h)} + … of `ass:Sent-density`. A counterterm built from h_ij can remove at most a part of |μ| log M fixed by the boundary data (for example the part set by N at fixed diagram); removal of the rank dependence is not shown. This is a statement about the fixed-frame functional, not about the minimised entropy of a block of rank M₁+M₂.
- **(d) No map to the composite metric.** The length s is not identified with a length of the composite metric g_{μν} of eq:g-from-e. That map runs through Assumption `ass:dictionary` and Problem D, and it is missing.

### 3. Lemma 1 (edge datum; established)

Write Y = [X,Θ]. It is anti-Hermitian, Y† = −Y.
- ΘXΘ̄ = ΘX − ΘXΘ = −ΘY. Hence Tr(X_{ΣΣ̄}X_{Σ̄Σ}) = Tr(ΘYY†Θ)·… Directly: Tr(ΘXΘ̄XΘ) = Tr(ΘX²) − Tr(ΘXΘX) = ½ Tr(Y†Y). Expanding −Tr(Y²) = 2Tr(ΘX²) − 2Tr(XΘXΘ) gives the identity.
- Tr(Θ[X,Y]) = Tr(Y(ΘX − XΘ)) = −Tr(Y²) = Tr(Y†Y).
- With G_ab the identity is bilinear.

Script s1 checks the identity on random data at N ≤ 40, d ≤ 6 (max relative error 4e−16).

Meaning. The off-diagonal block, which seeds the edge irrep, is the Dirichlet form of the projector in □ = Σ[X^a,[X^a,·]]. □ is the kinetic operator of the factor's fluctuations in the quadratic commutator term of eq:Smatrix. No metric beyond the contraction of matrix indices enters, so emergence is preserved.

### 4. Lemma 2 (deformed sphere; established, plus a Poisson-limit corollary)

1. Let U implement the rotation R with R e₃ = n in the spin-(N−1)/2 representation. Then U†(n·J)U = J³ and U†J^bU = R^b_c J^c. In the rotated basis X^a = (AR)^a_c J^c.
2. J¹ and J² are supported on the first off-diagonals. Their ΣΣ̄ block is the single entry (M−1, M), with values √(M(N−M))/2 and −i√(M(N−M))/2. The J³ block vanishes.
3. Hence X^a_{ΣΣ̄} = ½√(M(N−M))((AR)^a₁ − i(AR)^a₂)|u)(v|, and s² = ¼M(N−M)(‖ARe₁‖² + ‖ARe₂‖²) = ¼M(N−M) tr(A P_n Aᵀ).
4. Script s2 checks this at N ≤ 80 with random A and n (relative error 5e−15; off-support entries ≤ 1e−14).

Poisson limit:
- x = (N/2)Aω. The boundary circle is ω = cosθ n + sinθ(cosφ e₁ + sinφ e₂). Its image is an ellipse with semi-axes (N/2) sinθ σ_i.
- (4π)⁻¹∮|∂_φx|² dφ = (N sinθ/2)²(σ₁²+σ₂²)/4 = s², using M(N−M) → (N sinθ/2)², the paper's Moyal identification M = N sin²(θ/2).
- φ is the flow angle of n·ω for the round Poisson structure, i.e. the action-angle variable.
- Cauchy–Schwarz gives P = ∮|∂_φx| dφ ≤ (2π∮|∂_φx|²)^{1/2} = 2√2 π s, with equality iff |∂_φx| is constant, iff σ₁ = σ₂.
- Script s2 tabulates 2√2πs/P = 1, 1.0007, 1.025, 1.078, 1.111 for σ₂/σ₁ = 1, 0.9, 0.5, 0.2, 0.005.

So s is a "symplectic Dirichlet energy" of the boundary embedding. It is a length in the induced tensor g = η∂x∂x only when the target speed along the rotation angle about n (the flow of the symbol of n·J) is constant. In two dimensions K^{μν} = θ^{αμ}g_{αβ}θ^{βν} = θ² det g · g^{μν}, so K⁻¹ = g/(θ² det g) is conformal to g. A length in K⁻¹ is a length in g weighted by a non-constant conformal factor, which is again not s in general.

The paper's round sphere cannot discriminate, since all invariant metrics are proportional there. Off-saddle deformations do, and they show that the paper's "area measured in target space" is a round-sphere coincidence.

Caveat: the ellipsoid X = AJ is a configuration, not a stationary point of the NAMCG action. The statement is kinematic, about the classical edge datum. The state's response to the deformation is not computed.

### 5. Lemma 3 (rank-one charge; established)

1. With X^a_{ΣΣ̄} = c^a|u)(v|: X^a_{ΣΣ̄}Π^a_{Σ̄Σ} = |u)(w|, with (w|_j = Σ_a c^a(v|Π^a|j), j ∈ Σ.
2. Hermiticity of Π^a gives Π^a_{ΣΣ̄}X^a_{Σ̄Σ} = |w)(u|. So Q = −2i(|u)(w| − |w)(u|).
3. Decompose w = αu + β ŵ, with ŵ ⊥ u and β = |w_⊥|.
4. On span{u, ŵ}, Q = [[−4 Im α, −2iβ], [2iβ, 0]]. Its eigenvalues are −2Im α ± 2(Im²α + β²)^{1/2}, and Q vanishes on the complement.

Script s3 checks this exactly (max deviation 8e−16). It also checks, for the two coaxial caps at the poles of one axis with band-limited momenta in the n·J eigenbasis (|i−j| ≤ b), that the nonzero spectrum is the union of the single-cap pairs (2e−16).

α is one entry among the ~l_c entries of the row, so Im α is subleading. This gives the paper's classicalised row length ℓ = 2|w_⊥|.

### 6. Gaussian states: the boundary law holds; its coefficient is an input (numerical, scalar proxy)

**Proxy.** Π^a are independent Hermitian Gaussians with variance f(l) on spin-l matrices for l ≤ l_c. This is a proxy for the vector normal modes of the paper, which mix the three components.

**Schur.** Σ_k T_k T_k† over an orthonormal basis of the spin-l subspace commutes with the SU(2) action on C^N. Its trace is 2l+1, so it equals (2l+1)/N · 1. Hence each full row sum of ⟨|Π_ij|²⟩ is N⁻¹Σ(2l+1)f(l). Script s4 confirms this to 1e−13.

**Row length.** ⟨ℓ²⟩ = 4s²W, where W = Σ_{j<M, j≠M−1} ⟨|Π_{Mj}|²⟩ is the one-sided boundary-row sum.

**(i) f = νl/2**, i.e. ⟨π²⟩ = ν|ω|/2 with |ω_l| ≈ l, the paper's state:
- With ν = 1, ⟨ℓ²⟩/[ν³l_c³M(N−M)/3N] = 6NW/l_c³.
- At N = 400, M = N/2 the ratio is 1.041, 1.035, 1.030, 1.026, 1.023, 1.021 for l_c = 20, 24, …, 40.
- Local exponent 2.96 → 2.98. The latitude spread is 0.97–1.05 at l_c = 16.
- This reproduces the paper's ℓ² = ν³Λ³M(N−M)/(3N) (sympy, s5(a)) at leading order in l_c, with O(1/l_c) corrections. It is an independent cross-check of their Eq. (3.14) within the proxy: s² = ν²M(N−M)/2 and W → l_c³/(6N).

**(ii) f = l(l+1)/4**, the square-root-of-weight state for κ_l = l(l+1):
- W/(l_c⁴/16N) = 1.141 → 1.069 over l_c = 20 → 40.
- Local exponent 3.88 → 3.93.

The law ℓ ∝ s, i.e. the boundary law, holds for both states. The coefficient differs by a power of l_c. A precise prefactor for the paper's vector modes is not computed; I import it from the paper.

### 8. Functional form against `ass:Sent-density` (established representation theory, plus a conditional extension)

**(ii) Rank dependence.**
- Hook-content: dim_{U(M)}μ = Π_{boxes}(M+c−r)/hook(r,c). s5(b) checks it against the Weyl formula.
- So d log dim/d log M = Σ M/(M+c−r) → |μ|. s5(c) gives 0.9998|μ| at M = 10⁵.

**Non-additivity (conditional on the irrep formula for a two-row diagram).**
- The two coaxial caps of one axis, at fixed frame, give two rows ℓ₁, ℓ₂ of one diagram of U(M₁+M₂), by Lemma 3 for coaxial caps.
- log dim exceeds the sum of the single-cap values by ≈ ℓ₁ log((M₁+M₂)/M₁) + ℓ₂ log((M₁+M₂)/M₂), one-sided, minus a hook correction of order log. s5(d): differences 37.5, 33.5, 51.5 against estimates 38.9, 34.7, 52.7. The paper's two-sided diagrams are not computed; the r1 statement that the factor is 2 is withdrawn.
- The cause is structural: the block of the union contains the inter-cap blocks Σ₁Σ₂. The union's subsystem is not the tensor product of the components' subsystems, so this is not a violation of subadditivity of a fixed tensor factorisation.

**Consequence.**
- The integral over ∂M of any local density in (h_ij, φ) is additive over far-separated components and depends on ∂M only through those data. The fixed-frame functional log d_μ has neither property. This is not a statement about the minimised entropy min_V log d_{μ_V} of a block of rank M₁+M₂, whose minimum selects a minimal-boundary region rather than two separated caps.
- A counterterm built from h_ij alone can remove at most a part of |μ| log M that is fixed by the boundary data. An example is the part set by N at fixed diagram: log M = log N + log(M/N), and that part acts like a cutoff-dependent coefficient. That such a counterterm cannot remove the rank dependence is not shown.
- At this order, with the leading-order entropy of 2408.05274, the functional log d_μ therefore does not have the form eq:Sent-exp (conditional).
- This is a precise obstruction within the hypotheses of the construction: fixed-volume subsystem, single dominant irrep, a flat or few-row diagram.
