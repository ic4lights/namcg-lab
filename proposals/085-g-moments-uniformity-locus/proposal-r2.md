## Result
Within the named coupled proper-complex-Gaussian slot law of the integrated 084
paragraph, the three profile conditions generated by the existing G-block
paragraphs - decoupling of the induced pair (a,r) at a Haar-typical frame,
attainment of the sharp fixed-D profile minimum of 082, and vanishing of the
frame-averaged induced coupling - have exactly one common profile, the uniform
profile g_i = W/D. The frame average of the 084 coupling functional is
computed exactly: E_u Gamma = (D*sum g_i^2 - W^2)/(D(D+1)) =
W^2(D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1), so the frame-averaged coupled Tr Q^4 excess is
128 s^4 W^2 (D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1), a function of the profile through the 081
effective dimension alone, vanishing if and only if the profile is uniform. On a
frame-support constancy slice, sum g_i^2 is at least W^2/D with equality only at
the uniform profile, reaches W^2/min(m,D-m) on the equal-complement subfamily
and W^2 overall, so a single frame of smaller support does not enforce
uniformity. Negative datum against identification of the locus with the
integrated recovery mechanism: the integrated fuzzy variance proxies f = nu*l/2
(081's quoted profiles) have strictly increasing profiles, hence D_eff < D at
every cutoff, and remain off the uniformity locus while reaching
D_eff -> infinity through growth of the slot count; at fixed total weight W
their frame-averaged coupling vanishes like W^2/(8D^2), so averaged decoupling
does not imply uniformity at fixed D - while at the fixed-nu normalization of
the integrated convention it instead grows like nu^2 L^2/72 (s6, exact
rationals; the distinction is the F1 correction). None of this fixes the state
that sets W, g, s or u; Problem G's missing steps (i)-(iv) are untouched and G
stays open.

## Research and reasoning
Setup (all inside the 084 named class): slots w_i independent proper CN with
E w_i w_j* = s^2 g_i delta_ij, unit frame u, mu = sum g_i|u_i|^2,
Gamma(u) = sum |u_i|^2 (g_i - mu)^2, a = Im(u|w), r = |w|^2 - |(u|w)|^2.
Items 1-4 and 6 below are unchanged from round 1 (the gatekeeper independently
re-derived and confirmed all of them; no finding touched their mathematics);
item 5 is rewritten per F1/F5 and a new item 7 records the claim-7 withdrawal.

1. Locus of decoupling (corollary of the 084 iff, established algebra).
084: a independent of r iff Gamma(u) = 0 iff g constant on supp(u). Writing
w_i = |u_i|^2: Gamma(u) = sum w_i g_i^2 - mu^2. Consequences: (i) a frame with
m >= 2 nonzero entries forces equality of the two entries on its support (D=2
closed form Gamma = w_1 w_2 (g_1-g_2)^2, s1); (ii) a full-support frame forces
g_i = mu for all i, i.e. the uniform profile; Haar-a.e. frame is full-support,
so decoupling at a Haar-typical frame is equivalent to uniformity; (iii) a
frame with m <= D-1 entries constrains g only on its support.
2. Constancy-slice bounds (profile algebra, established). On {g constant on S,
|S| = m}, sum g_i^2 >= W^2/D by Cauchy with equality only at the uniform
profile; within the equal-complement two-block family g = (a^m, b^(D-m)),
b = (W-ma)/(D-m), the sum of squares is a convex quadratic in a with minimum
W^2/D at a = W/D and endpoint maxima W^2/m and W^2/(D-m), so the subfamily
reaches W^2/min(m,D-m); overall the slice contains the single-row profile
(m <= D-1) so the range of rho_2 - 1 = sum g_i^2/W^2 runs up to 1 (s1, s3).
3. Frame-averaged coupling (identity, established in class). With w = |u|^2
Haar (= Dirichlet(1,...,1)): E w_i = 1/D, E w_i w_j = (1+delta_ij)/(D(D+1));
hence E_u Gamma = (D sum g_i^2 - W^2)/(D(D+1)) = W^2(D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1),
081's D_eff = (sum g)^2/sum g_i^2. The identity uses only the frame measure,
not the slot law beyond 084's Gamma definition. Vanishing iff uniform; strictly
increasing in D_eff. By 084's excess identity the frame-averaged coupled Tr Q^4
excess is 128 s^4 times that. Exact Dirichlet integration D=2,3 and 400k-sample
Monte Carlo D=5,8 (s2); full-channel Monte Carlo D=4 confirming the pooled
covariance with the correct frame-level correction -Var_u(mu)/2 at |z| = 0.01
(s4).
4. Common locus and conditional selection reading. The uniform profile attains
all three requirements at once, and (1)+(2)+(3) show it is the unique profile
that can do so at fixed D. Reading this as selection needs an extra premise:
that the state-selection side of the matrix model drives the induced law to
decoupling at a Haar-typical frame at fixed D and W. Nothing in the repository
supplies such a premise (no selection dynamics is derived for G anywhere in
appH; the 081 paragraph keeps W and g as inputs), so the selection principle is
recorded as conditional mechanism, not derivation.
5. Negative datum on the proxies (established profile algebra + exact rational
numerics, s5/s6; revised per F1/F3/F5). Proxy convention (F5): boundary-row
slots with (2l+1) copies of f(l) = nu*l/2, 1 <= l <= l_c, so the number of
nonzero-variance slots is sum_{l=1..l_c}(2l+1) = (l_c+1)^2 - 1; the value
D = (l_c+1)^2 quoted in r1 adds one l=0 zero-variance slot, which is
D_eff-invariant and leaves W and sum g_i^2 unchanged (s5's D = 1681, 25921
include it; s6 prints both conventions and the difference in E_u Gamma at
l_c = 40 is 23.3264 vs 23.2332, a 0.4% convention effect that leaves every
qualitative statement intact). The profiles are strictly increasing, hence
D_eff < D strictly at every cutoff: they are never on the uniformity locus

6.7e-4 and 4.3e-5 (F3-corrected figures; these are exactly the numerals now
