For these observables, this order of limits, and \(\gamma\le0\) or \(\gamma\) as above, the regulated integral therefore carries at finite \(N\), conditionally for \(N\ge3\), no datum beyond the Euclidean model, with any mass term on \(\mathcal R_1\), and its phases. This is no statement about \(N\to\infty\) taken first, since the constants \(C_{O,N}\) are not shown to be uniform in \(N\); about fixed \(\gamma>0\); about degree \(14\) and above; or about non-polynomial observables such as \(\operatorname{Tr}\mathrm e^{\mathrm ik^\mu A_\mu}\), which grow on \(\mathcal R_u\), so that the domination above fails for them. It does not exclude a real-time reading obtained by continuation. It does not derive a signature, a background, a frame or the fermion content, and Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian} is not discharged. \emph{Exponential observables \(\operatorname{Tr}\mathrm e^{\mathrm ik^\mu A_\mu}\) at \(N=2\).} Let \(O_k(A)=\operatorname{Tr}\mathrm e^{\mathrm ik^\mu A_\mu}\) with \(k\in\mathbb C^{10}\), at \(\gamma=0\), regulator (A), contours \(\mathcal R_u\) and \(\epsilon\to0^+\) last. For each fixed \(\epsilon>0\) and \(u<2/3\) the integral converges absolutely and is entire in \(k\), because on \(\mathcal R_u\) the growth of \(O_k\) is at most \(\mathrm e^{c(u,k)\|\tilde A\|}\) against a Gaussian regulator, and the fixed-\(\epsilon\) Stokes identity extends to it. At \(N=2\) the matrix \(k^\mu A_\mu\) has eigenvalues \(\pm\rho_X/2\) with \(\rho_X^2=k^\mu k^\nu\mathcal G_{\mu\nu}\), so \(O_k=2\cos(\rho_X/2)\) is branch-independent, and \(|2\cos(\omega/2)|^2=4(\cos^2(\operatorname{Re}\omega/2)+\sinh^2(\operatorname{Im}\omega/2))\). Along rank-one rays \(\tilde A_\mu=\rho_\mu H\), \(\operatorname{Tr}H^2=1\), the Euclidean weight \(\mathrm e^{-S_{\mathrm E}}\) is not exponentially damped and tends to a bounded factor along the corner (\(S_{\mathrm E}\) vanishes on the exact rank-one rays), the Pfaffian vanishes on the ray and is polynomial in the coordinates nearby, and the remaining factors are polynomial (numerical, in a reduced chart), while \(|O_k|\ge 2\sinh\!\bigl(b_u(\hat\rho;k)\rho/2\bigr)\) with \(b_u(\hat\rho;k)=|\operatorname{Im}(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm iu/8}\hat\rho_0+k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm iu/8}\hat\rho_i)|\). At every \(u\in(0,1]\) the functional \(\hat\rho\mapsto\operatorname{Im}(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm iu/8}\hat\rho_0+k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm iu/8}\hat\rho_i)\) is nonzero for every nonzero real \(k\) (at \(u=1\) its coefficients are \(-\sin(3\pi/8)k^0\) and \(\sin(\pi/8)\vec k\)), so the Euclidean side of the displayed limit identity diverges in modulus for every nonzero real \(k\); more generally it diverges for every \(k\) outside the slice on which \(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm i/8}\) and all \(k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm i/8}\) are real, where \(|O_k|\le2\) and that side converges by the \(k<14\) Pfaffian window. For \(\operatorname{Im}k\ne0\) such that some commuting unit \(\hat\rho\) has \(|\operatorname{Im}(k\cdot\hat\rho)|>|\operatorname{Re}(k\cdot\hat\rho)|\), the commuting-ray contribution to the \(u=0\) integral has \(S_{\mathrm b}=0\) and grows along a sequence \(\epsilon\to0^+\) like \(\mathrm e^{c_2(k)/\epsilon}\) with \(c_2(k)>0\) (commuting-ray model; no transverse stationary-phase bound is claimed), and for real \(k\ne0\) the integrand at \(u=0\) carries \(|O_k|\le2\) pointwise, but no fixed-\(u\) domination in \(\epsilon\) is available at any \(u\in(0,2/3)\): the fixed-\(\epsilon\) modulus on \(\mathcal R_u\) grows without bound as \(\epsilon\to0\), numerically like \(\mathrm e^{c(k,u)/\sqrt\epsilon}\) on the sampled range. Hence within the class stated the displayed limit identity fails for every nonzero real \(k\) and for every non-real \(k\) outside the slice on which \(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm i/8}\) and all \(k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm i/8}\) are real, holds at \(k=0\), and is unresolved on that slice at \(k\ne0\): there is no analyticity strip and no non-polynomial extension of the limit statement at \(\gamma=0\) off that slice, within the hypothesis class \(N=2\), regulator (A), contours \(\mathcal R_u\), \(\epsilon\to0^+\) last. The obstruction is the massless flat direction, and at fixed \(\gamma<0\) it is absent: the mass contributes on \(\mathcal R_u\) the real exponent \((\gamma/2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)[\sin(3\pi u/4)\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sin(\pi u/4)\sum_{i=1}^{9}\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2]\), a Gaussian damping on every component that dominates \(\mathrm e^{c(k)\|\tilde A\|}\) uniformly for \(\epsilon\le\epsilon_0<|\gamma|/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\), and the displayed limit identity then extends to \(O_k\) for every \(k\in\mathbb C^{10}\) (numerical). At fixed \(\gamma>0\) the \(u=1\) integral diverges in modulus even at \(k=0\) (numerical), so the contour family and the thimbles of~\cite{ChouNishimuraTripathi2025} act on different contour integrals and produce no competing value; the classicalization of~\cite{AsanoNishimuraPiensukYamamori2025} is tied there to a Lorentz-breaking infrared cutoff and bears neither on this paragraph nor on the statements above. Everything in this paragraph is for the hypothesis class stated in it, does not address \(N\to\infty\) taken first, and derives no signature, real-time response, background or continuation; Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian} is not discharged. \emph{The slice at \(k\ne0\): refinement of the window and model-level growth control.} On the slice \(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^0\in\mathbb R\), \(k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^i\in\mathbb R\) the Cauchy-Binet identity \(\rho_X^2=\sum_{a=1}^{3}{(k^\mu A^a_\mu)}^2\) is exact at \(N=2\), and on \(\mathcal R_1\) the contraction \(k^\mu A_\mu\) reduces to the Hermitian real-linear form \(r^\mu\tilde A_\mu\), so \(\rho_X\) is real and \(|O_k|\le2\) pointwise there; right-hand-side boundedness on the slice is structural. The commuting-ray alternative also refines inside the slice: at \(u=0\), writing the growth functional as \(B=\mathbf v\mathbf v^{\mathrm T}-\mathbf u\mathbf u^{\mathrm T}\) with \(\mathbf u=(\cos(3\pi/8)r^0,\cos(\pi/8)\vec r\,)\), \(\mathbf v=(\sin(3\pi/8)r^0,-\sin(\pi/8)\vec r\,)\), the matrix \(B\) is supported on \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf e_0,\hat{\mathbf r}\}\), has rank at most two, and \(\lambda_{\max}(B)=[{(r^0)}^2-r^2+\sqrt{{(r^0)}^4+6{(r^0)}^2r^2+r^4}]/(2\sqrt2)\), which is positive iff \(r^0\ne0\); for pure spatial slice momenta (\(r^0=0\)) there is no commuting ray with growth, the neutral set is \(\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\hat\rho=0\), and \(B\) is negative off it. For \(r^0\ne0\) the one-dimensional branch integral \(\int_{\mathbb R}\mathrm e^{-Qa^2}\,2\cos(\gamma_{\mathrm c}|a|)\,\mathrm da=2\sqrt{\pi/Q}\,\mathrm e^{-\gamma_{\mathrm c}^2/(4Q)}\), \(\gamma_{\mathrm c}=\mathrm e^{3\pi\mathrm i/8}r^0/2\), gives pointwise modulus growth \(\mathrm e^{{(r^0)}^2\cos(3\pi u/4-\pi/4)/(16\epsilon)}\) for the soft temporal mode at \(\gamma=0\), saturating as \(\epsilon\to0^+\) at fixed \(\gamma<0\) to a constant of order \(\mathrm e^{{(r^0)}^2\sin(3\pi u/4-\pi/4)/(8|\gamma|)}\), which diverges as \(\gamma\to0^-\) when \(3\pi u/4-\pi/4>0\) and decays when it is negative. In the reduced \(N=2\) integral (regulator (A), contours \(\mathcal R_u\), \(\epsilon\to0^+\) last) with this insertion at pure temporal slice momenta, the modulus ratio \(|Z_\epsilon[O_k]|/|Z_\epsilon[1]|\) stays of order one and tends to \(2\) along the ladder down to \(\epsilon=3\cdot10^{-3}\) (fit slope \(0.0000\) of \(\ln\)-ratio against \(1/\epsilon\), against the pointwise corner value \(0.044/\epsilon\) at \(r^0=1\)), and at \(u=0.6\) with \(\gamma=-0.2,-0.05\) it stays between \(0.74\) and \(1.78\): in the reduction neither the commuting-ray growth \(\mathrm e^{c_2(k)/\epsilon}\) nor the pointwise saturation factor materialises, consistent with phase-space suppression of the corner branch growth near the soft corner by Vandermonde and measure powers, though no domination lemma is claimed. These are reduced-model computations with oscillation-limited resolution at \(\gamma=0\); whether the left-hand side is controlled on the slice at \(k\ne0\) with \(r^0\ne0\) remains open at full \(N=2\), the \(\gamma\to0^-\) bridge from fixed-\(\gamma<0\) success remains open, and the pure-spatial half of the slice is now ray-free; Problem~E remains open and PROGRAMME-CONDITIONAL.
\paragraph{Slice bridge band and modulus obstruction.} On the slice \(k^0\mathrm e^{-3\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^0\in\mathbb R\), \(k^i\mathrm e^{\pi\mathrm i/8}=r^i\in\mathbb R\), the insertion is bounded on the final contour, \(|O_k|\le2\), since \(k^\mu A_\mu\) reduces on \(\mathcal R_1\) to the Hermitian form \(r^\mu\tilde A_\mu\) and \(\rho_X^2=\sum_{a=1}^{3}(r^\mu\tilde A^a_\mu)^2\ge0\), so the \(k<14\) Pfaffian window applies with the \(k=0\) majorant. With the auxiliary factor at \(\delta=\lambda\epsilon\), the quadratic damping along \(\mathcal R_\theta\), \(\theta\in[u,1]\), is strictly positive on the whole slide if and only if \(\delta>\lambda_*\epsilon\), \(\lambda_*=\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}\): the spatial coefficient stays positive for all \(\delta\ge0\), and the temporal ratio \(-\cos(3\pi\theta/4)/\cos(3\pi(2\theta-1)/8)\) is strictly increasing with supremum \(\lambda_*\) at \(\theta=1\). For each fixed \(\lambda>\lambda_*\) the damped family at \(u=1\) converges to the right-hand side by dominated convergence within the window. Removing the auxiliary factor from either undamped side in modulus fails: on the rank-one corner \(\tilde A_0=\rho H\), \(\operatorname{Tr}H^2=1\), with transverse scale \(O(\epsilon)\), \(|O_k|\ge\mathrm e^{b_u\rho/2}/(2\sqrt2)\), \(b_u=\sin(3\pi(1-u)/8)|r^0|>0\) for \(u<1\), against regulator damping \(\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)\rho^2\), so the fixed-\(\epsilon\) modulus integral on \(\mathcal R_u\) obeys \(M_u(\epsilon)\ge\mathrm e^{c_0(u)/\epsilon}/\mathrm{poly}\), \(c_0(u)=\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(16\cos(3\pi u/4))>0\) for \(u<2/3\), and diverges for \(u\ge2/3\); on the flat contour \(|O_k|\) grows like \(\mathrm e^{c(k)\rho}\) and the corresponding bound grows like \(\mathrm e^{c(k)^2/(4\epsilon)}\). The linking band \(\delta\le\mathrm{poly}\cdot\mathrm e^{-c_0(u)/\epsilon}\) is therefore incompatible, as \(\epsilon\to0^+\), with the Stokes band \(\delta\ge\lambda_*\epsilon\): within the class \(N=2\), regulator (A), \(\gamma=0\), slice momenta with \(r^0\ne0\), and linking by absolute-value estimates along the family \(\mathcal R_u\), no rate \(\delta(\epsilon)\) and no contour slide bridges the undamped slice insertion (a fixed negative mass contributes only a phase at \(u=0\)). The displayed limit identity therefore remains unresolved on the slice at \(k\ne0\) beyond such bridges; closing it requires non-modulus (oscillation-based) estimates or an independent mechanism. On \(\mathcal R_u\) the mass exponent has real part \((\gamma/2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)[\sin(3\pi u/4)\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sin(\pi u/4)\sum_{i=1}^{9}\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2]\le0\) for \(\gamma<0\), and \(|\mathrm e^{z}-1|\le|z|\) holds for \(\operatorname{Re}z\le0\); since \(|\operatorname{Tr}A_\mu A^\mu|\le\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2\) on \(\mathcal R_u\), expanding the mass exponential gives \(|\Delta(\epsilon,\gamma)|\le(|\gamma|/2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\,\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\), where \(\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\) is the \(\gamma=0\) modulus functional on \(\mathcal R_u\) with insertion \(\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_0^2+\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}\tilde A_i^2\). Along the rank-one ray with transverse scale \(O(\epsilon)\), the bound \(|O_k|\ge\mathrm e^{b_u\rho/2}/(2\sqrt2)\), \(b_u=\sin(3\pi(1-u)/8)|r^0|\), against the regulator damping \(\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)\rho^2\) gives \(\tilde J(\epsilon,u)\ge\mathrm e^{c_0(u)/\epsilon}/\mathrm{poly}\) with \(c_0(u)=\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(16\cos(3\pi u/4))>0\): \(\Delta\to0\) as \(\gamma\to0^-\) at each fixed \(\epsilon\), but no absolute-value estimate for \(\Delta\) is \(o(1)\) uniformly on any ladder \(\epsilon\to0^+\), so at fixed \(u\in(0,2/3)\) the \(\gamma\to0^{-}\) link by modulus bounds is obstructed by the same flat-direction divergence that obstructs the undamped slice left-hand side, and any bridge must control the \(\gamma=0\) slice integral by signed (oscillation-based) estimates uniform in \(\epsilon\). On the mass side the soft-mode scale is \(\epsilon\)-uniform but not \(\gamma\)-uniform: maximizing \(b_u\rho/2-[\epsilon\cos(3\pi u/4)+|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)]\rho^2\) gives \(b_u^2/(16\beta(\epsilon,\gamma))\) with \(\beta(\epsilon,\gamma)\ge|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\), and the ray region contributes at least \(\mathrm e^{\Gamma_0(u)/|\gamma|}/\mathrm{poly}\) with \(\Gamma_0(u)=g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2\sin^2(3\pi(1-u)/8){(r^0)}^2/(8\sin(3\pi u/4))>0\) for every \(u\in(0,2/3)\), strictly decreasing in \(u\) (\(\Gamma_0(1/3)\approx0.0884{(r^0)}^2\) at \(g_{\mathrm{YM}}=1\)): the mass-side modulus also blows up super-polynomially as \(\gamma\to0^-\), most strongly at small \(u\), so the two sides of the link are not simultaneously controllable in modulus. With the mass Gaussian the extension of the displayed limit identity at fixed \(\gamma<0\) to \(O_k\) for every \(k\in\mathbb C^{10}\) is a derivation: for fixed \(u\in(0,2/3)\) the damping of each Hermitian component is bounded below by \(|\gamma|\sin(3\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\) on \(\tilde A_0\) and \(|\gamma|\sin(\pi u/4)/(2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\) on \(\tilde A_i\), independent of \(\epsilon\), so the Stokes boundary term on balls times \([0,u]\) decays and both sides of the limit are dominated.
