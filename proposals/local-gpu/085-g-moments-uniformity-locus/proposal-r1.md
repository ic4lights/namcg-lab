# Proposal 085-g-moments-uniformity-locus, round 1

## Result
Within the named coupled proper-complex-Gaussian slot law of the integrated 084
paragraph, the three profile conditions generated by the existing G-block
paragraphs - decoupling of the induced pair (a,r) at a Haar-typical frame,
attainment of the sharp fixed-D profile minimum of 082, and vanishing of the
frame-averaged induced coupling - have exactly one common profile, the uniform
profile g_i = W/D. The frame average of the 084 coupling functional is
computed exactly: E_u Gamma = (D*sum g_i^2 - W^2)/(D(D+1)) =
W^2(D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1), so the frame-averaged coupled Tr Q^4 excess is
128 s^4 W^2 (D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1), a function of the profile through the 081
effective dimension alone, vanishing if and only if the profile is uniform. On a
frame-support constancy slice, sum g_i^2 is at least W^2/D with equality only at
the uniform profile, reaches W^2/min(m,D-m) on the equal-complement subfamily
and W^2 overall, so a single frame of smaller support does not enforce
uniformity. A negative datum against identification of the locus with the
integrated recovery mechanism: the integrated fuzzy variance proxies f = nu*l/2
(081's quoted profiles) have strictly increasing profiles, hence D_eff < D at
every cutoff, and remain off the uniformity locus while reaching
D_eff -> infinity through growth of the slot count; their frame-averaged
coupling vanishes anyway, so averaged decoupling does not imply uniformity at
fixed D. None of this fixes the state that sets W, g, s or u; Problem G's
missing steps (i)-(iv) are untouched and G stays open.

## Research and reasoning
Setup (all inside the 084 named class): slots w_i independent proper CN with
E w_i w_j* = s^2 g_i delta_ij, unit frame u, mu = sum g_i|u_i|^2,
Gamma(u) = sum |u_i|^2 (g_i - mu)^2, a = Im(u|w), r = |w|^2 - |(u|w)|^2.

1. Locus of decoupling (corollary of the 084 iff, established algebra).
084: a independent of r iff Gamma(u) = 0 iff g constant on supp(u). Writing
w_i = |u_i|^2: Gamma(u) = sum w_i g_i^2 - mu^2. Consequences: (i) a frame with
m >= 2 nonzero entries forces equality of the two entries on its support (D=2
closed form Gamma = w_1 w_2 (g_1-g_2)^2, s1); (ii) a full-support frame forces
g_i = mu for all i, i.e. the uniform profile; Haar-a.e. frame is full-support,
so decoupling at a Haar-typical frame is equivalent to uniformity; (iii) a
frame with m <= D-1 entries constrains g only on its support.
2. Constancy-slice bounds (profile algebra, established). On {g constant on S,
|S| = m}, sum g_i^2 >= W^2/D by Cauchy with equality only at the uniform
profile; within the equal-complement two-block family g = (a^m, b^(D-m)),
b = (W-ma)/(D-m), the sum of squares is a convex quadratic in a with minimum
W^2/D at a = W/D and endpoint maxima W^2/m and W^2/(D-m), so the subfamily
reaches W^2/min(m,D-m); overall the slice contains the single-row profile
(m <= D-1) so the range of rho_2 - 1 = sum g_i^2/W^2 runs up to 1 (s1, s3).
3. Frame-averaged coupling (new identity, established in class). With w = |u|^2
Haar (= Dirichlet(1,...,1)): E w_i = 1/D, E w_i w_j = (1+delta_ij)/(D(D+1));
hence E mu^2 = (W^2 + sum g_i^2)/(D(D+1)) and
E_u Gamma = sum g_i^2/D - (W^2+sum g_i^2)/(D(D+1)) = (D sum g_i^2 - W^2)/
(D(D+1)) = W^2(D_eff^-1 - D^-1)/(D+1), 081's D_eff = (sum g)^2/sum g_i^2.
The identity uses only the frame measure, not the slot law beyond 084's Gamma
definition. Vanishing iff uniform; strictly increasing in D_eff. By 084's
excess identity the frame-averaged coupled Tr Q^4 excess is 128 s^4 times that.
Exact Dirichlet integration D=2,3 and 400k-sample Monte Carlo D=5,8 (s2);
full-channel Monte Carlo D=4 confirming the pooled covariance with the correct
frame-level correction -Var_u(mu)/2 at |z| = 0.01 (s4).
4. Common locus and conditional selection reading. The uniform profile attains
all three requirements at once, and (1)+(2)+(3) show it is the unique profile
that can do so at fixed D. Reading this as selection needs an extra premise:
that the state-selection side of the matrix model drives the induced law to
decoupling at a Haar-typical frame at fixed D and W. Nothing in the repository
supplies such a premise (no selection dynamics is derived for G anywhere in
appH; the 081 paragraph keeps W and g as inputs), so the selection principle is
recorded as conditional mechanism, not derivation.
5. Negative datum on the proxies (established profile algebra, s5). The
integrated proxy profiles (boundary-row slots, (2l+1) copies of nu*l/2,
1 <= l <= l_c, so D = (l_c+1)^2) are strictly increasing, hence D_eff < D
strictly at every cutoff: they are never on the uniformity locus. Exact
rational evaluation reproduces the integrated quoted effective dimensions
(1488.00 at l_c = 40, 23016.89 at l_c = 160) and shows the quoted fourth-moment
excesses 4.0e-3 and 2.6e-4 are 6/D_eff, i.e. the p = 4 leading bound C(4,2)/D_eff
of the 081 paragraph (1/D_eff would be 6.7e-5 and 4.3e-5): the integrated
numbers are internally consistent (an earlier suspicion of a 6x discrepancy is
resolved - it is the p=4 bound, not the p=2 value). D_eff/D -> 8/9 along the
ramp (numerical at l_c up to 1280), so these proxies decouple by growing the
slot count while remaining strictly non-uniform at every finite D; their
frame-averaged coupling vanishes like 1/D^2 at fixed W
(from the identity: D_eff^-1 - D^-1 ~ 1/(8L^2) and D+1 ~ L^2). Hence "vanishing
frame-averaged coupling" is strictly weaker than the locus condition, and the
locus condition is a strictly stronger (special) route to recovery: the uniform
family reaches the 082 floor -> 1 and the vanishing coupling only as D -> inf.
6. Comparison with the original target. The target was to derive or bind the
profile data from the matrix theory. This package binds nothing of the physical
state; it converts the existing conditional layer into an exact consistency
locus and a named, explicitly conditional selection mechanism, plus a proved
separation between the locus and the integrated recovery route. The profile
remains an input.

   minimality) elevated with the new frame-averaged identity, over (a) a bare
   restatement of 082 minimality as a selection principle (no new content),

   package; kept ranked third). The locus package both tightens the constraint
   on rank 1 and produces a checkable negative datum, which is the
   mission-relevant way to "constrain" a profile that cannot yet be derived.

   a pointer only (R1, R2 marked not independently verified here).

   numerics); the manuscript makes only the exactly-proved strict-inequality
   and growth statements.

   weight functional under the Haar frame measure (slot-law-free beyond 084's
   definition), avoiding any claim that averaged coupling equals averaged slot
   covariance outside the class.
5. Splice anchored to the full 084 tail pin per the dispatch (the 083 tail was
   superseded as anchor when 084 integrated); never the short suffix (counts 3-5).
