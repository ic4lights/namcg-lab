\emph{Selection-independence of the fixed-frame excess.} On the product of two fuzzy spheres, \(X^I=(A_1J)^a\otimes\mathbf 1\) and \(\mathbf 1\otimes(A_2J)^a\), with interval projectors \(\Theta_f\) of ranks \(M_1,M_2\), in the gap-free case the edge operator \(E=\sum_I\Theta X^I\bar\Theta X^I\Theta\) of the edge-datum paragraph is diagonal and its nonzero spectrum is the multiset of \(s_1^2=k_1M_1(N-M_1)\) with multiplicity \(M_2-1\), of \(s_2^2=k_2M_2(N-M_2)\) with multiplicity \(M_1-1\), and of \(s_1^2+s_2^2\) once, so that \(\operatorname{Tr}E^p=(M_2-1)s_1^{2p}+(M_1-1)s_2^{2p}+(s_1^2+s_2^2)^p\) and the rank is \(M_1+M_2-1\) (numerical at \(N\le12\), deviations \(\le1.43\times10^{-14}\), rank exact).
The trace is additive at \(p=1\), \(\operatorname{Tr}E=M_2s_1^2+M_1s_2^2\), and for \(p\ge2\) the only non-additive part is the corner power: \(\bigl((s_1^2+s_2^2)^p-s_1^{2p}-s_2^{2p}\bigr)/\operatorname{Tr}E^p\) is \(O(1/N)\) at fixed \(M_f/N\), equal to \(2.44/N\) at \(p=2\), \(N=3200\), \(M_1=N/2\), \(M_2=N/3\) (numerical).
The selection rule of~\cite{Frenkel2023EdgeModes} fixes the dominant diagram at leading semiclassical order from the moments of the edge charge, one row per disconnected component; it is stated for the diagonal cut, and its transposition to the block cut is a transplant (conditional); for the block of the union it suggests a two-row diagram whose row lengths follow the factor edge data up to the \(O(1/N)\) corner admixture above.
On the saddle class \(\ell_i=t\,s_i\) with fixed \(t\), the defect \(\Delta=\log\dim_{U(M_1+M_2)}(\ell_1,\ell_2)-\log\dim_{U(M_1)}(\ell_1)-\log\dim_{U(M_2)}(\ell_2)\) has the closed form \(\sum_{c=1}^{\ell_1}\log\frac{M_1+M_2-1+c}{M_1-1+c}+\sum_{c=1}^{\ell_2}\log\frac{M_1+M_2-2+c}{M_2-1+c}-\log\frac{\ell_1+1}{\ell_1-\ell_2+1}\), which is the exact form of the excess of about \(\ell_1\log\frac{M_1+M_2}{M_1}+\ell_2\log\frac{M_1+M_2}{M_2}\) of the preceding paragraph (exact identity, checked in rational arithmetic on 1155 cases; grid survey numerical), and it grows linearly in \(N\): at \(M_1=M_2=N/2\), \(k_1=k_2=1\), \(\Delta/N=0.521\) at \(t=1\), \(N=3200\), the ratio to the naive form stabilising near \(0.75\).
The rank dependence survives at the saddle: \(\partial\log\dim/\partial\log M=\sum_{(r,c)\in\mu}M/(M+c-r)=0.811\,|\mu|\) at the same saddle (numerical).
So within the dominant-representation class - compact factor, positive contraction, leading semiclassical order at the leading-order entropy of~\cite{FlissFrenkelHartnollSoni2025} with one diagram selected from the moments of the \(U(M_1+M_2)\)-invariant edge data, restricted to the saddle class \(\ell_i=t\,s_i\) - the non-additivity of the preceding paragraph is not an artefact of the fixed probe diagram.
These statements are for the product background with the block of the union at fixed frame, two-row diagrams and the saddle class \(\ell_i=t\,s_i\); they are not a minimisation over frames, they do not give monotonicity of the excess in \(\ell_2\) (minimum increment \(\Delta(\ell_1,\ell_2+1)-\Delta(\ell_1,\ell_2)=-0.666\) at \((M_1,M_2,\ell_1,\ell_2)=(2,24,13,12)\), numerical), they leave items (i)-(iii) of the preceding paragraph unchanged, and they close nothing in Problem~G.
