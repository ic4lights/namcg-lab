\paragraph{Entropy assigned to the bundle curvature, and the delta content of the Poisson cone (conditional; not closure).}
Keep the setting of the preceding paragraph: the weighted \(a_2\) density, the plane-wave modes with \(\delta\rho=0\) about a constant self-dual or anti-self-dual \(\bar\Theta\), the completed replica, a totally geodesic \(\Sigma\), cross terms at first order in \(1-n\). The term \(\tfrac{\kappa_X}{12}\operatorname{tr}\Omega^2\) is quadratic in the bundle curvature, so its cross term with a delta \(\Omega_\delta=2\pi(1-n)\,H\,\delta_\Sigma\,\mathrm dx^1\wedge\mathrm dx^2\) is \(\kappa_X\operatorname{tr}(H\Omega_{12})\), with \(H\in\mathfrak{so}(4)\) the holonomy of the bundle connection around \(\Sigma\). If the connection is treated as an auxiliary independent field whose internal rotations are tied to the normal plane of \(\Sigma\) through a regular frame (Lorentz-Lie Noether charge), the charge density is \(n^{ab}\,\partial L/\partial\Omega^{ab}\), which is this cross term with \(H=\mathcal J_{12}\) and no other generator: it reproduces \(\kappa_X\tau_\Omega\) and not more. If the internal group is not tied to the frame, the charge vanishes at the bifurcation surface and the term is absent. The first reading assigns the coefficient \(-2\kappa_X\) of \(W\) to \(\kappa_X\operatorname{tr}\Omega^2\) and the second assigns \(0\), against \(-4\kappa_X\) on the \(\delta\rho=0\) modes.

For the diagonal family \(\Theta^{12}=r^{-\epsilon c_a}\), \(\Theta^{34}=r^{-\epsilon c_b}\), \(\epsilon=n-1\), one finds exactly \(H=c_a\mathcal J_{12}+c_b\mathcal J_{34}\), with the Riemann deltas \(R_{1212}=-2\pi\epsilon c_a\), \(R_{a3a3}=R_{a4a4}=-\pi\epsilon c_b\) (symbolic computation). The cone angle of the normal plane fixes \(c_a=1\); \(c_b\neq0\) makes the metric induced on \(\Sigma\) singular at order \(\epsilon\). With all deltas inserted in the cross term of the weighted \(a_2\), the gap of the preceding paragraph becomes \(-N_{\mathrm{tot}}c_b/15+2\kappa_X(1+c_b)\) about a self-dual \(\bar\Theta\) and \(-N_{\mathrm{tot}}c_b/15+2\kappa_X(1-c_b)\) about an anti-self-dual one, and equals \(2\kappa_X\) at \(c_b=0\). The \(\kappa_X\) part vanishes only for a holonomy of chirality opposite to that of \(\bar\Theta\), while the \(N_{\mathrm{tot}}\) part then becomes nonzero, so no content-independent profile removes the gap in this family.

On the 17 sampled points with totally geodesic \(\Sigma\) (exact rational arithmetic; not proved), \(\tau_\Omega=R_\Sigma-2W+\bigl(a_1^{v}-\tfrac12X'\bigr)\), with \(a_1^{v}\) the \(a_1\) density of the vector sector. The coefficient of the Euler density \(R_\Sigma\) in the completed coefficient is therefore \(\kappa_X-N_{\mathrm{tot}}/30\), the value of the Levi-Civita cross-term rule, and the remainder is a combination of the \(\Lambda^2\)-order densities \(a_1\) and \(X'\), which are not Euler densities. These statements are for the diagonal family, the modes and replica stated, and the hypotheses of the preceding paragraphs; they do not derive the holonomy selected by the matrix model, the replica, or a value or sign of \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\).
