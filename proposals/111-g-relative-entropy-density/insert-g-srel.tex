\paragraph{Relative entropy of a cut state against a Gaussian reference (verified building block; input tier).}
Let \(\sigma_0=\exp(-R A_0 R/2)/Z_0\) be the faithful Gaussian reference state of the cut data, with covariance \(G_0\) of symplectic spectrum \(\nu_j>1/2\), modular exponent \(A_0\) in closed form, and normalization \(c_0=-\ln Z_0=\sum_j\ln\bigl(2\sinh(h(\nu_j)/2)\bigr)\), where \(h(\nu)=\ln\bigl((\nu+\tfrac12)/(\nu-\tfrac12)\bigr)\). Then for every state \(\rho_1\) with uncentered symmetrized second moment \((G_1)_{ij}=\operatorname{Tr}\rho_1(R_iR_j+R_jR_i)/2\),
\[
S(\rho_1\|\sigma_0)=-S(\rho_1)+\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(A_0G_1)-c_0
\]
(exact), since \(\log\sigma_0\) is the quadratic form \(-\tfrac12 R A_0 R-\ln Z_0\) and the expectation of a quadratic form in any state pairs with the symmetrized moment; the uncentered moment is essential, because the centered covariance misses the identity by exactly \(\tfrac12 m^{\mathsf T}A_0 m\) when \(\rho_1\) has mean \(m\) (exact algebra, confirmed numerically). When \(\rho_1\) is Gaussian the right side is a closed functional of the two covariances, the object the boundary-entropy route evaluates. The closed forms of \(A_0\) and \(c_0\) are verified algebraically and supported numerically on the certified grid, where they are Fock-anchored on actual chain-cut ground states by an 18-check battery with no failures; the anchored grid excludes cuts with \(\mu=0.01\) and near-pure sectors with \(N\ge12\) sampled, where \(h\) is unresolvable in double precision (fixed-input resolution limit, not a failure). A two-state Fock-anchor comparison of the full relative entropy agrees only to \(3.4\times10^{-3}\) to \(1.45\times10^{-2}\), with the whole discrepancy carried by the entropy term (residuals \(3.7\times10^{-3}\) and \(6.2\times10^{-3}\)) while the quadratic term matches to \(10^{-6}\) (numerical); the proof of the identity does not use the Fock construction of \(\rho_1\), so the discrepancy is attributed to the anchor truncation, of the same size as its documented tolerance (\(\le3\times10^{-2}\)), and the named route to remove it is a clean symbolic two-state anchor cross-checked against the Gaussian closed form of the relative entropy of two Gaussian states (named gap G-R).
