## Findings
1. **Severity: error** (wording). Location: proposal-r1.md L405 (drafted LaTeX). Finding: `$\mathcal G^{+}_{\mu\nu}$` is undefined — the coincidence tensor was introduced as `$g^{+}_{\mu\nu}$` (L391). Reason: undefined symbol in manuscript text. Required action: replace `$\mathcal G^{+}_{\mu\nu}$` with `$g^{+}_{\mu\nu}$`.
2. **Severity: error** (wording-level mathematical imprecision). Location: L406-407. Finding: "since the composite determinant is the squared frame determinant" is exact only for the Hermitian model composite ΦΦ^H (K3a); for the actual coincidence composite g^+ built from Φ^TηΦ with η = diag(−1,1,1,1), det = det η · (det Φ)² = −(det Φ)². The non-degeneracy equivalence is unaffected. Required action: see Corrected snippet (adds "differs … only by the constant factor det η").
3. **Severity: convention**. Location: L399, L401-402 (four occurrences of `\operatorname{Hom}_{G}`). Finding: `G` is not defined anywhere in the manuscript (grep confirms no prior `\operatorname{Hom}` and no defined group G); undefined notation. Required action: replace the subscript `G` with `\mathrm{SO}(3,1)` in all four occurrences — the same dimensions, since for these completely reducible finite-dimensional representations intertwiners over SO(3,1) equal those over the complexified algebra the scripts enumerate.
4. **Severity: minor conditionality-gap**. Location: L375. Finding: the expansion is stated "valid for separations large compared with $\LUV^{-1}$" only; the derivative expansion additionally requires separations small compared with the scale on which w varies (the proposal itself, L166-168, invokes only the lower bound). Required action: append "and small compared with the scale on which $w_{\rho}$ varies" (Corrected snippet).
5. **Severity: advisory**. Location: L372 ("with the internal indices … contracted after the locked identification of Equation~\eqref{eq:VEV}"). Finding: the internal δ-contraction through the internal transporter is invariant without the locked identification; the clause risks reading as if the definition imports eq:VEV (invariance itself needs only U_A transport and eq:Phi-transform). Suggested optional wording: "with the internal indices of the two insertions contracted through the internal transporter, the locked identification of Equation~\eqref{eq:VEV} being the case in which the coincidence term reproduces the frame datum". Not required for correctness (the paragraph's opening assumption clause already declares the locked sector hypothetical).

"(word-algebra check; … checked numerically at cubic scaling)" reads as computational shorthand; not chat/process language, but the integrator may prefer "verified as an exact identity of the truncated noncommutative series; agreement with the exact transport series through the same order confirmed at cubic scaling". Optional.
