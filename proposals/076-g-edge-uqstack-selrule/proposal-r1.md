# Proposal 076-g-edge-uqstack-selrule, round 1

## Result

Selection-rule consistency for the U(q)-repetition of the transplanted edge-charge
selection rule on stacked-row diagrams is settled as a MIXED, fully bounded package
inside the 072 dominant-representation class (H1)-(H4), extended by coincident-copy
backgrounds (H1'), and for Gaussian block-cut states:

1. POSITIVE, coincident-cap subclass (established operator identity, numerically
   verified): for q coincident copies of a compact fuzzy factor with the factor
   matrices and the cap projector lifted as tensor products I_q (x) (one-copy data)
   and copy-invariant momenta Pi~ = I_q (x) Pi, the block-cut edge charge satisfies
   Q~ = I_q (x) Q exactly (max deviation 7.105e-15, s1.out:2; exactly 0.000e+00 for
   the two-cap and product lifts, s2.out:3, s4.out:2-3). Hence every nonzero
   eigenvalue of Q~ has multiplicity exactly q and every even moment carries the
   common factor q: Tr Q~^{2p} = q Tr Q^{2p} (verified p = 1..4 to 4.185e-16
   relative, s1.out:4; all p follow from the identity). The moment signature the
   rule reads is therefore exactly the stacked diagram (l^q) of common depth q, to
   which the integrated 074 paragraph's exact closed form and continuum limits
   apply. The package realizes the SME-scout route (a) for this subclass.
2. STATE/BACKGROUND SEPARATION (negation of a natural over-read): on the SAME
   coincident background with generic (non-copy-invariant) momenta the charge keeps
   the same rank (rgeneric = rlift = 2q in all six s1 cases) but its nonzero
   eigenvalues are simple (least gap 8.968586, s1.out:6; product case 0.796973,
   s4.out:5). Multiplicity q is a property of the STATE, not of the background
   alone; background coincidence does not select stacking.
3. BOUNDED NEGATION on the band-separated multiplicity-1 subclass (the exact-union
   configuration of the Row length paragraph, appH L3147): with saddle row lengths
   l_f = 2 t s_f proportional to N and depths (q_1,q_2), the stacked moment
   signature exceeds the multiplicity-1 signature by the fraction
   rho_p = sum_f (q_f-1) w_f^p / sum_f w_f^p with w_f = k_f m_f(1-m_f), exactly
   independent of N and of the saddle parameter t (sympy, p = 1..4, s3.out:2-5);
   numerically rho_1 = 9/17 and rho_2 = 81/145 at (q_1,q_2) = (2,1),
   (m_1,m_2) = (1/2,1/3), k_f = 1 (exact rational, s3.out:7; float check at
   N = 600, 1200, 2400 with spread 0.000e+00, s3.out:12-16), and rho_p = 1 at
   equal depths (2,2) for all p (s3.out:8). The mismatch is O(1) in N, so within
   this subclass no stacked diagram is selected at the leading semiclassical order
   at which the rule fixes one dominant diagram.
4. BOUNDED NEGATION, product testbed (s4): the edge-operator spectrum is the
   one-copy multiset tiled q_1 q_2 times and rank E = q_1 q_2 (M_1+M_2-1),
   independently of the state (E is state-free in these conventions; s4.out:1);
   charge multiplicity under copy-invariant momenta is UNIFORM over the whole
   spectrum - depth q_1 q_2, or depth q_1 for factor-1-only invariance, the
   operator identities Q = I (x) Q_0 and Q = I_{q_1} (x) Q_{01} holding exactly
   (0.000e+00, s4.out:2-3). Hence inside this class the per-factor stacked diagram
   (l_1^{q_1}, l_2^{q_2}) with q_1 != q_2 is never the diagram read from charge
   multiplicity.

Nothing is closed. Problem G remains OPEN / CRITICAL PATH / PROGRAMME-CONDITIONAL.
The two-sided (+/- pair) reading is untouched; the state that realizes the
multiplicity is exhibited (copy-invariant), not fixed by the dynamics.

Setting and hypotheses. Hypothesis class: the 072 (H1)-(H4), verbatim from the 072
proposal L135-143: (H1) the 067 product testbed with block rank M = M_1+M_2,
M_f = m_f N; (H2) leading semiclassical order with one dominant representation, the
diagram selected by U(M)-invariant moments of the edge datum via Frenkel's rule
(one row per disconnected component; rows fixed by edge-charge moments, eqs.
(10)/(61)), transplanted to the block cut as the author of 2311.10131 himself
leaves open; (H3) saddle rows l_i = t s_i, t fixed as N -> infinity; (H4) two-row
diagrams. This package:
- (H1') EXTENSION, stated explicitly: factor f is replaced by q_f COINCIDENT
  copies - the representation space C^{q_f N} with X^a_f -> I_{q_f} (x) X^a_f and
  cap/interval projectors Theta_f -> I_{q_f} (x) Theta_f (s1: one factor, q copies;
  s2: two caps on one axis plus a q-copy lift of the whole two-cap background;
  s4: product of two factors with q_1, q_2 copies). This is a new legitimate

- (S) states: Gaussian-type block-cut momenta Pi^a Hermitian; the positive results
  require COPY-INVARIANT momenta Pi~ = I (x) Pi; the negations use generic (full
  Gaussian) or band-limited momenta. Band separation D-1 > 2W is assumed exactly
  where the multiplicity-1 union claim of L3147 is assumed (asserted in s2.py line

- (H3) used for the saddle signature arithmetic in s3 (l_f = 2 t s_f,

Derivation 1 (s1, positive coincident subclass). The block-cut edge charge is
Q = -2i sum_a (X^a_{S,Sb} Pi^a_{Sb,S} - h.c.), i.e. -2i(A - A^dagger) with
A = sum_a (Theta X^a (1-Theta))( (1-Theta) Pi^a Theta ), the same code form as the
L3147 formula. Under the (H1') lifts, every factor in A is a Kronecker product with
I_q, so A~ = I_q (x) A and Q~ = I_q (x) Q as an operator identity; s1 verifies it
on 6 explicit cases (N = 6, 8, 10; q = 2, 3; random X from random A in GL(3)-type
samples times scale nu, random-axis cap of rank M) to 7.105e-15, together with
rank-1-ness of the edge datum Theta X^a Thetabar (0 violations - the L3147 rank-1
hypothesis holds on these backgrounds, so the single-cap +/- pair structure
applies), the multiplicity-q spectrum tiling (2.132e-14), the moment identity
Tr Q~^{2p} = q Tr Q^{2p} for p = 1..4 (4.185e-16 relative; all p follow from the
operator identity), rank Q~ = q rank Q (exact), and the state-free edge operator
E~ = I (x) E spectral tiling (8.882e-15). Conclusion: on such states the
U(M)-invariant even moments the rule reads are q times the one-copy moments, i.e.
identical to the signature of the stacked diagram (l^q); the rule's U(q)-repeated
reading and the stacked diagram of common depth q agree, and 074's integrated
closed form / continuum limits apply to that diagram. This is the positive
selection-rule consistency on the degenerate-cap subclass (scout route (a)).

Derivation 2 (state property, s1(c), s2, s4(d)). On the SAME coincident background,
generic full-Gaussian momenta on C^{qN} give rank Q~ = 2q (equal to the
copy-invariant rank, so rank cannot diagnose the multiplicity) but a simple nonzero
spectrum: least consecutive gap 8.968586 (s1), 0.796973 (s4). Thus background
coincidence alone does NOT make the charge spectrum multiplicity-q, and the
stacking-selected-by-multiplicity mechanism requires state copy-invariance. Within
the tested instances, multiplicity q is a property of the state. (The word
"split" in the insert means exactly: simple spectrum with an O(1) gap in every
tested case.)

Derivation 3 (s2 + s3, band-separated multiplicity-1 negation). s2 makes the L3147
exact-union claim live numerically: two caps on one axis with band-limited Pi
(bandwidth W) and edge separation D-1 > 2W, the two-cap nonzero spectrum equals
the multiset union of the two single-cap spectra to 0.000e+00, and the union
spectrum is simple (least gap 10.572518 over three cases) - multiplicity 1. A
control with full Gaussian Pi breaks union exactness (2.388e+01, 1.567e+01,
2.343e+01), so the band hypothesis is essential and is stated, not silently
dropped. s2(c) additionally shows that lifting THIS background to q coincident
copies with copy-invariant band-limited states again gives Q~ = I (x) Q and exact
q-tiling (both 0.000e+00): the positive mechanism of Derivation 1 is not
restricted to single caps. s3 then quantifies the stacking failure in the
multiplicity-1 class: with the (H3) saddle convention, l_f^{2} = 4 t^2 k_f
m_f(1-m_f) N^2, true signature S_true(p) = l_1^{2p} + l_2^{2p}, stacked signature
S_stack(p) = q_1 l_1^{2p} + q_2 l_2^{2p}; the excess fraction
rho_p = (S_stack - S_true)/S_true = sum_f (q_f - 1)(k_f w_f)^p / sum_f (k_f w_f)^p,
w_f = m_f(1-m_f) (equivalently w_f = k_f m_f(1-m_f) with k_f absorbed, as the
insert writes it), is sympy-exactly free of N and t for p = 1..4; substituting
(q_1,q_2) = (2,1), (m_1,m_2) = (1/2, 1/3), k_f = 1 gives rho_1 = 9/17,
rho_2 = 81/145. I re-checked the arithmetic by hand: w_1 = 1/4, w_2 = 2/9,
rho_1 = (1/4)/(1/4 + 2/9) = (1/4)/(17/36) = 9/17; rho_2 = (1/16)/(1/16 + 4/81)
= (1/16)/(145/1296) = 81/145. Equal depths give rho_p = 1 identically (excess =
true signature), and the symmetric saddle gives excess/true = q - 1, p-independent.
Since rho_p is O(1) in N, replacing (l_1, l_2) by (l_1^{q_1}, l_2^{q_2}) misreads
the moment signature at leading order in N: within the band-separated multiplicity-1
subclass the rule selects (l_1, l_2), never the stacked diagram - the scout
obstacle quantified.

Derivation 4 (s4, product uniformity). Index order C^{q1} (x) C^{q2} (x) C^N (x)
C^N (copies first) makes all lifts plain Kronecker products; an explicit
associativity check (max deviation 0.000e+00) guards the grouping
C^{q1 q2} = C^{q1} (x) C^{q2}. Results: (a) E tiling by q_1 q_2 exact,
rank E = q_1 q_2 (M_1 + M_2 - 1) matching the integrated gap-free spectrum

(c) momenta invariant in factor 1 only give Q = I_{q_1} (x) Q_{01} exactly
with uniform depth q_1 over the whole spectrum including the factor-2 eigenvalues
