\paragraph{Realization of the spectral-positivity hypotheses in homogeneous Presentation-B matrix quantum mechanics (derivations in a named truncation class; not closure).} The hypotheses of the sublemma of the paragraph ``Spectral positivity of the exact correlator'' are realized as theorems in the following named class. Class: Equation~\eqref{eq:Smicro} with \(A_\mu=0\), no couplings beyond Equations~\eqref{eq:Smicro} and~\eqref{eq:V}, fixed flat background \(g^{(0)}=\eta\) of Assumption~\ref{ass:lorentzian}, truncated to the spatially homogeneous mode \(\Phi(\mathbf x,t)=M(t)\) at finite internal dimension \(N\), and canonically quantized as matrix quantum mechanics on \(L^2(\mathbb C^{N^2})\) with the flat field-space measure. Derivations in the class. The kinetic form of the truncation is \(\operatorname{Tr}\dot M^\dagger\dot M=\sum_{I,A}|\dot M_{IA}|^2\): the field-space metric is flat, positive and finite-dimensional, so there is no ordering ambiguity, and the Legendre transform gives the canonical Hamiltonian \(H=V_3^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}\Pi\Pi^\dagger+V_3(\lambda/4)\operatorname{Tr}(M^\dagger M-v^2\mathbb I)^2\) with \(V_3\) the volume of space (checked symbolically). The potential equals \((\lambda/4)\sum_a(\sigma_a^2-v^2)^2\) in the singular values \(\sigma_a\) of \(M\) (identity checked symbolically), hence is non-negative, vanishes exactly on the compact set \(M=vU\) with \(U\) unitary, and dominates \(V_3(\lambda/4N)(\operatorname{Tr}M^\dagger M-Nv^2)^2\) by Cauchy--Schwarz on the eigenvalues, \(N\operatorname{Tr}A^2\ge(\operatorname{Tr}A)^2\) for Hermitian \(A\) (the \(N=2\) sum-of-squares form \(2\operatorname{Tr}A^2-(\operatorname{Tr}A)^2=(a_{11}-a_{22})^2+4|a_{12}|^2\) checked symbolically). Then, for every finite \(N\): (i)~\(H\) is the Schr\"odinger operator \(-V_3^{-1}\Delta+V_3(\lambda/4)\operatorname{Tr}(M^\dagger M-v^2\mathbb I)^2\) on \(\mathbb R^{2N^2}\): by the classical essential self-adjointness criterion for Schr\"odinger operators with continuous potentials bounded below it is essentially self-adjoint on \(C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^{2N^2})\), and its Friedrichs extension obeys \(H\ge0\); the potential is confining, so the Rellich compactness criterion gives compact resolvent, pure point spectrum \(E_n\to\infty\) with an orthonormal eigenbasis, and an \(L^2\)-normalizable ground-state eigenvector \(\Omega\); no gap above \(E_0\) is claimed or needed. (ii)~For every self-adjoint observable \(\mathcal O\) of the truncated model and every projection with real constant coefficients (the class analogue of the spin-two projection, which preserves self-adjointness), \(\langle\Omega|\mathcal O(t)\mathcal O(0)|\Omega\rangle=\sum_ne^{-\mathrm i(E_n-E_0)t}|\langle\Omega|\mathcal O|n\rangle|^2\) by the spectral resolution of (i): the spectral measure is a positive sum of point masses and the residue at each isolated simple real pole of the projected two-point function is a sum of squared matrix elements and so non-negative. This realizes the residue-positivity hypotheses of the sublemma above as theorems in the class for every self-adjoint observable, not only for a derived composite \(T_{\mu\nu}\). (iii)~The Euclidean measure of the class realized as the kernel of \(e^{-\tau H}\) is reflection-positive for the reflection \(\theta\) that negates Euclidean time about \(0\): the \(\theta\)-reflected pairing of any bounded measurable functional of the fields at times of one side equals \(\|e^{-(T/2)H}\widetilde{\mathcal F}\|^2\ge0\), in terms of the time-zero representative \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}\) and the time length \(T>0\), by self-adjointness and \(H\ge0\). Contrast, fixing the scope of the recorded no-go statements of Sections~\ref{sec:status-E} and~\ref{sec:status-D}. The weights failing reflection positivity there are of the Presentation-A family with \(\eta_{\mu\nu}\)-contracted field-space metric, so that failure is a property of that family at finite \(N\) and not of finite-\(N\) matrix measures at large; and the sign of the kinetic convention of Equation~\eqref{eq:Smicro} is decisive rather than cosmetic, since the canonical classical-mechanics lift of Equation~\eqref{eq:Smatrix} to a time \(\tau\) with Hermitian \(\hat X^\mu(\tau)\) and kinetic metric \(\eta_{\mu\nu}\) has Hamiltonian kinetic polynomial \(\sum_{\mu,\nu}\eta^{\mu\nu}\operatorname{Tr}(P^\mu P^\nu)\), which evaluates to \(-\operatorname{Tr}(P^0)^2<0\) at \(P^i=0\) and whose quantum form is \(-\lambda^2\|\chi\|^2+O(\lambda)\) on states \(e^{\mathrm i\lambda q_0}\chi\) in a coordinate \(q_0\) of the \(\hat X^0\) fibre, while its quartic form equals \(g_{\mathrm{YM}}^{-2}\bigl(\tfrac14\sum_{i,j}\|[\hat X^i,\hat X^j]\|^2-\tfrac12\sum_i\|[\hat X^0,\hat X^i]\|^2\bigr)\) with \(\|[\,\cdot\,,\cdot\,]\|^2=-\operatorname{Tr}[\,\cdot\,,\cdot\,]^2\ge0\) (identity checked symbolically on traceless \(2\times2\) Hermitian matrices), which tends to \(-\infty\) on the ray \(\hat X^0=t\sigma_1/2\), \(\hat X^1=t\sigma_2/2\), where it equals \(-t^4/(4g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2)\): in that lift the sublemma hypotheses fail. Together the two halves pin the open content of the sublemma hypotheses to recorded inputs: the kinetic-sign content of Assumption~\ref{ass:dictionary} that Presentation~A reproduces the convention of Equation~\eqref{eq:Smicro}, the derivation of the composite fluctuation \(T_{\mu\nu}\) of Problem~D, and the restriction to the matrix-quantum-mechanics truncation class named here.
