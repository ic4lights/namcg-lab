\emph{Exact flat-Dirichlet statistics of the obstruction factor at general dimension.} On the unit-trace profile simplex \(p=(p_{1},\dots,p_{D})\), \(p_{i}\ge0\), \(\sum_{i}p_{i}=1\), under the flat member \(a=1\), \(\beta=0\) of the fixed-trace Dirichlet-beta family - the member identified in the fixed-trace beta deformation paragraph above as the flat Haar frame weight and as the slot pushforward at uniform profile, and whose flat Dirichlet moments were confirmed numerically at \(D=3\) in the eigenvalue-reduction paragraph above - the moment-map factor \(\rho_{p}=p!\,h_{p}\) at unit trace, with \(h_{p}\) the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial and \(\rho_{1}=1\) identically on the simplex, has closed-form means at general \(D\): \(\mathbb{E}\,\rho_{2}=(D+3)/(D+1)\), \(\mathbb{E}\,\rho_{3}=(D^{2}+9D+26)/((D+1)(D+2))\), \(\mathbb{E}\,\rho_{4}=(D^{3}+18D^{2}+131D+426)/((D+1)(D+2)(D+3))\), obtained from the exact partition form with the unit-trace substitution imposed before the average and the power-sum factors grouped into equality patterns of \(b\) equal-index blocks, each pattern contributing the falling factorial \(D!/(D-b)!\) of slot assignments times the standard Dirichlet monomial moment \(\mathbb{E}\prod_{i}p_{i}^{a_{i}}=(D-1)!\bigl(\prod_{i}a_{i}!\bigr)/(D+A-1)!\) with \(A=\sum_{i}a_{i}\) (symbolic in \(D\)). The means sit strictly above the uniform floor at every \(D\ge2\): the \(p=2\) gap is \(\mathbb{E}\,\rho_{2}-(1+1/D)=(D-1)/(D(D+1))\), and the \(p=3\) and \(p=4\) gaps are \((D-1)(3D^{2}+16D+4)/(D^{2}(D+1)(D+2))\) and \((D-1)(6D^{4}+79D^{3}+361D^{2}+168D+36)/(D^{3}(D+1)(D+2)(D+3))\), each a positive factor times a polynomial in \(D\) with positive coefficients. The power sum \(s_{2}=\sum_{i}p_{i}^{2}=1/D_{\mathrm{eff}}\), so that \(\rho_{2}=1+s_{2}\), carries \(\mathbb{E}\,s_{2}=2/(D+1)\), \(\mathbb{E}\,s_{2}^{2}=4(D+5)/((D+1)(D+2)(D+3))\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(s_{2})=4(D-1)/((D+1)^{2}(D+2)(D+3))\), strictly positive for every \(D\ge2\) and zero only at \(D=1\), where \(s_{2}=1\) is forced; hence \(D\,\mathbb{E}\,s_{2}\to2\) and \(D^{3}\operatorname{Var}(s_{2})\to4\), so \(s_{2}-\mathbb{E}\,s_{2}\to0\) in \(L^{2}\) at rate \(D^{-3/2}\). The pointwise pin \(1\le\rho_{p}\le1+(p!-1)s_{2}\) on the simplex, with \(\rho_{2}-1=s_{2}\) giving equality at \(p=2\), then yields \(\mathbb{E}\,\rho_{p}\to1\) as \(D\to\infty\) at fixed \(p\); in mean the pin is attained at \(p=2\) and is strict at \(p=3\) and \(p=4\) at \(D=3,6,24\). The closed means are corroborated by fixed-seed Monte Carlo on the flat simplex law at \(D=3,6,24\): all nine sampled means agree with the closed values to within relative \(10^{-2}\), worst relative deviation \(8.19\times10^{-4}\) (numerical corroboration); the pin holds pointwise on random rational profiles at \(p=2,\dots,5\).
