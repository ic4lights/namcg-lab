Within the quadratic transfer class the requirement sharpens to a no-go: for real non-degenerate \(\Theta^{\mu\nu}\), every \(\eta\)- or \(g^{(0)}\)-contracted quadratic commutator transfer yields a kinetic tensor equal, up to a constant factor, an overall sign, and index placement, to the background-form \(K^{\mu\nu}=\Theta^{\alpha\mu}g^{(0)}_{\alpha\beta}\Theta^{\beta\nu}\) of the kinetic tensor of Definition~\ref{def:matrix-coords-ext} (the induced tensor \(g_{\alpha\beta}\) of the definition replaced by \(g^{(0)}_{\alpha\beta}\)), which is congruent to \(g^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}\) (hence Lorentzian) and nowhere proportional to \(g^{(0)\mu\nu}\); the energy of a kinetic term of this type is bounded below only for time directions within the cone of \(K\), so no normalization makes the transfer positive for the full \(g^{(0)}\)-time cone, and on these data the sign of the kinetic term is an independent datum of the dictionary rather than an open consequence of the transfer (Appendix~\ref{app:status}).
