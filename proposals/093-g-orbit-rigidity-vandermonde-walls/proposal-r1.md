## Result
Within the named coupled gaussian slot law at strictly positive profile and the named fixed-trace Dirichlet-beta family, this package gives the quantitative orbit-rigidity reading of the Vandermonde singularity of the edge-charge moment coordinate near coincidence walls:

1. Exact chamber Jacobian. On the trace-fixed chamber g_1>...>g_D>0 with g_D = W - sum eliminated, det d(rho_2,...,rho_D)/d(g_1,...,g_{D-1}) is exactly +- (prod_{k=1}^D k!) V(g) / W^{D(D+1)/2-1} with V(g) = prod_{i<j}(g_i-g_j); verified symbolically at D=2,3 and at three rational profiles at D=4 (s1 Part A). It vanishes to first order exactly on the coincidence walls and nowhere else in the chamber, so the moment tuple is a genuine local chamber coordinate off the walls.
2. D=2 exact global fold. rho_2 = 1 + s_2/W^2 = (3W^2 + (g_1-g_2)^2)/(2W^2); the wall value 3/2 is the uniform-profile value and equals the uniform lower bound prod_{k=1}^{p-1}(1+k/D) at (p,D)=(2,2); the single-row limit is 2 = p!; exact inversion (g_1-g_2)^2 = 2W^2(rho_2 - 3/2); the inverse is globally 1/2-Holder with sharp constant sqrt(2) W (exact on a 17x17 grid) and Lipschitz with constant W^2/delta on abs(g_1-g_2) >= delta.
3. Transverse normal form at general D. Along the wall g_1=g_2=c with the pair mean and the remaining slots fixed, s_k(c+u)+s_k(c-u)-2s_k(c) = 2 sum_j binom(k,2j) c^{k-2j} u^{2j} is exactly even (k=2..6); the rho_2 deviation is exactly (2/W^2)u^2 independently of the wall position (D=2,3,4 with symbolic rest); the scaled power-sum deviations sigma_k = s_k(g)/W^k have leading transverse coefficients k(k-1)c^{k-2}u^2/W^k. The fold u -> u^2 is the transverse normal form at simple walls. At D=3 numerically: sigma_min of the coordinate differential vanishes like the gap at a single wall (ratio -> 2 under delta -> 2delta) and like delta^{D-1} near total coincidence (ratio -> 4), and the sharp inverse modulus along the symmetric path into the triple point is 1/2-Holder (log-log slope 2.0000).
4. Beta-family integrability dichotomy. On the D=2 Gram separation coordinate u = abs(p_1-p_2) with density proportional to u^beta (1-u^2)^{a-1}: E abs(p_1-p_2)^{-1} at beta=2 is exactly (2a+1)Gamma(a+1/2)/(a sqrt(pi) Gamma(a)), equal to 3/2 at a=1 (the computed (a,beta) = (|N-2M|+1,2) member); beta=0 members diverge logarithmically with leading coefficient 2Gamma(a+1/2)/(sqrt(pi)Gamma(a)) = 1/Z(a,0), equal to 1 at a=1; int |V|^{-1} dmu converges near an m-fold coincidence exactly for beta > 1-2/m (beta>0 at a simple wall, beta>1/3 at the D=3 triple point), from the radial exponent e = binom(m,2)(beta-1)+(m-2) with e+1 = binom(m,2)(beta-(1-2/m)) checked at m=2,3,4.

None of this selects a profile, a group element or a state, evaluates any matrix integral of the core action, or changes any missing step; the insert closes nothing in Problem G.

## Research and reasoning
1. Convention. Chamber C = {g_1>...>g_D>0, sum g_i = W} with g_D eliminated; rho_p = p! h_p(g)/W^p, h_p the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial, matching the appH identity rho_p = 2^{p-1} m_{2p}/m_2^p; the Newton recursion between h and the power sums is used and checked symbolically (s1 Part A).
2. Jacobian factorization. det d(s_2,...,s_D)/d(g_1,...,g_{D-1}) and the h-version are alternating polynomials of degree binom(D,2) in g, hence V(g) times a symmetric factor; W-homogeneity forces the factor to be a constant times a power of W, and the triangular reduction s_k -> (k-1)! g^{k-1} in the one-row direction fixes the constant to prod_{k=1}^D k! after the p!/W^p normalization (W-exponents D(D+1)/2-1 = 2,5,9 at D=2,3,4; constants 2, 12, 288). The naive guess D! V is wrong at D>=3 (s1 exposed it: ratio -2 at D=3); the correct constant is prod_{k=1}^D k!. The sign is orientation, fixed by the ordering convention (+1 at D=2, -1 at D=3).
3. D=2 fold. From g_1+g_2 = W, s_2 = (W^2+Delta^2)/2 with Delta = g_1-g_2, so rho_2 = 1 + s_2/W^2 = (3W^2+Delta^2)/(2W^2). Inversion is a square root, |Delta| = sqrt(2) W sqrt(rho_2-3/2), giving the sharp 1/2 exponent globally; d(rho_2)/dDelta = Delta/W^2, so dDelta/drho_2 = W^2/Delta and the regularized inverse is Lipschitz with constant W^2/delta (an earlier hand guess 2W^2/delta was wrong and is not used).
4. Transverse normal form. Binomial pairing of the (c+u, c-u) pair kills all odd powers exactly, so the wall-cross data are fixed and the leading transverse deviation of every symmetric coordinate is quadratic; the rho_2 coefficient 2/W^2 is universal. This is the normal-form statement behind the Jacobian zero: the map folds quadratically across each wall, and the two chamber points over a wall are the square-root pair.
5. Rates (D=3, numerical). Single-wall blow-up of sigma_min is linear in the gap; total-coincidence blow-up is quadratic (delta^{D-1}); the inverse modulus of the full coordinate map is 1/2-Holder at every stratum checked, including the triple point along the symmetric path (profile y = (1,0,-1)/sqrt(2), gaps t = 2^{-e}, e = 3..9). Root-conditioning exponents 1/m that appear for truncated moment sets do not apply to the full coordinate map and are not claimed.
6. Beta-family integrals. For density proportional to u^beta (1-u^2)^{a-1} on (0,1), E[u^q] = (1/2) B((q+beta+1)/2, a) / B((beta+1)/2, a). At beta=2, q=-1: B(1,a)/B(3/2,a) = Gamma(a+3/2)/(a Gamma(3/2) Gamma(a)), and the elementary recursion Gamma(a+3/2) = (a+1/2) Gamma(a+1/2) turns this into the stated closed form (s2 Part E: chain and recursion checks plus integer sweep). At beta=0, q=-1 the integral diverges at u=0; the truncated integral is log(1/eps)/Z(a,0) + O(1) with Z(a,0) = sqrt(pi) Gamma(a)/(2 Gamma(a+1/2)) (s2 Part E2 at a=1..4). Wall and m-fold dichotomies are exponent counts (s2 Part F): the family weight contributes a factor abs(g_i-g_j)^beta per pair (checked at D=2 and at the D=3 wall, where weight/u^beta -> (1/8)^{2beta} neq 0 at v = 3/4; wall integral 4^{-beta}/beta), |V|^{-1} contributes -binom(m,2), and the (m-1)-dimensional cluster relative space at fixed trace contributes the area element t^{m-2} dt; convergence e > -1 is exactly beta > 1-2/m.
7. Relation to bounded no-gos (none re-attempted). 090 (S_D-invariant weights select orbits only): respected - these are orbit-local geometry statements and select no chamber point. 091 (rank-one uniform subclass flat): not contradicted - the wall value 3/2 is exactly the uniform profile value, and the fold statements concern the chamber map, not the flat subclass. 089 (fibre-integration negativity): untouched. 092 (no in-class transport or coordinate re-attempts): only normal-form and integrability statements are added.
