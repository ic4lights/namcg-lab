\paragraph{Bounded obstruction: analytic source dependence reproduces the exponential-linear class.}
Suppose the regulated source-dependent ladder of the conditional lemma is obtained
by first-order spectral perturbation about the source-free diamond: the source of
acceleration \(g_{\mathrm N}\) enters the diamond state of Section~\ref{sec:ch6-modular}
through a coupling analytic in the source strength (for a classical source linearly
coupled to a free or linearized field the state is a Weyl displacement, i.e.\ a
coherent state, whose modular structure is exactly controlled \cite{Longo2022Weyl}),
and the ladder Hamiltonian obeys
\(H_{\mathrm{lad}}(\lambda)=H_{\mathrm{lad}}(0)+\lambda V+O(\lambda^{2})\)
with \(V\) relatively bounded with relative bound below one, \(\lambda\propto g_{\mathrm N}\),
and an isolated simple ladder gap. Analytic perturbation theory then gives an affine
gap at first order \cite{Kato1966},
\[
\Delta k(\lambda)=\Delta k(0)+\lambda\,c_{1}+O(\lambda^{2}),
\]
so the single-ladder response of the conditional lemma takes the form
\(1/(1-e^{-b-c\,g_{\mathrm N}})\), the retired exponential-linear family
\eqref{eq:appE-exp} at \(\alpha=e^{-b}\) and scale \(1/c\): a linear gap feeds a
linear argument into the exponential, as anticipated in Chapter~\ref{ch:agenda}.
The target \eqref{eq:nu-target} lies outside that family. Setting
\(x=g_{\mathrm N}/g_s\), the target obeys
\(1/(1-e^{-\sqrt{x}})=x^{-1/2}+\tfrac12+\tfrac{1}{12}\sqrt{x}+O(x)\) as \(x\to0\),
with half-integer powers and a divergent leading term, and its deficit from \(1\)
decays as \(e^{-\sqrt{x}}\) as \(x\to\infty\), whereas \(1/(1-\alpha e^{-c x})\) is
bounded at \(x=0\) for \(\alpha<1\), diverges as \((c x)^{-1}\) for \(\alpha=1\), and
has deficit \(\alpha e^{-c x}\); equality for all \(x>0\) would force
\(\sqrt{x}=b'+c x\), which fails. An exactly displaced state moves no spectral gap at
all, and the published diamond first law gives the Einstein response at first order in
the metric perturbation \cite{Jacobson2016,JacobsonVisser2019}, so the extra-stress
enhancement does not appear at that order either. These statements hold for the stated
class only: source dependence analytic in the source strength at fixed infrared
boundary data, a relatively bounded ladder perturbation, and the single-ladder
response of the conditional lemma. They do not cover a source dependence that is
non-analytic at zero source strength -- a ladder resummation, a spectral-measure
effect, or a source dependence carried by the infrared boundary condition defining
the regulated modular operator -- and the square-root gap, if it exists, can only
live in that non-analytic regime. This is a bounded obstruction within the stated
class, not a derivation and not a closure; Problem~H remains open.
